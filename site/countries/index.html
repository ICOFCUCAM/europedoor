03The country as the door

Every country is a door.

France drawn on its own frontier with a photograph of it inside the outline. Not a picture beside a map: the country is the aperture, which is the one thing this atlas can draw that a list of names cannot.

France

The most visited country on earth, and still mostly empty fifty kilometres from anywhere famous.

France is the country this atlas can show best: the register holds 37 photographs of it, its regions, its cities and its places, against 18 for the next. That is a count rather than a preference, and it moves as the library fills. 41 countries can be drawn this way today; nine cannot, and the reason is geometry rather than choice — six of them have no outline in this dataset at all and are drawn as a ringed point, and three carry a travel advisory and are never lit.
