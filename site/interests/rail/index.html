Germany · The Rhine & the West

Bacharach & the Rhine Gorge

Half-timbered wine village under a ruined chapel, in the 65-kilometre UNESCO stretch of castles.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Bergen

A hanseatic trading port wedged between seven mountains, wet 200 days a year and entirely unbothered about it.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Flåm

A hamlet at the end of a fjord arm, famous for a railway that drops 865 metres in twenty kilometres.

Norway · Northern Norway

Narvik

An ice-free ore port at the end of a Swedish railway, ringed by mountains that drop into the fjord.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Voss

A lake town between two fjords that decided to become Norway's outdoor sports capital and succeeded.

Portugal · Porto & the Douro

Pinhão & the Douro valley

Terraced schist vineyards falling into a dammed river, worked by hand because machines cannot stand up on them.

Serbia · Western & Southern Serbia

Mokra Gora

A mountain village on the Šargan Eight, a narrow-gauge line that climbs a figure of eight to gain height.

Switzerland · The Bernese Oberland

Lauterbrunnen

A valley with vertical walls and seventy-two waterfalls, and car-free villages on the shelves above it.

Switzerland · Graubünden & the East

St. Moritz & the Engadin

A high valley of lakes at 1,800 metres, with sgraffito villages and 322 days of sun a year.

Switzerland · Valais, Ticino & the South

Zermatt

A car-free village under the most photographed mountain on earth, with skiing possible in July.

United Kingdom · Wales

Eryri / Snowdonia (Betws-y-Coed)

Slate, lakes and the highest mountains in England and Wales, in a national park with a Welsh-speaking heart.
