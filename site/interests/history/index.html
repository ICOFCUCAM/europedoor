Travelling for

History & ruins

200 cities across 47 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Gjirokastër

A UNESCO-listed Ottoman town of stone-roofed tower houses on a steep hillside under a castle.

Albania · The Albanian Alps

Theth

A valley village of stone houses with no road in until recently, a lock-in tower, and a waterfall above it.

Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Tirana

A capital whose mayor painted the grey communist blocks in primary colours, and where a nuclear bunker is now a museum.

Armenia · Yerevan & the Ararat Plain

Garni & Geghard

A Hellenistic temple on a gorge, and a monastery partly carved out of the rock face next door.

Armenia · The North & South

Tatev

A 9th-century monastery on a cliff in the far south, reached by the world's longest reversible cable car.

Armenia · Yerevan & the Ararat Plain

Yerevan

A capital rebuilt in pink volcanic stone to a 1924 plan, with Ararat filling the southern horizon.

Austria · Vienna & the East

Dürnstein & the Wachau

A blue baroque tower over the Danube, terraced vineyards behind, and the castle where Richard the Lionheart was held.

Austria · Salzburg & the Salzkammergut

Hallstatt

A lakeside village under a salt mountain, with 7,000 years of continuous mining and more visitors than it can hold.

Austria · Salzburg & the Salzkammergut

Salzburg

Baroque built on salt money, under a fortress, with Mozart born in the middle of it.

Azerbaijan · Baku & the Caspian

Baku

A UNESCO-listed walled city with a mysterious stone tower, ringed by oil-boom mansions and three flame-shaped skyscrapers.

Azerbaijan · The Northwest & the Mountains

Sheki

A silk road town under wooded mountains, with a khan's summer palace whose windows are built without glue or nails.

Azerbaijan · The Northwest & the Mountains

Xınalıq

A village at 2,300 metres whose people speak a language related to nothing else, reached by a single mountain road.

Belarus · Minsk & the Centre

Minsk

A city rebuilt from almost nothing after 1944 along one monumental avenue, and unusually complete as a piece of urban design.

Belgium · Flanders

Bruges

A trading city frozen when its river silted up in the 1500s, which is why it looks like this.

Belgium · Brussels & Wallonia

Dinant

A citadel on a cliff over the Meuse, a bulbous cathedral below, and the birthplace of the saxophone.

Belgium · Flanders

Ghent

Bruges's rival, larger, younger and still a working student city rather than a museum of itself.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Herzegovina

Mostar

A 16th-century Ottoman bridge, destroyed in 1993 and rebuilt from the river stones, over a green-blue river.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Sarajevo & Central Bosnia

Sarajevo

An Ottoman bazaar at one end of a street and Habsburg apartment blocks at the other, in a valley ringed by mountains.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Sarajevo & Central Bosnia

Travnik

The Ottoman viziers' capital for 150 years, under a fortress, with a coloured mosque and a spring beneath it.

Bulgaria · Thrace & the Black Sea

Nessebar

A rocky peninsula joined by a causeway, with more medieval churches per square metre than anywhere on the Black Sea.

Bulgaria · Thrace & the Black Sea

Plovdiv

One of Europe's oldest continuously inhabited cities, on six hills, with a Roman theatre still in use.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Rila Monastery

A fortified monastery in a forested valley, founded in the 10th century, with striped arcades and frescoes over every surface.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Sofia

A capital with Roman ruins under the metro stations and a mountain at the end of the tram line.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Dubrovnik

A maritime republic in near-perfect limestone, walled, car-free, and under real pressure from its own popularity.

Croatia · Istria & Kvarner

Motovun

A walled hill town over a truffle forest, with a walkable rampart and a view of the whole Mirna valley.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Split

A Roman emperor's retirement palace that became a town — people live inside the walls and always have.

Cyprus · Nicosia & the South Coast

Nicosia

A Venetian star-shaped fortress city with a UN buffer zone through the middle and a crossing on the main shopping street.

Cyprus · Nicosia & the South Coast

Paphos

A harbour town with Roman villa floors that are among the finest mosaics anywhere, and royal tombs cut into the rock.

Czechia · Moravia

Brno

Czechia's second city: student, modernist and self-deprecating, with an ossuary of 50,000 under the square.

Czechia · Moravia

Mikulov

A chateau town on the Austrian border, surrounded by the country's best vineyards and a limestone ridge.

Czechia · Prague & Bohemia

Prague

Romanesque, gothic, baroque and cubist stacked on top of each other, with a castle above and a bridge in between.

Czechia · Prague & Bohemia

Český Krumlov

A castle town in a tight loop of the Vltava, essentially unchanged since the 18th century.

Czechia · Prague & Bohemia

Český ráj (Turnov)

'Bohemian Paradise': sandstone towers, rock cities and castles on pinnacles, ninety minutes from Prague.

Denmark · Zealand & Copenhagen

Roskilde

Viking ships raised from the fjord, thirty-nine Danish monarchs in one cathedral, and a festival in a field.

Estonia · The Islands & the South

Kuressaare, Saaremaa

An island town around a moated bishop's castle, on Estonia's largest and slowest island.

Estonia · Tallinn & the North Coast

Lahemaa

A national park of bog, forest, manor houses and abandoned Soviet submarine bases, an hour east of the capital.

Estonia · Tallinn & the North Coast

Tallinn

A walled hanseatic town where the merchants' houses, the guild halls and the city wall all survived, and a startup district grew up beside them.

Estonia · The Islands & the South

Tartu

Estonia's intellectual capital since 1632, small, brick and argumentative.

Finland · Finnish Lapland

Inari

The centre of Sámi Finland, on a lake with three thousand islands.

Finland · Helsinki & the Southern Coast

Porvoo

Red ochre warehouses on a river, and Finland's second-oldest town an hour from the capital.

Finland · The Lakeland

Savonlinna

A 15th-century castle on an island in the middle of Saimaa, which hosts opera in its courtyard each July.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Amboise & the Loire châteaux

A royal town on the Loire, with Leonardo's last house at the top of it and Chenonceau downstream.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Arles

A Roman arena still in use, the Rhône delta at the door, and the year Van Gogh painted two hundred canvases here.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Avignon

Seven popes ruled from here in the 14th century and left the largest gothic palace in the world behind them.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Bayeux & the Landing Beaches

A town that kept its cathedral, its 70-metre embroidery of 1066, and the beaches of 1944 twenty minutes away.

France · The Alps & the East

Beaune

The wine capital of Burgundy, inside its own walls, on the most valuable agricultural land on earth.

France · Corsica

Bonifacio

A citadel on white limestone cliffs undercut by the sea, with Sardinia visible across twelve kilometres of strait.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Carcassonne

A double-walled citadel of 52 towers, heavily restored in the 1850s and argued about ever since.

France · Paris & Île-de-France

Chartres

The most complete Gothic cathedral in France, with 172 windows of 12th-century glass that survived two wars in boxes.

France · Corsica

Corte

The old Corsican capital in the mountainous centre, with the university that keeps the language alive and the GR20 above it.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

La Rochelle

A Protestant merchant port behind three medieval towers, with an island of white houses across a bridge.

France · The Auvergne & the Massif Central

Le Puy-en-Velay

Chapels on top of volcanic plugs, green lentils with their own appellation, and the start of the oldest road to Santiago.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Mont-Saint-Michel

An abbey on a rock in a bay with the fastest tides in Europe, and a village winding up to it.

France · Champagne & the North

Reims

Where thirty-three French kings were crowned, above twenty kilometres of Roman chalk cellars full of champagne.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Saint-Malo

A corsair city inside granite ramparts, rebuilt stone by stone after 1944, with a tidal range of twelve metres.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Sarlat & the Dordogne

A honey-coloured medieval town in a valley of castles, walnut groves and the oldest painted caves in Europe.

Georgia · The Greater Caucasus

Mestia, Svaneti

A mountain region of defensive stone towers built by families against each other, under 5,000-metre peaks.

Georgia · Tbilisi & Kakheti

Sighnaghi & Kakheti

A walled hill town over the Alazani valley, with the Caucasus on the horizon and vineyards to the foot of them.

Georgia · Tbilisi & Kakheti

Tbilisi

Wooden balconies, a fortress above, sulphur baths below, and Persian, Russian and Soviet layers in the same street.

Germany · The Rhine & the West

Bacharach & the Rhine Gorge

Half-timbered wine village under a ruined chapel, in the 65-kilometre UNESCO stretch of castles.
