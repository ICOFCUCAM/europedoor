Europe/Experiences/Snow & winter

Travelling for

Snow & winter

25 cities across 14 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Andorra · The Valleys

Andorra la Vella

Europe's highest capital at 1,023 metres, in a narrow valley, mostly shops with mountains directly behind them.

Austria · Tyrol & the West

Innsbruck

A university city at 574 metres with a 2,300-metre mountain reachable from the centre in twenty minutes.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Bansko

A stone-built mountain town under Pirin, with cheap skiing in winter and hut-to-hut walking in summer.

Finland · Finnish Lapland

Inari

The centre of Sámi Finland, on a lake with three thousand islands.

Finland · Finnish Lapland

Rovaniemi

The gateway to Lapland, rebuilt after 1944 to an Alvar Aalto plan shaped like reindeer antlers.

France · The Alps & the East

Chamonix

A valley town under the highest mountain in western Europe, where alpinism was invented.

Germany · Bavaria & the Alps

Berchtesgaden

An alpine corner surrounded on three sides by Austria, with a fjord-green lake below the Watzmann.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Ortisei & the Dolomites

Pale limestone towers over alpine meadows, in a valley where Ladin is still the first language.

Liechtenstein · The Rhine Valley & the Mountains

Malbun

The country's only ski area, in a high bowl at 1,600 metres, with marmots and a chairlift.

Montenegro · Durmitor & the North

Žabljak

The highest town in the Balkans at 1,450 metres, at the edge of Durmitor national park.

Norway · Northern Norway

Alta

Finnmark's largest town, with 6,000-year-old rock carvings and a cathedral shaped like the aurora.

Norway · Østlandet & the East

Lillehammer

Olympic town on a long lake, with an open-air museum that is really a village of 200 relocated buildings.

Norway · Svalbard

Longyearbyen

The world's northernmost town of any size: a coal settlement turned research station, where leaving the settlement requires a rifle.

Norway · Northern Norway

Narvik

An ice-free ore port at the end of a Swedish railway, ringed by mountains that drop into the fjord.

Norway · Northern Norway

Nordkapp (Honningsvåg)

A cliff at 71°N sold as the top of Europe, on an island reached by tunnel, with a fishing town below it.

Norway · Østlandet & the East

Rjukan

A company town in a gorge so deep the sun does not reach it for six months, so the town installed mirrors on the mountain.

Norway · Trøndelag

Røros

A 17th-century copper-mining town of black timber houses, at -30°C for weeks at a stretch and listed by UNESCO for surviving intact.

Norway · Northern Norway

Tromsø

A university town at 69°N with more bars per head than anywhere else in Norway, and the aurora directly overhead.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Voss

A lake town between two fjords that decided to become Norway's outdoor sports capital and succeeded.

Poland · Lesser Poland & the South

Zakopane & the Tatras

A wooden mountain town under the only alpine range in Poland, with its own architectural style.

Slovakia · The Tatras & the North

Poprad & the High Tatras

The gateway town to a 26-kilometre range that reaches 2,655 metres and behaves like the Alps.

Sweden · Swedish Lapland

Abisko

A rain-shadowed valley with Sweden's clearest winter skies and the northern end of its greatest trail.

Sweden · Swedish Lapland

Kiruna

An iron-ore town so successful at mining that it is being physically moved, building by building, off the hole it dug.

Switzerland · Graubünden & the East

St. Moritz & the Engadin

A high valley of lakes at 1,800 metres, with sgraffito villages and 322 days of sun a year.

Switzerland · Valais, Ticino & the South

Zermatt

A car-free village under the most photographed mountain on earth, with skiing possible in July.
