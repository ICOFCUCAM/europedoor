Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Gjirokastër

A UNESCO-listed Ottoman town of stone-roofed tower houses on a steep hillside under a castle.

Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Himarë & the Riviera

Ionian villages between mountain and sea, with beaches that were empty ten years ago and are filling fast.

Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Tirana

A capital whose mayor painted the grey communist blocks in primary colours, and where a nuclear bunker is now a museum.

Armenia · Yerevan & the Ararat Plain

Yerevan

A capital rebuilt in pink volcanic stone to a 1924 plan, with Ararat filling the southern horizon.

Austria · Vienna & the East

Vienna

Habsburg on the outside, Freud and Klimt and social housing on the inside, with the world's best-funded music scene.

Azerbaijan · Baku & the Caspian

Baku

A UNESCO-listed walled city with a mysterious stone tower, ringed by oil-boom mansions and three flame-shaped skyscrapers.

Azerbaijan · The Northwest & the Mountains

Sheki

A silk road town under wooded mountains, with a khan's summer palace whose windows are built without glue or nails.

Belgium · Flanders

Antwerp

Rubens's city and the world's diamond exchange, with a fashion school that reset the industry in the 1980s.

Belgium · Flanders

Bruges

A trading city frozen when its river silted up in the 1500s, which is why it looks like this.

Belgium · Brussels & Wallonia

Brussels

Art Nouveau streets, comic-strip walls, a gilded square, and the machinery of a continent in the eastern quarter.

Belgium · Flanders

Ghent

Bruges's rival, larger, younger and still a working student city rather than a museum of itself.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Sarajevo & Central Bosnia

Sarajevo

An Ottoman bazaar at one end of a street and Habsburg apartment blocks at the other, in a valley ringed by mountains.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Sarajevo & Central Bosnia

Travnik

The Ottoman viziers' capital for 150 years, under a fortress, with a coloured mosque and a spring beneath it.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Bansko

A stone-built mountain town under Pirin, with cheap skiing in winter and hut-to-hut walking in summer.

Bulgaria · Thrace & the Black Sea

Plovdiv

One of Europe's oldest continuously inhabited cities, on six hills, with a Roman theatre still in use.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Sofia

A capital with Roman ruins under the metro stations and a mountain at the end of the tram line.

Croatia · Istria & Kvarner

Motovun

A walled hill town over a truffle forest, with a walkable rampart and a view of the whole Mirna valley.

Croatia · Istria & Kvarner

Rovinj

A Venetian fishing town on a former island, with a campanile copied from San Marco and houses straight into the sea.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Split

A Roman emperor's retirement palace that became a town — people live inside the walls and always have.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Vis

The furthest inhabited Dalmatian island, closed to foreigners as a military base until 1989 and undeveloped because of it.

Croatia · Inland Croatia

Zagreb

A central European capital of coffee terraces and museums, with almost nothing Mediterranean about it.

Cyprus · Nicosia & the South Coast

Nicosia

A Venetian star-shaped fortress city with a UN buffer zone through the middle and a crossing on the main shopping street.

Cyprus · The Troodos Mountains

Omodos & the wine villages

Cobbled mountain villages growing indigenous grapes nobody else has, around a 12th-century monastery.

Czechia · Moravia

Brno

Czechia's second city: student, modernist and self-deprecating, with an ossuary of 50,000 under the square.

Czechia · Prague & Bohemia

Prague

Romanesque, gothic, baroque and cubist stacked on top of each other, with a castle above and a bridge in between.

Denmark · Jutland

Aarhus

A student city with a rainbow walkway on its art museum and a reconstructed old town beneath it.

Denmark · Zealand & Copenhagen

Copenhagen

A harbour city clean enough to swim in the middle of, with the densest good eating in the Nordics.

Estonia · Tallinn & the North Coast

Tallinn

A walled hanseatic town where the merchants' houses, the guild halls and the city wall all survived, and a startup district grew up beside them.

Finland · Helsinki & the Southern Coast

Helsinki

Neoclassical centre, brutal-modern edges, and a design district you can walk in an afternoon.

Finland · Helsinki & the Southern Coast

Porvoo

Red ochre warehouses on a river, and Finland's second-oldest town an hour from the capital.

France · Champagne & the North

Amiens

The largest gothic cathedral in France by volume, and market gardens on canals behind it.

France · The Alps & the East

Annecy

A canal town on the cleanest large lake in Europe, with the Alps standing straight up behind it.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Arles

A Roman arena still in use, the Rhône delta at the door, and the year Van Gogh painted two hundred canvases here.

France · The Alps & the East

Beaune

The wine capital of Burgundy, inside its own walls, on the most valuable agricultural land on earth.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Biarritz & the Basque Coast

Atlantic surf, a Napoleonic resort, and a food culture that belongs to a nation without a state.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Bordeaux

An 18th-century stone city on a river bend, cleaned in the 2000s, with the world's most famous vineyards at the gates.

France · The Alps & the East

Colmar & the Alsace wine road

Half-timbered, geranium-hung, and German four times in a century — the food and the wine still say so.

France · Corsica

Corte

The old Corsican capital in the mountainous centre, with the university that keeps the language alive and the GR20 above it.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

La Rochelle

A Protestant merchant port behind three medieval towers, with an island of white houses across a bridge.

France · The Auvergne & the Massif Central

Le Puy-en-Velay

Chapels on top of volcanic plugs, green lentils with their own appellation, and the start of the oldest road to Santiago.

France · Champagne & the North

Lille

Flemish in its architecture and French in its administration, with the best food market north of Lyon.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Marseille

France's oldest city, founded by Greeks in 600 BC, Mediterranean rather than French in almost every respect.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Nice

An Italian city until 1860, with an old town that still cooks like one and a pebble bay that never closes.

France · Paris & Île-de-France

Paris

Nineteenth-century town planning at monumental scale, with the world's densest concentration of art inside it.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Saint-Malo

A corsair city inside granite ramparts, rebuilt stone by stone after 1944, with a tidal range of twelve metres.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Sarlat & the Dordogne

A honey-coloured medieval town in a valley of castles, walnut groves and the oldest painted caves in Europe.

France · The Alps & the East

Strasbourg

A cathedral of pink sandstone over a half-timbered island, and the European Parliament twenty minutes away.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Toulouse

The pink city: brick everything, a Romanesque basilica, aerospace money and a canal to the Mediterranean.

Georgia · Tbilisi & Kakheti

Sighnaghi & Kakheti

A walled hill town over the Alazani valley, with the Caucasus on the horizon and vineyards to the foot of them.

Georgia · Tbilisi & Kakheti

Tbilisi

Wooden balconies, a fortress above, sulphur baths below, and Persian, Russian and Soviet layers in the same street.

Germany · The Rhine & the West

Cologne

A Roman city with a Gothic cathedral that took 632 years and dominates everything.

Germany · The North

Hamburg

A merchant republic and Europe's third port, with more bridges than Venice and Amsterdam together.

Germany · The North

Lübeck

The queen of the Hanseatic League, on an island, in brick gothic, and the home of marzipan.

Germany · Bavaria & the Alps

Munich

A wealthy southern capital with the Alps an hour away and a river you can surf in the middle of a park.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Athens

A sprawling modern capital with a 2,500-year-old temple on a rock in the middle and a food scene that got very good, quietly.

Greece · The Islands

Chania, Crete

A Venetian harbour with an Ottoman quarter behind it, on an island large enough to be its own country.

Greece · The Islands

Corfu

Venetian for four centuries and British for fifty, green all year, with an old town that looks nothing like the Aegean.

Greece · The Islands

Heraklion & Knossos

Crete's capital and the Minoan palace beside it — Europe's oldest city, and a reconstruction people still argue about.

Greece · The Islands

Ikaria

A steep island where people routinely live past ninety, festivals start at midnight and shops open when they open.

Greece · Northern Greece

Ioannina

An Ottoman lake city with an island in it, a silversmithing tradition and the gateway to Zagori.
