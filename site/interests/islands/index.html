Travelling for

Islands

12 cities across 11 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Vis

The furthest inhabited Dalmatian island, closed to foreigners as a military base until 1989 and undeveloped because of it.

Estonia · The Islands & the South

Kuressaare, Saaremaa

An island town around a moated bishop's castle, on Estonia's largest and slowest island.

Finland · Helsinki & the Southern Coast

Helsinki

Neoclassical centre, brutal-modern edges, and a design district you can walk in an afternoon.

Greece · The Islands

Naxos

The largest Cycladic island: mountain villages, marble quarries, a working agricultural interior and long sandy beaches.

Greece · The Islands

Santorini

The rim of a drowned volcano, with villages on the edge of a 300-metre cliff and vines grown in baskets on the ground.

Ireland · The West & the Atlantic

The Aran Islands (Inis Mór)

Three limestone islands where Irish is the daily language and stone walls divide fields the size of rooms.

Lithuania · The Coast & Centre

Nida, Curonian Spit

Wooden fishermen's houses under a moving dune, on a sand spit between lagoon and sea.

Malta · Gozo & Comino

Victoria, Gozo

A citadel town in the middle of an island where the pace drops noticeably the moment the ferry docks.

Netherlands · The North & the Wadden Islands

Texel

The largest Wadden island: dunes, sheep, seals and thirty kilometres of empty North Sea beach.

Norway · Northern Norway

Lofoten (Svolvær)

Granite peaks that rise straight from the sea, red fishermen's cabins below them, and cod drying on racks every spring.

Sweden · Stockholm & the Archipelago

Stockholm

Water on every side, a preserved medieval core, and the best-funded museums in the Nordics.

United Kingdom · Scotland

Isle of Skye (Portree)

Black basalt ridges, sea cliffs and more visitors than the single-track roads were built for.
