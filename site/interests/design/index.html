Travelling for

Design & making

20 cities across 12 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Belgium · Flanders

Antwerp

Rubens's city and the world's diamond exchange, with a fashion school that reset the industry in the 1980s.

Denmark · Jutland

Aarhus

A student city with a rainbow walkway on its art museum and a reconstructed old town beneath it.

Denmark · Zealand & Copenhagen

Copenhagen

A harbour city clean enough to swim in the middle of, with the densest good eating in the Nordics.

Estonia · Tallinn & the North Coast

Tallinn

A walled hanseatic town where the merchants' houses, the guild halls and the city wall all survived, and a startup district grew up beside them.

Finland · Helsinki & the Southern Coast

Helsinki

Neoclassical centre, brutal-modern edges, and a design district you can walk in an afternoon.

Finland · Finnish Lapland

Rovaniemi

The gateway to Lapland, rebuilt after 1944 to an Alvar Aalto plan shaped like reindeer antlers.

Iceland · Reykjavík & the Southwest

Reykjavík

The northernmost capital of a sovereign state, small enough to cross on foot and loud on Friday nights.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Milan

Italy's working city: fashion, design, finance, and a Last Supper that has to be booked months ahead.

Netherlands · The Randstad

Rotterdam

Flattened in 1940 and rebuilt as an argument for modern architecture, on Europe's largest port.

Norway · Northern Norway

Alta

Finnmark's largest town, with 6,000-year-old rock carvings and a cathedral shaped like the aurora.

Norway · Northern Norway

Bodø

A rebuilt northern port with the strongest tidal current in the world at its door and Lofoten across the water.

Norway · Østlandet & the East

Oslo

A capital that spent its oil money on architecture and then put a forest and a fjord within a tram ride of the result.

Norway · Sørlandet & the South Coast

Risør

'The white town by the Skagerrak' — 300 wooden houses round a harbour, and a wooden boat festival each August.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Ålesund

Burned to the ground in 1904 and rebuilt, all at once, in Art Nouveau — so the whole town matches.

Slovenia · Ljubljana, the Karst & the Coast

Ljubljana

A capital of 300,000 with a car-free centre, a castle on the hill and Plečnik's architecture holding it together.

Spain · Catalonia & the East

Valencia

A river turned into a nine-kilometre park, a gothic silk exchange, and the actual home of paella.

Sweden · Swedish Lapland

Kiruna

An iron-ore town so successful at mining that it is being physically moved, building by building, off the hole it dug.

Sweden · Skåne & the South

Malmö

Post-industrial, young, and twenty minutes by train from Copenhagen across the bridge.

Sweden · Stockholm & the Archipelago

Stockholm

Water on every side, a preserved medieval core, and the best-funded museums in the Nordics.

Switzerland · Graubünden & the East

St. Moritz & the Engadin

A high valley of lakes at 1,800 metres, with sgraffito villages and 322 days of sun a year.
