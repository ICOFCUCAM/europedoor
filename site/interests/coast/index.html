Coast & beaches

92 cities across 29 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Himarë & the Riviera

Ionian villages between mountain and sea, with beaches that were empty ten years ago and are filling fast.

Bulgaria · Thrace & the Black Sea

Nessebar

A rocky peninsula joined by a causeway, with more medieval churches per square metre than anywhere on the Black Sea.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Dubrovnik

A maritime republic in near-perfect limestone, walled, car-free, and under real pressure from its own popularity.

Croatia · Istria & Kvarner

Rovinj

A Venetian fishing town on a former island, with a campanile copied from San Marco and houses straight into the sea.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Split

A Roman emperor's retirement palace that became a town — people live inside the walls and always have.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Vis

The furthest inhabited Dalmatian island, closed to foreigners as a military base until 1989 and undeveloped because of it.

Cyprus · Nicosia & the South Coast

Paphos

A harbour town with Roman villa floors that are among the finest mosaics anywhere, and royal tombs cut into the rock.

Denmark · Jutland

Skagen

The country's northern tip, where two seas meet visibly and a school of painters came for the light.

Estonia · Tallinn & the North Coast

Lahemaa

A national park of bog, forest, manor houses and abandoned Soviet submarine bases, an hour east of the capital.

Finland · Helsinki & the Southern Coast

Porvoo

Red ochre warehouses on a river, and Finland's second-oldest town an hour from the capital.

France · The Alps & the East

Annecy

A canal town on the cleanest large lake in Europe, with the Alps standing straight up behind it.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Bayeux & the Landing Beaches

A town that kept its cathedral, its 70-metre embroidery of 1066, and the beaches of 1944 twenty minutes away.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Biarritz & the Basque Coast

Atlantic surf, a Napoleonic resort, and a food culture that belongs to a nation without a state.

France · Corsica

Bonifacio

A citadel on white limestone cliffs undercut by the sea, with Sardinia visible across twelve kilometres of strait.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

La Rochelle

A Protestant merchant port behind three medieval towers, with an island of white houses across a bridge.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Marseille

France's oldest city, founded by Greeks in 600 BC, Mediterranean rather than French in almost every respect.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Mont-Saint-Michel

An abbey on a rock in a bay with the fastest tides in Europe, and a village winding up to it.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Nice

An Italian city until 1860, with an old town that still cooks like one and a pebble bay that never closes.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Saint-Malo

A corsair city inside granite ramparts, rebuilt stone by stone after 1944, with a tidal range of twelve metres.

Greece · The Islands

Chania, Crete

A Venetian harbour with an Ottoman quarter behind it, on an island large enough to be its own country.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Hydra

An island two hours from Athens with no cars and no bicycles at all — mules, boats and feet.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Kardamyli & the Mani

Stone tower houses on a wild peninsula where blood feuds ran into the 20th century, above a clear sea.

Greece · Northern Greece

Kavala

An Ottoman aqueduct over a harbour town, with Philippi's ruins inland and Thasos across the water.

Greece · The Islands

Kefalonia

The largest Ionian island: an underground lake, a mountain of black fir, and the loggerhead turtles offshore.

Greece · The Islands

Milos

A volcanic island of white rock formations, coloured mining beaches and boat-only coves.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Monemvasia

A Byzantine town hidden on the seaward side of a rock island, invisible from the mainland.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Nafplio

Greece's first capital: Venetian, neoclassical, under two fortresses, on a gulf in the Peloponnese.

Greece · The Islands

Naxos

The largest Cycladic island: mountain villages, marble quarries, a working agricultural interior and long sandy beaches.

Greece · The Islands

Paros

Cycladic in the way people imagine the Cyclades, with marble quarries that supplied the ancient world and a working town behind the beaches.

Greece · Northern Greece

Pelion (Makrinitsa)

Stone villages with slate roofs on a wooded mountain that drops to beaches on both sides.

Greece · The Islands

Rhodes

A walled medieval city built by crusader knights, on an island of Ottoman villages and butterfly valleys.

Greece · The Islands

Santorini

The rim of a drowned volcano, with villages on the edge of a 300-metre cliff and vines grown in baskets on the ground.

Greece · The Islands

Symi

Neoclassical mansions in ochre and blue stacked round a horseshoe harbour, an hour from Rhodes.

Hungary · Balaton & the Wine Country

Tihany, Lake Balaton

A lavender peninsula with an abbey on a cliff, sticking into a shallow warm lake.

Iceland · The Westfjords & North

Húsavík

A wooden church, a whale museum, and the most reliable whale watching in Europe.

Iceland · The South Coast & East

Vík í Mýrdal

A village of three hundred under a glacier, with black sand and dangerous surf.

Iceland · The Westfjords & North

Ísafjörður

The Westfjords' capital: 2,700 people at the end of a fjord under a flat-topped mountain.

Ireland · The West & the Atlantic

Dingle

A fishing town on a peninsula of beehive huts, empty beaches and the westernmost point of Europe's mainland fringe.

Ireland · The West & the Atlantic

Galway

A small port city that plays music every night of the week and treats a festival as its natural state.

Ireland · The West & the Atlantic

The Aran Islands (Inis Mór)

Three limestone islands where Irish is the daily language and stone walls divide fields the size of rooms.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Alghero, Sardinia

A Catalan-speaking walled port on Sardinia's northwest coast, with sea caves and Bronze Age towers inland.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Bellagio & Lake Como

A village on the point where the lake divides, with villa gardens on both arms and ferries between them.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Lecce

Baroque carved in soft golden limestone, in the heel of Italy, an hour from two seas.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Lipari & the Aeolians

Seven volcanic islands off Sicily, one of them erupting every twenty minutes, all of them on the UNESCO list.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Naples

Loud, dense, Greek in origin, with the best pizza and the best archaeological museum in the world.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Syracuse & Ortigia

Once the most powerful Greek city in the world, now an island old town with a Doric temple inside the cathedral.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Trieste

Austro-Hungarian, Italian, Slovene and Jewish at once, on a limestone edge above the Adriatic, with the best coffee culture in Italy.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Venice

A city on wooden piles in a lagoon, with no roads, fewer than 50,000 residents left, and no equivalent anywhere.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Vernazza & the Cinque Terre

Five fishing villages on a cliff coast, joined by terraced footpaths and a railway in a tunnel.

Latvia · Kurzeme & the Coast

Liepāja

A wind-blown port with a derelict tsarist naval town at its northern end and a rock music habit.

Lithuania · The Coast & Centre

Nida, Curonian Spit

Wooden fishermen's houses under a moving dune, on a sand spit between lagoon and sea.

Malta · Malta

Valletta

A planned fortress city of 1566, on a peninsula between two harbours, with Caravaggio's largest painting in its cathedral.

Malta · Gozo & Comino

Victoria, Gozo

A citadel town in the middle of an island where the pace drops noticeably the moment the ferry docks.

Malta · Malta

Ħaġar Qim & the South Cliffs

Megalithic temples on a cliff over the sea, built between 3600 and 3200 BC.

Monaco · The Rock & the Harbour

Monaco-Ville

The medieval quarter on the rock: palace, cathedral, and an oceanographic museum built into the cliff face.

Montenegro · The Bay of Kotor & the Coast

Kotor

A walled Venetian town at the head of the bay, with fortifications climbing 260 metres up the cliff behind it.

Montenegro · The Bay of Kotor & the Coast

Perast

One street, sixteen baroque palaces, two islands offshore, and no room for a car.

Netherlands · The North & the Wadden Islands

Texel

The largest Wadden island: dunes, sheep, seals and thirty kilometres of empty North Sea beach.

North Macedonia · Ohrid & the Southwest

Ohrid

A town of Byzantine churches on a cliff over one of the world's oldest lakes, with a Slavic literary tradition that began here.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Balestrand

A Sognefjord village of dragon-style timber villas, where 19th-century painters came and then stayed.
