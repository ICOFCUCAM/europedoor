Europe/Experiences/Wildlife watching

Travelling for

Wildlife watching

19 cities across 16 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Andorra · The Valleys

Madriu-Perafita-Claror

A glacial valley with no road into it, on the UNESCO list for a cultural landscape of summer pasture and stone huts.

Belarus · The Forests

Belovezhskaya Pushcha

The Belarusian half of Europe's last primeval lowland forest, with bison and trees that have never been cut.

Finland · Finnish Lapland

Rovaniemi

The gateway to Lapland, rebuilt after 1944 to an Alvar Aalto plan shaped like reindeer antlers.

Georgia · The Greater Caucasus

Mestia, Svaneti

A mountain region of defensive stone towers built by families against each other, under 5,000-metre peaks.

Greece · The Islands

Kefalonia

The largest Ionian island: an underground lake, a mountain of black fir, and the loggerhead turtles offshore.

Greece · Northern Greece

Zagori (Monodendri)

Forty-six stone villages in the Pindus mountains, linked by packhorse bridges over the deepest gorge in the world by ratio.

Iceland · The Westfjords & North

Húsavík

A wooden church, a whale museum, and the most reliable whale watching in Europe.

Iceland · The Westfjords & North

Ísafjörður

The Westfjords' capital: 2,700 people at the end of a fjord under a flat-topped mountain.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Lipari & the Aeolians

Seven volcanic islands off Sicily, one of them erupting every twenty minutes, all of them on the UNESCO list.

Kosovo · Prizren & the Mountains

Peja & Rugova

A bazaar town at the mouth of a limestone gorge, with a patriarchate monastery and the Accursed Mountains behind.

Montenegro · Durmitor & the North

Žabljak

The highest town in the Balkans at 1,450 metres, at the edge of Durmitor national park.

Netherlands · The North & the Wadden Islands

Texel

The largest Wadden island: dunes, sheep, seals and thirty kilometres of empty North Sea beach.

Norway · Svalbard

Longyearbyen

The world's northernmost town of any size: a coal settlement turned research station, where leaving the settlement requires a rifle.

Poland · Pomerania & the East

Białowieża

The last primeval lowland forest in Europe, with wild bison in it, on the Belarusian border.

Romania · Bucharest & the Danube Delta

Tulcea & the Danube Delta

The gateway to 5,800 square kilometres of reed, channel and lake, with 300 bird species and villages reachable only by boat.

Slovenia · The Julian Alps

Bovec & the Soča

An alpine valley on an unreally turquoise river, and the front line of the Isonzo battles above it.

Spain · Green Spain

Picos de Europa (Cangas de Onís)

Limestone peaks twenty kilometres from the sea, with a cable car, gorges and blue cheese aged in caves.

Spain · Aragón & the Pyrenees

Torla & Ordesa

A stone village at the mouth of a glacial canyon, under the third-highest peak in the Pyrenees.

United Kingdom · Scotland

Islay

A low green island of nine working distilleries, 3,000 people and 50,000 wintering geese.
