Travelling for

Sacred places

59 cities across 32 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Armenia · The North & South

Dilijan

A forested spa town called 'Armenian Switzerland', with two of the country's finest monasteries in the woods nearby.

Armenia · Yerevan & the Ararat Plain

Garni & Geghard

A Hellenistic temple on a gorge, and a monastery partly carved out of the rock face next door.

Armenia · The North & South

Tatev

A 9th-century monastery on a cliff in the far south, reached by the world's longest reversible cable car.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Herzegovina

Blagaj

A dervish monastery built against a cliff at the source of the Buna, where a river comes out of a cave at full size.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Herzegovina

Mostar

A 16th-century Ottoman bridge, destroyed in 1993 and rebuilt from the river stones, over a green-blue river.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Sarajevo & Central Bosnia

Sarajevo

An Ottoman bazaar at one end of a street and Habsburg apartment blocks at the other, in a valley ringed by mountains.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Sarajevo & Central Bosnia

Travnik

The Ottoman viziers' capital for 150 years, under a fortress, with a coloured mosque and a spring beneath it.

Bulgaria · Thrace & the Black Sea

Nessebar

A rocky peninsula joined by a causeway, with more medieval churches per square metre than anywhere on the Black Sea.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Rila Monastery

A fortified monastery in a forested valley, founded in the 10th century, with striped arcades and frescoes over every surface.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Sofia

A capital with Roman ruins under the metro stations and a mountain at the end of the tram line.

Cyprus · The Troodos Mountains

Omodos & the wine villages

Cobbled mountain villages growing indigenous grapes nobody else has, around a 12th-century monastery.

Denmark · Zealand & Copenhagen

Roskilde

Viking ships raised from the fjord, thirty-nine Danish monarchs in one cathedral, and a festival in a field.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Bayeux & the Landing Beaches

A town that kept its cathedral, its 70-metre embroidery of 1066, and the beaches of 1944 twenty minutes away.

France · Paris & Île-de-France

Chartres

The most complete Gothic cathedral in France, with 172 windows of 12th-century glass that survived two wars in boxes.

France · Normandy & Brittany

Mont-Saint-Michel

An abbey on a rock in a bay with the fastest tides in Europe, and a village winding up to it.

Georgia · The Greater Caucasus

Stepantsminda (Kazbegi)

A village under a 5,047-metre volcano, with a 14th-century church on a spur above it.

Germany · The Rhine & the West

Cologne

A Roman city with a Gothic cathedral that took 632 years and dominates everything.

Greece · Northern Greece

Meteora (Kalambaka)

Six working monasteries on top of sandstone pillars, reached for centuries by rope ladder and net.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Monemvasia

A Byzantine town hidden on the seaward side of a rock island, invisible from the mainland.

Greece · Northern Greece

Thessaloniki

Greece's second city: Byzantine, Ottoman and Sephardic layers, and the country's most confident food.

Hungary · Budapest & the Danube Bend

Szentendre

A Serbian Orthodox artists' town on the river, forty minutes from the capital by suburban rail.

Hungary · Balaton & the Wine Country

Tihany, Lake Balaton

A lavender peninsula with an abbey on a cliff, sticking into a shallow warm lake.

Ireland · The West & the Atlantic

Dingle

A fishing town on a peninsula of beehive huts, empty beaches and the westernmost point of Europe's mainland fringe.

Ireland · Dublin & the East

Glendalough

A 6th-century monastic city in a glacial valley with two lakes, an hour from Dublin.

Italy · Rome & Lazio

Orvieto

A town on a plug of volcanic rock with a striped cathedral and an Etruscan city tunnelled underneath it.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Palermo

Norman, Arab and Byzantine in the same building, with the best street food in Italy underneath.

Italy · Rome & Lazio

Rome

A city where an insurance office sits on a temple and nobody comments, with the best street-level food in Italy.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Syracuse & Ortigia

Once the most powerful Greek city in the world, now an island old town with a Doric temple inside the cathedral.

Kosovo · Prizren & the Mountains

Peja & Rugova

A bazaar town at the mouth of a limestone gorge, with a patriarchate monastery and the Accursed Mountains behind.

Kosovo · Prizren & the Mountains

Prizren

A river town of mosques, Orthodox churches and a stone bridge, under a fortress with the whole valley below it.

Lithuania · Vilnius & the East

Vilnius

Forty churches, endless courtyards, and a self-declared republic of artists over the river.

Lithuania · The Coast & Centre

Šiauliai

An industrial town whose reason to visit stands in a field twelve kilometres north.

Luxembourg · Mullerthal & the Ardennes

Echternach

The country's oldest town, with a 7th-century abbey and a hopping procession on the UNESCO list.

Malta · Malta

Mdina & Rabat

The old walled capital on a hill, with about 250 residents and no traffic — called the silent city for a reason.

Malta · Malta

Ħaġar Qim & the South Cliffs

Megalithic temples on a cliff over the sea, built between 3600 and 3200 BC.

Moldova · Orheiul Vechi & the North

Orheiul Vechi

A monastery dug into a limestone cliff over a loop of the Răut, inhabited on and off since the 13th century.

Montenegro · The Bay of Kotor & the Coast

Perast

One street, sixteen baroque palaces, two islands offshore, and no room for a car.

North Macedonia · Ohrid & the Southwest

Ohrid

A town of Byzantine churches on a cliff over one of the world's oldest lakes, with a Slavic literary tradition that began here.

Norway · Trøndelag

Trondheim

A student city built around the northernmost medieval cathedral in the world, and the end of a thousand-year pilgrim path.

Poland · Lesser Poland & the South

Kraków

The royal capital that survived the war, with Europe's largest medieval square and a Jewish quarter that is now a district again.

Portugal · The Alentejo & the South

Évora

A walled town with a Roman temple in the middle, a chapel of bones, and megaliths in the fields around it.

Romania · Bucovina & Maramureș

Gura Humorului & the painted monasteries

The base for a group of 15th- and 16th-century monasteries frescoed on the outside walls and still bright.

Romania · Bucovina & Maramureș

Săpânța & Maramureș

Wooden churches with tall shingled spires, and a cemetery where every headstone is painted, rhymed and often rude.

Romania · Transylvania

Viscri

A single-street Saxon village with a fortified church, horses on the road, and no through traffic.

Russia · Moscow & the Golden Ring

Moscow

A fortress, a cathedral square and a metro system built as underground palaces.

Serbia · Western & Southern Serbia

Studenica

A 12th-century monastery of white marble in a wooded valley, with frescoes from 1209 and monks still living there.

Slovakia · The Tatras & the North

Levoča & the Spiš

A walled medieval town with the tallest wooden altar in the world, under the ruins of Spiš castle.

Spain · Andalusia

Córdoba

The largest city in the world in the 10th century, with a mosque-cathedral of 856 columns at its centre.

Spain · Green Spain

Santiago de Compostela

The end of the Camino: a granite city that rains constantly and fills each afternoon with people who have walked there.

Spain · Andalusia

Seville

Orange trees, the world's largest gothic cathedral, and flamenco that belongs to specific families.

Spain · Madrid & the Centre

Toledo

A city on a granite outcrop in a river loop, where Christian, Jewish and Muslim Spain were one place for centuries.

Sweden · Stockholm & the Archipelago

Uppsala

Sweden's oldest university, its largest cathedral, and the burial mounds of kings who predate both.

Türkiye · Istanbul & Thrace

Edirne

The Ottoman capital before Istanbul, in the European corner, with Sinan's masterpiece mosque in it.

Türkiye · Central Anatolia

Göreme, Cappadocia

Soft volcanic rock eroded into cones and carved out for a thousand years into churches, houses and underground cities.

Türkiye · Istanbul & Thrace

Istanbul

Capital of two empires, on two continents, with a 1,500-year-old church-mosque at the centre of it.

Türkiye · The Aegean & Mediterranean

Selçuk & Ephesus

A market town beside one of the best-preserved classical cities in the Mediterranean.

Ukraine · Kyiv & the Centre

Kyiv

A city founded in the 5th century on hills above a wide river, with a cave monastery from 1051 beneath it.

United Kingdom · London & the South East

Canterbury

The mother church of a global communion, in a walled town on the pilgrims' road from London.

Vatican City · Vatican City

Vatican City

A state the size of a large park, holding Michelangelo's ceiling, his dome and his Pietà.
