Travelling for

Big cities

74 cities across 41 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Tirana

A capital whose mayor painted the grey communist blocks in primary colours, and where a nuclear bunker is now a museum.

Armenia · Yerevan & the Ararat Plain

Yerevan

A capital rebuilt in pink volcanic stone to a 1924 plan, with Ararat filling the southern horizon.

Austria · Tyrol & the West

Innsbruck

A university city at 574 metres with a 2,300-metre mountain reachable from the centre in twenty minutes.

Austria · Vienna & the East

Vienna

Habsburg on the outside, Freud and Klimt and social housing on the inside, with the world's best-funded music scene.

Azerbaijan · Baku & the Caspian

Baku

A UNESCO-listed walled city with a mysterious stone tower, ringed by oil-boom mansions and three flame-shaped skyscrapers.

Belarus · Minsk & the Centre

Minsk

A city rebuilt from almost nothing after 1944 along one monumental avenue, and unusually complete as a piece of urban design.

Belgium · Flanders

Antwerp

Rubens's city and the world's diamond exchange, with a fashion school that reset the industry in the 1980s.

Belgium · Brussels & Wallonia

Brussels

Art Nouveau streets, comic-strip walls, a gilded square, and the machinery of a continent in the eastern quarter.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Sarajevo & Central Bosnia

Sarajevo

An Ottoman bazaar at one end of a street and Habsburg apartment blocks at the other, in a valley ringed by mountains.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Sofia

A capital with Roman ruins under the metro stations and a mountain at the end of the tram line.

Croatia · Dalmatia

Split

A Roman emperor's retirement palace that became a town — people live inside the walls and always have.

Croatia · Inland Croatia

Zagreb

A central European capital of coffee terraces and museums, with almost nothing Mediterranean about it.

Cyprus · Nicosia & the South Coast

Nicosia

A Venetian star-shaped fortress city with a UN buffer zone through the middle and a crossing on the main shopping street.

Czechia · Prague & Bohemia

Prague

Romanesque, gothic, baroque and cubist stacked on top of each other, with a castle above and a bridge in between.

Denmark · Jutland

Aarhus

A student city with a rainbow walkway on its art museum and a reconstructed old town beneath it.

Denmark · Zealand & Copenhagen

Copenhagen

A harbour city clean enough to swim in the middle of, with the densest good eating in the Nordics.

Estonia · Tallinn & the North Coast

Tallinn

A walled hanseatic town where the merchants' houses, the guild halls and the city wall all survived, and a startup district grew up beside them.

Estonia · The Islands & the South

Tartu

Estonia's intellectual capital since 1632, small, brick and argumentative.

Finland · Helsinki & the Southern Coast

Helsinki

Neoclassical centre, brutal-modern edges, and a design district you can walk in an afternoon.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Bordeaux

An 18th-century stone city on a river bend, cleaned in the 2000s, with the world's most famous vineyards at the gates.

France · Champagne & the North

Lille

Flemish in its architecture and French in its administration, with the best food market north of Lyon.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Marseille

France's oldest city, founded by Greeks in 600 BC, Mediterranean rather than French in almost every respect.

France · Paris & Île-de-France

Paris

Nineteenth-century town planning at monumental scale, with the world's densest concentration of art inside it.

France · The Alps & the East

Strasbourg

A cathedral of pink sandstone over a half-timbered island, and the European Parliament twenty minutes away.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Toulouse

The pink city: brick everything, a Romanesque basilica, aerospace money and a canal to the Mediterranean.

Georgia · Tbilisi & Kakheti

Tbilisi

Wooden balconies, a fortress above, sulphur baths below, and Persian, Russian and Soviet layers in the same street.

Germany · Berlin & the East

Berlin

Poor, sexy, enormous and unfinished — five museums on one island and a wall memorial that refuses to be comfortable.

Germany · The Rhine & the West

Cologne

A Roman city with a Gothic cathedral that took 632 years and dominates everything.

Germany · The North

Hamburg

A merchant republic and Europe's third port, with more bridges than Venice and Amsterdam together.

Germany · Bavaria & the Alps

Munich

A wealthy southern capital with the Alps an hour away and a river you can surf in the middle of a park.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Athens

A sprawling modern capital with a 2,500-year-old temple on a rock in the middle and a food scene that got very good, quietly.

Hungary · Budapest & the Danube Bend

Budapest

Hilly Buda and flat Pest, joined by bridges, with a parliament on the water and a hundred hot springs underneath.

Iceland · Reykjavík & the Southwest

Reykjavík

The northernmost capital of a sovereign state, small enough to cross on foot and loud on Friday nights.

Ireland · Dublin & the East

Dublin

Georgian squares, a 1,200-year-old river crossing, and four Nobel laureates in literature from one small city.

Ireland · The West & the Atlantic

Galway

A small port city that plays music every night of the week and treats a festival as its natural state.

Italy · Tuscany & the Centre

Lucca

An intact Renaissance wall, four kilometres round, wide enough on top to be a park, with a Roman amphitheatre turned into a square inside it.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Milan

Italy's working city: fashion, design, finance, and a Last Supper that has to be booked months ahead.

Italy · Rome & Lazio

Rome

A city where an insurance office sits on a temple and nobody comments, with the best street-level food in Italy.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Turin

The first capital of Italy: baroque arcades, an Egyptian museum second only to Cairo's, and vermouth invented in a bar here.

Kosovo · Pristina & the Plain

Pristina

A capital of cafés, brutalist landmarks and a median age under thirty.

Latvia · Riga & Around

Riga

A hanseatic old town wrapped in a boulevard ring, wrapped in the best Art Nouveau streets in Europe.

Lithuania · Vilnius & the East

Vilnius

Forty churches, endless courtyards, and a self-declared republic of artists over the river.

Luxembourg · The Capital & the Moselle

Luxembourg City

A UNESCO-listed fortress on cliffs, with 17 kilometres of tunnels underneath and a modern quarter on the plateau above.

Moldova · Chișinău & the Cellars

Chișinău

A green, low-rise capital of parks and Soviet-era planning, with the wine country immediately outside it.

Netherlands · The Randstad

Amsterdam

A 17th-century canal plan that still works, with two of the world's great collections inside it.

Netherlands · The Randstad

Rotterdam

Flattened in 1940 and rebuilt as an argument for modern architecture, on Europe's largest port.

Netherlands · The Randstad

Utrecht

Amsterdam's canals at half the scale and a fifth of the crowd, with wharf cellars at water level.

North Macedonia · Skopje & the North

Skopje

A capital with the largest Ottoman bazaar in the Balkans on one bank and a marble-clad neoclassical rebuild on the other.

Norway · Sørlandet & the South Coast

Kristiansand

A grid-planned town on the warmest stretch of Norwegian coast, with a bathing park in the middle of it.

Norway · Østlandet & the East

Oslo

A capital that spent its oil money on architecture and then put a forest and a fjord within a tram ride of the result.

Norway · Northern Norway

Tromsø

A university town at 69°N with more bars per head than anywhere else in Norway, and the aurora directly overhead.

Norway · Trøndelag

Trondheim

A student city built around the northernmost medieval cathedral in the world, and the end of a thousand-year pilgrim path.

Poland · Masovia & the Centre

Warsaw

Eighty-five per cent destroyed in 1944 and rebuilt from Canaletto's paintings, with a Stalinist tower and glass towers around it.

Portugal · Lisbon & the West

Lisbon

A tiled, hilly, sea-facing capital rebuilt on a grid after the 1755 earthquake, with trams that still climb the old parts.

Portugal · Porto & the Douro

Porto

Steep, granite and blue-tiled, with port lodges across the river and an ocean at the end of the tram line.

Romania · Bucharest & the Danube Delta

Bucharest

Belle Époque boulevards, communist megastructures, and the second-largest administrative building on earth.

Serbia · Belgrade & Vojvodina

Belgrade

A fortress above the confluence of the Sava and the Danube, with floating clubs below it and brutalist New Belgrade opposite.

Slovakia · Bratislava & the Southwest

Bratislava

A compact old town under a white castle, with a communist-era bridge and a UFO on top of it.

Slovenia · Ljubljana, the Karst & the Coast

Ljubljana

A capital of 300,000 with a car-free centre, a castle on the hill and Plečnik's architecture holding it together.

Spain · Catalonia & the East

Barcelona

A Roman city inside a medieval city inside a 19th-century grid, with Gaudí's work scattered across all three.
