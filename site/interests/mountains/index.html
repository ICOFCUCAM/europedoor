Europe/Experiences/Mountains

Travelling for

Mountains

63 cities across 30 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Albania · The Albanian Alps

Theth

A valley village of stone houses with no road in until recently, a lock-in tower, and a waterfall above it.

Andorra · The Valleys

Andorra la Vella

Europe's highest capital at 1,023 metres, in a narrow valley, mostly shops with mountains directly behind them.

Andorra · The Valleys

Madriu-Perafita-Claror

A glacial valley with no road into it, on the UNESCO list for a cultural landscape of summer pasture and stone huts.

Armenia · The North & South

Dilijan

A forested spa town called 'Armenian Switzerland', with two of the country's finest monasteries in the woods nearby.

Armenia · The North & South

Tatev

A 9th-century monastery on a cliff in the far south, reached by the world's longest reversible cable car.

Austria · Tyrol & the West

Innsbruck

A university city at 574 metres with a 2,300-metre mountain reachable from the centre in twenty minutes.

Azerbaijan · The Northwest & the Mountains

Xınalıq

A village at 2,300 metres whose people speak a language related to nothing else, reached by a single mountain road.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Bansko

A stone-built mountain town under Pirin, with cheap skiing in winter and hut-to-hut walking in summer.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Rila Monastery

A fortified monastery in a forested valley, founded in the 10th century, with striped arcades and frescoes over every surface.

Croatia · Inland Croatia

Plitvice Lakes

Sixteen terraced lakes falling into each other over travertine barriers that are still growing.

Cyprus · The Troodos Mountains

Omodos & the wine villages

Cobbled mountain villages growing indigenous grapes nobody else has, around a 12th-century monastery.

Czechia · Prague & Bohemia

Český ráj (Turnov)

'Bohemian Paradise': sandstone towers, rock cities and castles on pinnacles, ninety minutes from Prague.

France · The Alps & the East

Annecy

A canal town on the cleanest large lake in Europe, with the Alps standing straight up behind it.

France · The Alps & the East

Chamonix

A valley town under the highest mountain in western Europe, where alpinism was invented.

France · The Auvergne & the Massif Central

Clermont-Ferrand

A city built in black volcanic stone under a chain of eighty dormant volcanoes.

France · Corsica

Corte

The old Corsican capital in the mountainous centre, with the university that keeps the language alive and the GR20 above it.

Georgia · The Greater Caucasus

Mestia, Svaneti

A mountain region of defensive stone towers built by families against each other, under 5,000-metre peaks.

Georgia · The Greater Caucasus

Stepantsminda (Kazbegi)

A village under a 5,047-metre volcano, with a 14th-century church on a spur above it.

Germany · Bavaria & the Alps

Berchtesgaden

An alpine corner surrounded on three sides by Austria, with a fjord-green lake below the Watzmann.

Germany · Bavaria & the Alps

Munich

A wealthy southern capital with the Alps an hour away and a river you can surf in the middle of a park.

Greece · The Islands

Chania, Crete

A Venetian harbour with an Ottoman quarter behind it, on an island large enough to be its own country.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Delphi

The navel of the ancient world, on a terrace of Mount Parnassus above a sea of olive trees.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Kardamyli & the Mani

Stone tower houses on a wild peninsula where blood feuds ran into the 20th century, above a clear sea.

Greece · Northern Greece

Meteora (Kalambaka)

Six working monasteries on top of sandstone pillars, reached for centuries by rope ladder and net.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Mystras

A Byzantine city on a spur of Taygetos, abandoned in the 1950s and left standing: palaces, churches and frescoes.

Greece · The Islands

Naxos

The largest Cycladic island: mountain villages, marble quarries, a working agricultural interior and long sandy beaches.

Greece · Northern Greece

Pelion (Makrinitsa)

Stone villages with slate roofs on a wooded mountain that drops to beaches on both sides.

Greece · Northern Greece

Zagori (Monodendri)

Forty-six stone villages in the Pindus mountains, linked by packhorse bridges over the deepest gorge in the world by ratio.

Iceland · The South Coast & East

Höfn & Jökulsárlón

A langoustine port beside Europe's largest ice cap, where icebergs float out to sea past a car park.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Ortisei & the Dolomites

Pale limestone towers over alpine meadows, in a valley where Ladin is still the first language.

Kosovo · Prizren & the Mountains

Peja & Rugova

A bazaar town at the mouth of a limestone gorge, with a patriarchate monastery and the Accursed Mountains behind.

Liechtenstein · The Rhine Valley & the Mountains

Malbun

The country's only ski area, in a high bowl at 1,600 metres, with marmots and a chairlift.

Liechtenstein · The Rhine Valley & the Mountains

Vaduz

A capital of 5,700 people under a castle the prince actually lives in, with a black concrete art museum on the main street.

Montenegro · The Bay of Kotor & the Coast

Kotor

A walled Venetian town at the head of the bay, with fortifications climbing 260 metres up the cliff behind it.

Montenegro · Durmitor & the North

Žabljak

The highest town in the Balkans at 1,450 metres, at the edge of Durmitor national park.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Flåm

A hamlet at the end of a fjord arm, famous for a railway that drops 865 metres in twenty kilometres.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Geiranger

A UNESCO fjord arm 15 km long with waterfalls falling straight into it and abandoned farms on ledges above.

Norway · Northern Norway

Lofoten (Svolvær)

Granite peaks that rise straight from the sea, red fishermen's cabins below them, and cod drying on racks every spring.

Norway · Northern Norway

Narvik

An ice-free ore port at the end of a Swedish railway, ringed by mountains that drop into the fjord.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Odda & Trolltunga

A smelter town at the end of a fjord arm, and a slab of rock that sticks 700 metres out over a lake.

Norway · Østlandet & the East

Rjukan

A company town in a gorge so deep the sun does not reach it for six months, so the town installed mirrors on the mountain.

Norway · Northern Norway

Senja

Norway's second-largest island — Lofoten's landscape without Lofoten's traffic, and almost no one on the trails.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Stavanger

Oil capital with a preserved wooden old town, and the most photographed cliff in Norway an hour away.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Voss

A lake town between two fjords that decided to become Norway's outdoor sports capital and succeeded.

Poland · Lesser Poland & the South

Zakopane & the Tatras

A wooden mountain town under the only alpine range in Poland, with its own architectural style.

Romania · Transylvania

Brașov

A Saxon town under a wooded mountain, with the largest gothic church between Vienna and Istanbul.

San Marino · Monte Titano

City of San Marino

A walled town at 750 metres with three towers along the crest and the Adriatic visible on a clear day.

Serbia · Western & Southern Serbia

Mokra Gora

A mountain village on the Šargan Eight, a narrow-gauge line that climbs a figure of eight to gain height.

Slovakia · The Tatras & the North

Poprad & the High Tatras

The gateway town to a 26-kilometre range that reaches 2,655 metres and behaves like the Alps.

Slovenia · The Julian Alps

Bled

An island church in a glacial lake under a cliff-top castle — the most photographed thing in the country and still lovely at 7 am.

Slovenia · The Julian Alps

Bovec & the Soča

An alpine valley on an unreally turquoise river, and the front line of the Isonzo battles above it.

Spain · Andalusia

Granada

The last Moorish city in Spain, under the Sierra Nevada, with the Alhambra above it.

Spain · The Balearic Islands

Palma de Mallorca

A cathedral with a Gaudí interior on the harbour, and the Tramuntana mountains behind it for the other three seasons.

Spain · Green Spain

Picos de Europa (Cangas de Onís)

Limestone peaks twenty kilometres from the sea, with a cable car, gorges and blue cheese aged in caves.

Spain · Aragón & the Pyrenees

Torla & Ordesa

A stone village at the mouth of a glacial canyon, under the third-highest peak in the Pyrenees.

Sweden · Swedish Lapland

Abisko

A rain-shadowed valley with Sweden's clearest winter skies and the northern end of its greatest trail.

Switzerland · The Bernese Oberland

Lauterbrunnen

A valley with vertical walls and seventy-two waterfalls, and car-free villages on the shelves above it.

Switzerland · Graubünden & the East

St. Moritz & the Engadin

A high valley of lakes at 1,800 metres, with sgraffito villages and 322 days of sun a year.

Switzerland · Valais, Ticino & the South

Zermatt

A car-free village under the most photographed mountain on earth, with skiing possible in July.

Türkiye · The Aegean & Mediterranean

Fethiye & the Lycian coast

Rock-cut tombs in the cliffs above a harbour, and a 540-kilometre coastal path starting nearby.
