Travelling for

Art & museums

58 cities across 29 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Tirana

A capital whose mayor painted the grey communist blocks in primary colours, and where a nuclear bunker is now a museum.

Armenia · Yerevan & the Ararat Plain

Yerevan

A capital rebuilt in pink volcanic stone to a 1924 plan, with Ararat filling the southern horizon.

Austria · Vienna & the East

Vienna

Habsburg on the outside, Freud and Klimt and social housing on the inside, with the world's best-funded music scene.

Belgium · Flanders

Antwerp

Rubens's city and the world's diamond exchange, with a fashion school that reset the industry in the 1980s.

Belgium · Flanders

Bruges

A trading city frozen when its river silted up in the 1500s, which is why it looks like this.

Belgium · Brussels & Wallonia

Brussels

Art Nouveau streets, comic-strip walls, a gilded square, and the machinery of a continent in the eastern quarter.

Belgium · Flanders

Ghent

Bruges's rival, larger, younger and still a working student city rather than a museum of itself.

Bulgaria · Thrace & the Black Sea

Plovdiv

One of Europe's oldest continuously inhabited cities, on six hills, with a Roman theatre still in use.

Croatia · Inland Croatia

Zagreb

A central European capital of coffee terraces and museums, with almost nothing Mediterranean about it.

Cyprus · Nicosia & the South Coast

Paphos

A harbour town with Roman villa floors that are among the finest mosaics anywhere, and royal tombs cut into the rock.

Denmark · Jutland

Aarhus

A student city with a rainbow walkway on its art museum and a reconstructed old town beneath it.

Denmark · Jutland

Skagen

The country's northern tip, where two seas meet visibly and a school of painters came for the light.

Estonia · The Islands & the South

Tartu

Estonia's intellectual capital since 1632, small, brick and argumentative.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Arles

A Roman arena still in use, the Rhône delta at the door, and the year Van Gogh painted two hundred canvases here.

France · Champagne & the North

Lille

Flemish in its architecture and French in its administration, with the best food market north of Lyon.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Marseille

France's oldest city, founded by Greeks in 600 BC, Mediterranean rather than French in almost every respect.

France · Provence & the Mediterranean

Nice

An Italian city until 1860, with an old town that still cooks like one and a pebble bay that never closes.

France · Paris & Île-de-France

Paris

Nineteenth-century town planning at monumental scale, with the world's densest concentration of art inside it.

Germany · Berlin & the East

Berlin

Poor, sexy, enormous and unfinished — five museums on one island and a wall memorial that refuses to be comfortable.

Germany · Berlin & the East

Dresden

A baroque royal capital burned in 1945 and rebuilt stone by numbered stone, on a river bend.

Germany · Berlin & the East

Leipzig

Bach's church, a peaceful revolution that started in it, and a cotton mill full of painters.

Germany · Bavaria & the Alps

Munich

A wealthy southern capital with the Alps an hour away and a river you can surf in the middle of a park.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Athens

A sprawling modern capital with a 2,500-year-old temple on a rock in the middle and a food scene that got very good, quietly.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Hydra

An island two hours from Athens with no cars and no bicycles at all — mules, boats and feet.

Hungary · Budapest & the Danube Bend

Szentendre

A Serbian Orthodox artists' town on the river, forty minutes from the capital by suburban rail.

Ireland · Dublin & the East

Dublin

Georgian squares, a 1,200-year-old river crossing, and four Nobel laureates in literature from one small city.

Italy · Tuscany & the Centre

Arezzo

A hill town with Piero della Francesca's greatest fresco cycle and none of Florence's queue.

Italy · Umbria & the Marches

Assisi

A pink stone town on Monte Subasio, built around the basilica of a man who rejected everything it is made of.

Italy · Tuscany & the Centre

Florence

A banking city that funded the Renaissance and kept nearly all of it inside a twenty-minute walk.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Milan

Italy's working city: fashion, design, finance, and a Last Supper that has to be booked months ahead.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Naples

Loud, dense, Greek in origin, with the best pizza and the best archaeological museum in the world.

Italy · Rome & Lazio

Rome

A city where an insurance office sits on a temple and nobody comments, with the best street-level food in Italy.

Italy · Tuscany & the Centre

Siena

A medieval city that lost to Florence and was preserved by losing, arranged around a shell-shaped square.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Turin

The first capital of Italy: baroque arcades, an Egyptian museum second only to Cairo's, and vermouth invented in a bar here.

Italy · Umbria & the Marches

Urbino

A hilltop ducal palace that was the model Renaissance court, in a town that has been a university since 1506.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Venice

A city on wooden piles in a lagoon, with no roads, fewer than 50,000 residents left, and no equivalent anywhere.

Kosovo · Pristina & the Plain

Pristina

A capital of cafés, brutalist landmarks and a median age under thirty.

Latvia · Riga & Around

Riga

A hanseatic old town wrapped in a boulevard ring, wrapped in the best Art Nouveau streets in Europe.

Liechtenstein · The Rhine Valley & the Mountains

Vaduz

A capital of 5,700 people under a castle the prince actually lives in, with a black concrete art museum on the main street.

Lithuania · Vilnius & the East

Vilnius

Forty churches, endless courtyards, and a self-declared republic of artists over the river.

Malta · Malta

Valletta

A planned fortress city of 1566, on a peninsula between two harbours, with Caravaggio's largest painting in its cathedral.

Netherlands · The Randstad

Amsterdam

A 17th-century canal plan that still works, with two of the world's great collections inside it.

Norway · Fjord Norway

Balestrand

A Sognefjord village of dragon-style timber villas, where 19th-century painters came and then stayed.

Norway · Østlandet & the East

Oslo

A capital that spent its oil money on architecture and then put a forest and a fjord within a tram ride of the result.

Romania · Bucovina & Maramureș

Gura Humorului & the painted monasteries

The base for a group of 15th- and 16th-century monasteries frescoed on the outside walls and still bright.

Russia · Moscow & the Golden Ring

Moscow

A fortress, a cathedral square and a metro system built as underground palaces.

Russia · The Northwest

Saint Petersburg

A planned imperial capital of canals and neoclassical facades, built on piles in a delta from 1703.

Spain · Catalonia & the East

Barcelona

A Roman city inside a medieval city inside a 19th-century grid, with Gaudí's work scattered across all three.

Spain · Green Spain

Bilbao

A steel city that commissioned one building in 1997 and changed what cities thought was possible.

Spain · Madrid & the Centre

Cuenca

Houses hanging over a gorge on two sides, with an abstract art museum inside them.

Spain · Madrid & the Centre

Madrid

A high plateau capital with three of the world's great galleries in one kilometre and a nightlife that starts at midnight.

Spain · Andalusia

Málaga

A port that spent a decade turning itself into a museum city, with Picasso's birthplace and a Moorish fortress above.

Spain · Madrid & the Centre

Toledo

A city on a granite outcrop in a river loop, where Christian, Jewish and Muslim Spain were one place for centuries.

Sweden · Stockholm & the Archipelago

Stockholm

Water on every side, a preserved medieval core, and the best-funded museums in the Nordics.

Switzerland · Valais, Ticino & the South

Lugano

Palm trees, a glacial lake and Italian spoken everywhere, twenty minutes from the Alps.

United Kingdom · London & the South East

London

A city of villages that grew together, with the free national collections of a former empire in the middle of it.

United Kingdom · The West Country

St Ives

A Cornish fishing town whose light brought the modernists, and kept them.

Vatican City · Vatican City

Vatican City

A state the size of a large park, holding Michelangelo's ceiling, his dome and his Pietà.
