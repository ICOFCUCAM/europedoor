Europe/Experiences/Wild nature

Travelling for

Wild nature

108 cities across 43 countries are tagged for this. The Journey Planner weights the same tag, so what you see here is what it will build from.

Albania · Tirana, the Riviera & the South

Himarë & the Riviera

Ionian villages between mountain and sea, with beaches that were empty ten years ago and are filling fast.

Albania · The Albanian Alps

Theth

A valley village of stone houses with no road in until recently, a lock-in tower, and a waterfall above it.

Andorra · The Valleys

Madriu-Perafita-Claror

A glacial valley with no road into it, on the UNESCO list for a cultural landscape of summer pasture and stone huts.

Armenia · The North & South

Dilijan

A forested spa town called 'Armenian Switzerland', with two of the country's finest monasteries in the woods nearby.

Armenia · Yerevan & the Ararat Plain

Garni & Geghard

A Hellenistic temple on a gorge, and a monastery partly carved out of the rock face next door.

Austria · Vienna & the East

Dürnstein & the Wachau

A blue baroque tower over the Danube, terraced vineyards behind, and the castle where Richard the Lionheart was held.

Austria · Salzburg & the Salzkammergut

Hallstatt

A lakeside village under a salt mountain, with 7,000 years of continuous mining and more visitors than it can hold.

Azerbaijan · The Northwest & the Mountains

Xınalıq

A village at 2,300 metres whose people speak a language related to nothing else, reached by a single mountain road.

Belarus · The Forests

Belovezhskaya Pushcha

The Belarusian half of Europe's last primeval lowland forest, with bison and trees that have never been cut.

Belgium · Brussels & Wallonia

Dinant

A citadel on a cliff over the Meuse, a bulbous cathedral below, and the birthplace of the saxophone.

Bosnia and Herzegovina · Herzegovina

Blagaj

A dervish monastery built against a cliff at the source of the Buna, where a river comes out of a cave at full size.

Bulgaria · Sofia & the Mountains

Bansko

A stone-built mountain town under Pirin, with cheap skiing in winter and hut-to-hut walking in summer.

Croatia · Inland Croatia

Plitvice Lakes

Sixteen terraced lakes falling into each other over travertine barriers that are still growing.

Czechia · Moravia

Mikulov

A chateau town on the Austrian border, surrounded by the country's best vineyards and a limestone ridge.

Czechia · Prague & Bohemia

Český Krumlov

A castle town in a tight loop of the Vltava, essentially unchanged since the 18th century.

Czechia · Prague & Bohemia

Český ráj (Turnov)

'Bohemian Paradise': sandstone towers, rock cities and castles on pinnacles, ninety minutes from Prague.

Denmark · Jutland

Skagen

The country's northern tip, where two seas meet visibly and a school of painters came for the light.

Estonia · The Islands & the South

Kuressaare, Saaremaa

An island town around a moated bishop's castle, on Estonia's largest and slowest island.

Estonia · Tallinn & the North Coast

Lahemaa

A national park of bog, forest, manor houses and abandoned Soviet submarine bases, an hour east of the capital.

Finland · Finnish Lapland

Inari

The centre of Sámi Finland, on a lake with three thousand islands.

Finland · The Lakeland

Savonlinna

A 15th-century castle on an island in the middle of Saimaa, which hosts opera in its courtyard each July.

France · Champagne & the North

Amiens

The largest gothic cathedral in France by volume, and market gardens on canals behind it.

France · The Alps & the East

Annecy

A canal town on the cleanest large lake in Europe, with the Alps standing straight up behind it.

France · The Alps & the East

Chamonix

A valley town under the highest mountain in western Europe, where alpinism was invented.

France · The Auvergne & the Massif Central

Clermont-Ferrand

A city built in black volcanic stone under a chain of eighty dormant volcanoes.

France · Corsica

Corte

The old Corsican capital in the mountainous centre, with the university that keeps the language alive and the GR20 above it.

France · The Auvergne & the Massif Central

Le Puy-en-Velay

Chapels on top of volcanic plugs, green lentils with their own appellation, and the start of the oldest road to Santiago.

France · The Loire & the Southwest

Sarlat & the Dordogne

A honey-coloured medieval town in a valley of castles, walnut groves and the oldest painted caves in Europe.

Georgia · The Greater Caucasus

Stepantsminda (Kazbegi)

A village under a 5,047-metre volcano, with a 14th-century church on a spur above it.

Germany · The Rhine & the West

Bacharach & the Rhine Gorge

Half-timbered wine village under a ruined chapel, in the 65-kilometre UNESCO stretch of castles.

Germany · Bavaria & the Alps

Berchtesgaden

An alpine corner surrounded on three sides by Austria, with a fjord-green lake below the Watzmann.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Delphi

The navel of the ancient world, on a terrace of Mount Parnassus above a sea of olive trees.

Greece · The Islands

Ikaria

A steep island where people routinely live past ninety, festivals start at midnight and shops open when they open.

Greece · Northern Greece

Ioannina

An Ottoman lake city with an island in it, a silversmithing tradition and the gateway to Zagori.

Greece · The Islands

Kefalonia

The largest Ionian island: an underground lake, a mountain of black fir, and the loggerhead turtles offshore.

Greece · The Islands

Milos

A volcanic island of white rock formations, coloured mining beaches and boat-only coves.

Greece · Athens & the Peloponnese

Olympia

Where the games were held every four years for a millennium, in a wooded valley between two rivers.

Greece · Northern Greece

Pelion (Makrinitsa)

Stone villages with slate roofs on a wooded mountain that drops to beaches on both sides.

Greece · Northern Greece

Zagori (Monodendri)

Forty-six stone villages in the Pindus mountains, linked by packhorse bridges over the deepest gorge in the world by ratio.

Hungary · Balaton & the Wine Country

Tihany, Lake Balaton

A lavender peninsula with an abbey on a cliff, sticking into a shallow warm lake.

Iceland · The South Coast & East

Höfn & Jökulsárlón

A langoustine port beside Europe's largest ice cap, where icebergs float out to sea past a car park.

Iceland · The South Coast & East

Vík í Mýrdal

A village of three hundred under a glacier, with black sand and dangerous surf.

Iceland · The Westfjords & North

Ísafjörður

The Westfjords' capital: 2,700 people at the end of a fjord under a flat-topped mountain.

Iceland · Reykjavík & the Southwest

Þingvellir

A rift valley where the Atlantic plates pull apart, and where Iceland's parliament met in the open air from 930.

Ireland · Dublin & the East

Glendalough

A 6th-century monastic city in a glacial valley with two lakes, an hour from Dublin.

Ireland · The West & the Atlantic

The Aran Islands (Inis Mór)

Three limestone islands where Irish is the daily language and stone walls divide fields the size of rooms.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Bellagio & Lake Como

A village on the point where the lake divides, with villa gardens on both arms and ferries between them.

Italy · Rome & Lazio

Civita di Bagnoregio

A village on an eroding tufa pillar, reached by a footbridge, with about a dozen permanent residents left.

Italy · The South & the Islands

Lipari & the Aeolians

Seven volcanic islands off Sicily, one of them erupting every twenty minutes, all of them on the UNESCO list.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Ortisei & the Dolomites

Pale limestone towers over alpine meadows, in a valley where Ladin is still the first language.

Italy · The North & the Dolomites

Vernazza & the Cinque Terre

Five fishing villages on a cliff coast, joined by terraced footpaths and a railway in a tunnel.

Kosovo · Prizren & the Mountains

Peja & Rugova

A bazaar town at the mouth of a limestone gorge, with a patriarchate monastery and the Accursed Mountains behind.

Latvia · Kurzeme & the Coast

Kuldīga

A wooden town on Europe's widest waterfall, which is only two metres high but 249 metres across.

Latvia · Riga & Around

Sigulda

Three castles above a sandstone river gorge, an hour from Riga by train.

Liechtenstein · The Rhine Valley & the Mountains

Malbun

The country's only ski area, in a high bowl at 1,600 metres, with marmots and a chairlift.

Lithuania · The Coast & Centre

Nida, Curonian Spit

Wooden fishermen's houses under a moving dune, on a sand spit between lagoon and sea.

Lithuania · Vilnius & the East

Trakai

A red brick island castle in a lake, and the Karaim community that has lived beside it since the 14th century.

Luxembourg · Mullerthal & the Ardennes

Echternach

The country's oldest town, with a 7th-century abbey and a hopping procession on the UNESCO list.

Luxembourg · The Capital & the Moselle

Remich & the Moselle

Terraced vineyards on a slow river, with Germany on the far bank and Crémant cellars on this one.

Luxembourg · Mullerthal & the Ardennes

Vianden

A restored hilltop castle above a river village, where Victor Hugo spent his exile.
