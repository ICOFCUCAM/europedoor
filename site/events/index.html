04What a month is made of

What a month is made of.

Eight kinds of fixture across the 150, and each one has a month it belongs to. Five months are decisive — they hold at least an average month’s share of the year and half or more of what is on is one kind: February is cultural (10 of 13), April is religious (9 of 15), June is seasonal (10 of 20), September is food (8 of 16), December is market (9 of 14).

And the busiest month is the least characteristic. July holds 28 fixtures, more than any other, and no single kind is more than 43% of them — where February has 13 and 77% of those are one thing. A crowded month is not the same as a month with a character, which is the whole reason this band is not a ranking.
