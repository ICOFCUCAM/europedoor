Coast path erosion, the Lycian Way

540 kilometres of waymarked path opened in 1999, now carrying far more feet than it was built for, across soft limestone and through villages with no maintenance budget.

What it needs

Waymark repainting, drainage work on the eroded sections above Kabak, and payment to the village households who maintain the stages nearest them.
