Wadden Sea mudflat guiding

Walking the exposed sea floor at low tide requires a guide who knows the channels and the tide tables, and the licensing and training of those guides is entirely voluntary work.

What it needs

Training and licensing costs for new guides, and safety equipment for the routes to Ameland and Schiermonnikoog.
