Faith & heritage

Pilgrimage routes, cathedrals, monasteries and sacred sites of several traditions.

42 across 23 countries

  • Pilgrimage routes 20
  • Cathedrals & churches 13
  • Monasteries 4
  • Sacred sites 16

The four bars are each a share of the largest rather than of the whole: an experience can answer more than one of these, so they sum to 53 against 42.

  1. Garni & Geghard, Armenia · Sacred site · low

    Chant in a rock-cut chamber

    Singers perform in the carved chambers because the acoustics are extraordinary; the sound arrives from the stone itself.

  2. Rila Monastery, Bulgaria · Sacred site · low

    A night in a monastery cell

    Simple rooms, an early curfew, and the courtyard entirely to yourself at dawn.

  3. Omodos & the wine villages, Cyprus · Sacred site · low

    The barn-roofed painted churches

    Small stone chapels under enormous pitched roofs, frescoed inside floor to ceiling; several need a key fetched from a village house.

  4. Prague, Czechia · Walk or hike · low

    Vyšehrad and the river bend

    The city's first castle, a cemetery of composers, and the whole of Prague from the ramparts without a crowd.

  5. Amboise & the Loire châteaux, France · Rail, road or ride · moderate

    Château to château by bicycle

    900 km of signed, flat riverside route; two days of it between Blois and Tours is the best introduction.

  6. Le Puy-en-Velay, France · Walk or hike · low

    The first day of the Via Podiensis

    The oldest of the Santiago routes leaves from the cathedral steps after the 7 am pilgrim mass. The first day to Saint-Privat is twenty-three kilometres and sets the tone.

  7. Mont-Saint-Michel, France · Walk or hike · moderate

    Cross the bay on foot

    Guided, barefoot, over quicksand and channels; the mount is a different thing entirely when you arrive the medieval way.

  8. Mestia, Svaneti, Georgia · Walk or hike · moderate

    Mestia to Ushguli on foot

    Four days between villages with guesthouses each night, no need to carry a tent, and glaciers in view most of the way.

  9. Bacharach & the Rhine Gorge, Germany · Walk or hike · low

    Above the vineyards on the Rheinsteig

    The high path gives you the whole gorge; the trains and boats below give you the way back.

  10. Delphi, Greece · Sacred site · low

    The sacred way at opening

    The site opens at eight and the coaches arrive at ten. Two hours of Delphi alone is a different place from Delphi at noon.

  11. Ikaria, Greece · Music & performance · low

    A panigiri that ends at dawn

    A village saint's day: one long table, live violin and lute, dancing in a circle, and food priced to cover the church roof.

  12. Meteora (Kalambaka), Greece · Walk or hike · low

    The old monks' paths

    Stone-cut trails link the pillars through the woods; three hours on foot beats a whole day by road.

  13. Milos, Greece · On the water · moderate

    Round the island by boat

    Half the coast has no road to it. A day boat from Adamas is the only way to see Kleftiko and the southern coves.

  14. Nafplio, Greece · Sacred site · low

    The acoustics at Epidaurus

    Sit in the top row and have someone whisper on the orchestra floor. Built in the 4th century BC and still unexplained in full.

  15. Assisi, Italy · Sacred site · low

    Up to the hermitage on foot

    An hour uphill through holm oak to the caves Francis actually used, which are quieter and more convincing than the basilica.

  16. Bologna, Italy · Walk or hike · low

    The 666 arches to San Luca

    Four kilometres of covered walkway climbing out of the city, built so a procession would never get wet.

  17. Florence, Italy · Sacred site · low

    Vespers at San Miniato

    Gregorian chant in an 11th-century church above the city, at 17:30, free, and almost always uncrowded.

  18. Rome, Italy · Walk or hike · low

    The Appian Way on a Sunday

    The road is closed to traffic on Sundays: basalt paving from 312 BC, catacombs, aqueducts and countryside, inside the city.

  19. Turin, Italy · Cellar & vineyard · low

    Vermouth where it was invented

    Carpano started here in 1786. The historic cafés still serve it the way it was drunk: cold, alone, with a small plate.

  20. Vaduz, Liechtenstein · Walk or hike · low

    A day on the Liechtenstein Trail

    75 km through all eleven municipalities; the Vaduz-to-Balzers section takes an afternoon and crosses a quarter of the country.

  21. Šiauliai, Lithuania · Sacred site · low

    The Hill of Crosses

    Not a monument — an act repeated by hundreds of thousands of people, most of them anonymous, over two centuries.

  22. Kotor, Montenegro · Walk or hike · low

    The city walls at dawn

    Ninety minutes up, the whole bay below, and a small church halfway. Go before eight or don't go in July.

  23. Žabljak, Montenegro · On the water · moderate

    Rafting the Tara

    The deepest canyon in Europe, drinkable water the whole way, and a season from May to October.

  24. Amsterdam, Netherlands · Rail, road or ride · low

    The city the way it is used

    Rent for a day, ride out to Amsterdam-Noord on the free ferry, and stop thinking of the bicycle as an activity.

  25. Ohrid, North Macedonia · On the water · low

    Across the lake to Sveti Naum

    An hour and a half by boat to a monastery at the springs on the Albanian border, past cliffs with nothing else on them.

  26. Skopje, North Macedonia · Walk or hike · low

    The Old Bazaar at working hours

    Coppersmiths, filigree, tea houses and mosques — a working market rather than a preserved one, best before noon.

  27. Flåm, Norway · Rail, road or ride · moderate

    Ride the Rallarvegen down

    The old navvies' road from Myrdal, gravel all the way, downhill nearly all the way — a bike, a train ticket and a whole afternoon.

  28. Trondheim, Norway · Sacred site · low

    The cathedral, from the workshop side

    The stoneyard has been cutting replacement blocks continuously for a century; see that before you see the nave.

  29. Kraków, Poland · Museum or collection · moderate

    The salt cathedral at Wieliczka

    A chapel 101 metres underground with chandeliers of salt crystal, carved by miners over decades in their own time.

  30. Brașov, Romania · Wildlife & nature · moderate

    A bear hide in the Carpathians

    Licensed hides run by biologists at dusk; Romania holds most of Europe's brown bears and this is the ethical way to see one.

  31. Gura Humorului & the painted monasteries, Romania · Sacred site · low

    The blue at Voroneț

    A 1547 exterior fresco whose blue has not faded in five hundred winters, and whose recipe is still not known.

  32. Bovec & the Soča, Slovenia · Walk or hike · low

    The Walk of Peace

    Trenches, chapels and cemeteries left in place along the mountain front where 300,000 died; the signage is unusually honest.

  33. Granada, Spain · Sacred site · moderate

    The Nasrid palaces, on a timed ticket

    Half-hour entry slots, sold out weeks ahead. Take the earliest of the day — the courtyards were designed for low light and few people.

  34. Granada, Spain · Food & table · low

    Tapas, the Granada way

    Every drink comes with a plate you did not order and cannot choose; four bars is a dinner.

  35. Ribeira Sacra, Spain · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

    Heroic viticulture, from the river

    The growers call it viticultura heroica without irony: everything is carried up and down by hand or cable, and the boat is the only way to see why.

  36. Santiago de Compostela, Spain · Walk or hike · low

    The last hundred kilometres

    From Sarria, five days, and the minimum distance for a compostela. Busy, sociable, and still the real thing.

  37. Lauterbrunnen, Switzerland · Walk or hike · moderate

    The North Face trail from Mürren

    Two hours on a balcony path with the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau directly across the valley the whole way.

  38. Fethiye & the Lycian coast, Türkiye · Walk or hike · low

    A section of the Lycian Way

    Waymarked in red and white since 1999, through pine, ruins and goat country, with village pensions at the ends of most stages.

  39. Göreme, Cappadocia, Türkiye · Walk or hike · low

    The valleys on foot, not from the air

    Ihlara, Rose and Pigeon valleys are walked in a morning each, with abandoned rock churches nobody signposts.

  40. Canterbury, United Kingdom · Walk or hike · low

    The last day of the Pilgrims' Way

    Twenty miles from Chilham along the North Downs, arriving on foot the way it was meant to be done.

  41. Vatican City, Vatican City · Sacred site · low

    Up the dome of St Peter's

    The staircase follows the curve of the dome and tilts with it; the reward is the whole of Rome and the gardens nobody else sees.

  42. Vatican City, Vatican City · Sacred site · moderate

    The scavi, under the basilica

    A Roman street of tombs beneath the church, ending at what may be St Peter's grave. Twelve people at a time, booked by email, months ahead.

How this list is built: Everything we classified as sacred, plus anything whose own name or description matches one of the 33 terms under the sub-categories below. Both tests are about the experience itself. Neither the name of the town nor the interests the town is tagged with can put something in this list: a salt mine in Hallstatt is not a market, and a bunker museum in a city known for its food is not a meal.
