Faith & heritage

Pilgrimage routes, cathedrals, monasteries and sacred sites of several traditions.

59 across 31 countries

  • Pilgrimage routes 21
  • Cathedrals & churches 13
  • Monasteries 22
  • Sacred sites 16

  1. Chant in a rock-cut chamber

    Singers perform in the carved chambers because the acoustics are extraordinary; the sound arrives from the stone itself.

    Garni & Geghard, Armenia · Sacred site · low

  2. A heuriger on the city edge

    Wine taverns in Vienna's own vineyards, open only when the grower has wine to sell — a pine branch over the door means open.

    Vienna, Austria · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  3. A lambic brewery, spontaneously fermented

    Beer left open to the air of one valley, aged three years in barrel — the last place in the world doing this at scale.

    Brussels, Belgium · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  4. A night in a monastery cell

    Simple rooms, an early curfew, and the courtyard entirely to yourself at dawn.

    Rila Monastery, Bulgaria · Sacred site · low

  5. The palace substructures

    The cellars survive because they were filled with rubbish for centuries; they map the imperial floor plan above exactly.

    Split, Croatia · Walk or hike · low

  6. The barn-roofed painted churches

    Small stone chapels under enormous pitched roofs, frescoed inside floor to ceiling; several need a key fetched from a village house.

    Omodos & the wine villages, Cyprus · Sacred site · low

  7. A Moravian cellar at harvest

    Burčák — half-fermented, cloudy and briefly available each September — is drunk cold from the barrel and does not travel.

    Mikulov, Czechia · Cellar & vineyard · low

  8. Vyšehrad and the river bend

    The city's first castle, a cemetery of composers, and the whole of Prague from the ramparts without a crowd.

    Prague, Czechia · Walk or hike · low

  9. Château to château by bicycle

    900 km of signed, flat riverside route; two days of it between Blois and Tours is the best introduction.

    Amboise & the Loire châteaux, France · Rail, road or ride · moderate

  10. Châteauneuf-du-Pape, in the village

    Thirteen permitted grape varieties and stones the size of fists that hold the day's heat into the night; the growers pour in their own cellars.

    Avignon, France · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  11. A small Saint-Émilion cellar

    Skip the classified growths and visit a family making 20,000 bottles; you will taste from the barrel and be told the truth about the vintage.

    Bordeaux, France · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  12. The first day of the Via Podiensis

    The oldest of the Santiago routes leaves from the cathedral steps after the 7 am pilgrim mass. The first day to Saint-Privat is twenty-three kilometres and sets the tone.

    Le Puy-en-Velay, France · Walk or hike · low

  13. Cross the bay on foot

    Guided, barefoot, over quicksand and channels; the mount is a different thing entirely when you arrive the medieval way.

    Mont-Saint-Michel, France · Walk or hike · moderate

  14. Down into the crayères

    Chalk quarried by Romans, thirty metres down, holding millions of bottles at a constant temperature nobody has to pay for.

    Reims, France · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  15. Mestia to Ushguli on foot

    Four days between villages with guesthouses each night, no need to carry a tent, and glaciers in view most of the way.

    Mestia, Svaneti, Georgia · Walk or hike · moderate

  16. A qvevri cellar and a supra

    Wine fermented in clay buried to the neck, then a table that goes on for hours with a tamada running it.

    Sighnaghi & Kakheti, Georgia · Cellar & vineyard · low

  17. Above the vineyards on the Rheinsteig

    The high path gives you the whole gorge; the trains and boats below give you the way back.

    Bacharach & the Rhine Gorge, Germany · Walk or hike · low

  18. The sacred way at opening

    The site opens at eight and the coaches arrive at ten. Two hours of Delphi alone is a different place from Delphi at noon.

    Delphi, Greece · Sacred site · low

  19. A panigiri that ends at dawn

    A village saint's day: one long table, live violin and lute, dancing in a circle, and food priced to cover the church roof.

    Ikaria, Greece · Music & performance · low

  20. The old monks' paths

    Stone-cut trails link the pillars through the woods; three hours on foot beats a whole day by road.

    Meteora (Kalambaka), Greece · Walk or hike · low

  21. Round the island by boat

    Half the coast has no road to it. A day boat from Adamas is the only way to see Kleftiko and the southern coves.

    Milos, Greece · On the water · moderate

  22. The acoustics at Epidaurus

    Sit in the top row and have someone whisper on the orchestra floor. Built in the 4th century BC and still unexplained in full.

    Nafplio, Greece · Sacred site · low

  23. Vines grown in baskets

    No trellises: the vines are woven into circles on the ground to shelter the fruit from wind and catch night moisture.

    Santorini, Greece · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  24. A mouldy cellar in Tokaj

    The black fungus on the walls regulates humidity and is left alone deliberately; taste dry furmint first and six-puttonyos last.

    Tokaj, Hungary · Cellar & vineyard · low

  25. Up to the hermitage on foot

    An hour uphill through holm oak to the caves Francis actually used, which are quieter and more convincing than the basilica.

    Assisi, Italy · Sacred site · low

  26. The 666 arches to San Luca

    Four kilometres of covered walkway climbing out of the city, built so a procession would never get wet.

    Bologna, Italy · Walk or hike · low

  27. Vespers at San Miniato

    Gregorian chant in an 11th-century church above the city, at 17:30, free, and almost always uncrowded.

    Florence, Italy · Sacred site · low

  28. The Appian Way on a Sunday

    The road is closed to traffic on Sundays: basalt paving from 312 BC, catacombs, aqueducts and countryside, inside the city.

    Rome, Italy · Walk or hike · low

  29. Vermouth where it was invented

    Carpano started here in 1786. The historic cafés still serve it the way it was drunk: cold, alone, with a small plate.

    Turin, Italy · Cellar & vineyard · low

  30. A day on the Liechtenstein Trail

    75 km through all eleven municipalities; the Vaduz-to-Balzers section takes an afternoon and crosses a quarter of the country.

    Vaduz, Liechtenstein · Walk or hike · low

  31. The Hill of Crosses

    Not a monument — an act repeated by hundreds of thousands of people, most of them anonymous, over two centuries.

    Šiauliai, Lithuania · Sacred site · low

  32. Little Switzerland on foot

    112 km of waymarked trail through gorges narrow enough to touch both walls; a day section is easy and free.

    Echternach, Luxembourg · Walk or hike · low

  33. Driving through a wine cellar

    200 km of limestone galleries with street names, and two million bottles. Visitors go in by vehicle because walking would take days.

    Chișinău, Moldova · Cellar & vineyard · low

  34. The city walls at dawn

    Ninety minutes up, the whole bay below, and a small church halfway. Go before eight or don't go in July.

    Kotor, Montenegro · Walk or hike · low

  35. Rafting the Tara

    The deepest canyon in Europe, drinkable water the whole way, and a season from May to October.

    Žabljak, Montenegro · On the water · moderate

  36. The city the way it is used

    Rent for a day, ride out to Amsterdam-Noord on the free ferry, and stop thinking of the bicycle as an activity.

    Amsterdam, Netherlands · Rail, road or ride · low

  37. Across the lake to Sveti Naum

    An hour and a half by boat to a monastery at the springs on the Albanian border, past cliffs with nothing else on them.

    Ohrid, North Macedonia · On the water · low

  38. The Old Bazaar at working hours

    Coppersmiths, filigree, tea houses and mosques — a working market rather than a preserved one, best before noon.

    Skopje, North Macedonia · Walk or hike · low

  39. Ride the Rallarvegen down

    The old navvies' road from Myrdal, gravel all the way, downhill nearly all the way — a bike, a train ticket and a whole afternoon.

    Flåm, Norway · Rail, road or ride · moderate

  40. The cathedral, from the workshop side

    The stoneyard has been cutting replacement blocks continuously for a century; see that before you see the nave.

    Trondheim, Norway · Sacred site · low

  41. The salt cathedral at Wieliczka

    A chapel 101 metres underground with chandeliers of salt crystal, carved by miners over decades in their own time.

    Kraków, Poland · Museum or collection · moderate

  42. Treading grapes at harvest

    Knee-deep in a granite tank for two hours, in a line, to a rhythm — the least romantic and most enjoyable part of winemaking.

    Pinhão & the Douro valley, Portugal · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  43. Port, tasted in the right order

    White, then tawny by age, then vintage — the tawnies are where the interest is and where the guides usually rush.

    Porto, Portugal · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  44. Cork oaks and Alentejo reds

    The trees are stripped every nine years and the date is painted on the trunk; the wine estates between them charge a fraction of the Douro.

    Évora, Portugal · Cellar & vineyard · low

  45. A bear hide in the Carpathians

    Licensed hides run by biologists at dusk; Romania holds most of Europe's brown bears and this is the ethical way to see one.

    Brașov, Romania · Wildlife & nature · moderate

  46. The blue at Voroneț

    A 1547 exterior fresco whose blue has not faded in five hundred winters, and whose recipe is still not known.

    Gura Humorului & the painted monasteries, Romania · Sacred site · low

  47. The Small Carpathian wine road

    Twenty minutes from the capital: family cellars in Pezinok and Modra that sell almost nothing beyond the region.

    Bratislava, Slovakia · Cellar & vineyard · low

  48. The Walk of Peace

    Trenches, chapels and cemeteries left in place along the mountain front where 300,000 died; the signage is unusually honest.

    Bovec & the Soča, Slovenia · Walk or hike · low

  49. The Nasrid palaces, on a timed ticket

    Half-hour entry slots, sold out weeks ahead. Take the earliest of the day — the courtyards were designed for low light and few people.

    Granada, Spain · Sacred site · moderate

  50. Tapas, the Granada way

    Every drink comes with a plate you did not order and cannot choose; four bars is a dinner.

    Granada, Spain · Food & table · low

  51. Heroic viticulture, from the river

    The growers call it viticultura heroica without irony: everything is carried up and down by hand or cable, and the boat is the only way to see why.

    Ribeira Sacra, Spain · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  52. The last hundred kilometres

    From Sarria, five days, and the minimum distance for a compostela. Busy, sociable, and still the real thing.

    Santiago de Compostela, Spain · Walk or hike · low

  53. The North Face trail from Mürren

    Two hours on a balcony path with the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau directly across the valley the whole way.

    Lauterbrunnen, Switzerland · Walk or hike · moderate

  54. A section of the Lycian Way

    Waymarked in red and white since 1999, through pine, ruins and goat country, with village pensions at the ends of most stages.

    Fethiye & the Lycian coast, Türkiye · Walk or hike · low

  55. The valleys on foot, not from the air

    Ihlara, Rose and Pigeon valleys are walked in a morning each, with abandoned rock churches nobody signposts.

    Göreme, Cappadocia, Türkiye · Walk or hike · low

  56. The last day of the Pilgrims' Way

    Twenty miles from Chilham along the North Downs, arriving on foot the way it was meant to be done.

    Canterbury, United Kingdom · Walk or hike · low

  57. Three distilleries on foot

    Ardbeg, Lagavulin and Laphroaig sit within five kilometres of each other along one coastal path — walk it, for obvious reasons.

    Islay, United Kingdom · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  58. Up the dome of St Peter's

    The staircase follows the curve of the dome and tilts with it; the reward is the whole of Rome and the gardens nobody else sees.

    Vatican City, Vatican City · Sacred site · low

  59. The scavi, under the basilica

    A Roman street of tombs beneath the church, ending at what may be St Peter's grave. Twelve people at a time, booked by email, months ahead.

    Vatican City, Vatican City · Sacred site · moderate

How this list is built: Everything we classified as sacred, plus anything whose own name or description matches one of the 33 terms under the sub-categories below. Both tests are about the experience itself. Neither the name of the town nor the interests the town is tagged with can put something in this list: a salt mine in Hallstatt is not a market, and a bunker museum in a city known for its food is not a meal.
