How this list is built

Everything we classified as table, cellar, plus anything whose own name or description matches one of the 41 terms under the sub-categories below. Both tests are about the experience itself. Neither the name of the town nor the interests the town is tagged with can put something in this list: a salt mine in Hallstatt is not a market, and a bunker museum in a city known for its food is not a meal.
