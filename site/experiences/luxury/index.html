Experience category

Luxury

The expensive end, listed as expensive rather than as special.

5 across 2 countries

  1. Into a glacier, in winter only

    The blue caves form and collapse each year; go with a guide who checks them that morning, and only from November to March.

    Höfn & Jökulsárlón, Iceland · Walk or hike · high

  2. Snorkel the Silfra fissure

    Two degrees, a dry suit, and water filtered through lava for decades — the clearest you will ever swim in.

    Þingvellir, Iceland · On the water · high

  3. Out of town, legally

    Any trip beyond the settlement needs a guide carrying a rifle and trained to avoid using it. That is the law and it is also the honest reading of the risk.

    Longyearbyen, Norway · Wildlife & nature · high

  4. Dog sledding on the glacier

    Wheeled carts in summer, runners in winter, and the same dogs that supplied the mines before snowmobiles.

    Longyearbyen, Norway · Rail, road or ride · high

  5. An aurora night with a forecast, not a promise

    A guide who drives to clear sky — sometimes into Finland — is worth more than one who stays put; nobody can guarantee the lights, and anyone who does is selling something.

    Tromsø, Norway · Wildlife & nature · high

How this list is built: Every experience we priced in the high band. There is no separate luxury inventory and no partner arrangement behind this list — it is the expensive end of what is already in the Atlas, labelled as expensive.
