Nature

Waterfalls, fjords, forest, lakes, wildlife and the national parks that hold them.

29 across 18 countries

  • Fjords & sea inlets 1
  • Forests 3
  • Wildlife 7
  • Lakes & rivers 15

  1. The Koman Lake ferry

    Three hours through a flooded gorge on a working passenger boat that carries villagers, goats and the occasional car.

    Theth, Albania · On the water · low

  2. Into the salt mountain

    Miners' slides, a salt lake underground, and the Bronze Age tools that came out of the same shafts.

    Hallstatt, Austria · Museum or collection · moderate

  3. Vyšehrad and the river bend

    The city's first castle, a cemetery of composers, and the whole of Prague from the ramparts without a crowd.

    Prague, Czechia · Walk or hike · low

  4. Looking for the Saimaa seal

    Fewer than four hundred exist. A quiet boat, an early morning, and no promises — which is how it should be sold.

    Savonlinna, Finland · Wildlife & nature · moderate

  5. Château to château by bicycle

    900 km of signed, flat riverside route; two days of it between Blois and Tours is the best introduction.

    Amboise & the Loire châteaux, France · Rail, road or ride · moderate

  6. Swim in Lake Annecy

    Fed by mountain springs and cleaned up by a 1960s sewer scheme that is now the textbook example; you can see the bottom at four metres.

    Annecy, France · On the water · low

  7. Walk into the Calanques

    From Luminy to Sugiton in ninety minutes; in summer access is capped and booked, which is why the water is still clear.

    Marseille, France · Walk or hike · low

  8. The trumpet on the Königssee

    The boatman stops mid-lake and plays into the cliff; the echo comes back a full phrase later.

    Berchtesgaden, Germany · On the water · moderate

  9. The old monks' paths

    Stone-cut trails link the pillars through the woods; three hours on foot beats a whole day by road.

    Meteora (Kalambaka), Greece · Walk or hike · low

  10. Whales from an old oak boat

    The refitted fishing schooners cut their engines and drift; the silence is half of what you came for.

    Húsavík, Iceland · Wildlife & nature · moderate

  11. The local pool, not the lagoon

    Every district has one: hot pots at different temperatures, a strict pre-shower rule, and the day's arguments happening in them.

    Reykjavík, Iceland · On the water · low

  12. Stromboli, from the water

    The volcano has erupted more or less continuously for two thousand years. Boats hold position off the Sciara del Fuoco after dark.

    Lipari & the Aeolians, Italy · Wildlife & nature · moderate

  13. Rafting the Tara

    The deepest canyon in Europe, drinkable water the whole way, and a season from May to October.

    Žabljak, Montenegro · On the water · moderate

  14. Across the lake to Sveti Naum

    An hour and a half by boat to a monastery at the springs on the Albanian border, past cliffs with nothing else on them.

    Ohrid, North Macedonia · On the water · low

  15. Six thousand years of rock carving

    Thousands of figures — boats, reindeer, bears, people — pecked into coastal rock over five millennia, and read in order along a boardwalk.

    Alta, Norway · Museum or collection · low

  16. Saltstraumen at full flow

    Check the tide table and stand on the bridge: whirlpools ten metres across, and fishermen working the edge of them.

    Bodø, Norway · Wildlife & nature · low

  17. The free ferry, all day

    Fredrikstad runs its river crossings free of charge as ordinary transport; ride them between the old town and the new for as long as you like.

    Fredrikstad, Norway · On the water · low

  18. Out of town, legally

    Any trip beyond the settlement needs a guide carrying a rifle and trained to avoid using it. That is the law and it is also the honest reading of the risk.

    Longyearbyen, Norway · Wildlife & nature · high

  19. An aurora night with a forecast, not a promise

    A guide who drives to clear sky — sometimes into Finland — is worth more than one who stays put; nobody can guarantee the lights, and anyone who does is selling something.

    Tromsø, Norway · Wildlife & nature · high

  20. A Sámi reindeer herding afternoon

    Book with a Sámi-owned operator; the difference between a working herd and a photo backdrop is obvious within ten minutes.

    Tromsø, Norway · Wildlife & nature · moderate

  21. Bison at first light

    A guide, a hide and a 4 am start; the forest's edge meadows are where the herds actually come out.

    Białowieża, Poland · Wildlife & nature · moderate

  22. A bear hide in the Carpathians

    Licensed hides run by biologists at dusk; Romania holds most of Europe's brown bears and this is the ethical way to see one.

    Brașov, Romania · Wildlife & nature · moderate

  23. Into the delta by small boat

    The big tour boats stay in the main channels; a local with a small engine gets you into the reed corridors where the birds are.

    Tulcea & the Danube Delta, Romania · Wildlife & nature · moderate

  24. The pletna, rowed standing

    A boat design and a rowing licence handed down through the same families since 1740; there is no engine on the lake by law.

    Bled, Slovenia · On the water · moderate

  25. The bridge over the Škocjan gorge

    A river 100 metres below you, inside a mountain, crossed on a suspended footbridge. Quieter and more astonishing than its famous neighbour.

    Postojna & Škocjan, Slovenia · Walk or hike · moderate

  26. Heroic viticulture, from the river

    The growers call it viticultura heroica without irony: everything is carried up and down by hand or cable, and the boat is the only way to see why.

    Ribeira Sacra, Spain · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  27. Paella where the rice grows

    The Albufera lagoon south of the city grows the rice and cooks it over orange wood; lunchtime only, and never in the evening.

    Valencia, Spain · Food & table · moderate

  28. Float the Aare

    Walk upstream, get in, and let the river carry you back through the city — with a dry bag and a firm plan for getting out.

    Bern, Switzerland · On the water · low

  29. The slate railway to the coast

    Built in 1836 to run loaded downhill by gravity; it still descends 700 feet to Porthmadog through the woods.

    Eryri / Snowdonia (Betws-y-Coed), United Kingdom · Rail, road or ride · moderate

How this list is built: Everything we classified as wild, plus anything whose own name or description matches one of the 27 terms under the sub-categories below. Both tests are about the experience itself. Neither the name of the town nor the interests the town is tagged with can put something in this list: a salt mine in Hallstatt is not a market, and a bunker museum in a city known for its food is not a meal.
