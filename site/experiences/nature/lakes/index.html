The Koman Lake ferry

Into the salt mountain

Miners' slides, a salt lake underground, and the Bronze Age tools that came out of the same shafts.

Czechia Prague

Vyšehrad and the river bend

The city's first castle, a cemetery of composers, and the whole of Prague from the ramparts without a crowd.

France Amboise & the Loire châteaux

Château to château by bicycle

900 km of signed, flat riverside route; two days of it between Blois and Tours is the best introduction.

France Annecy

Swim in Lake Annecy

Fed by mountain springs and cleaned up by a 1960s sewer scheme that is now the textbook example; you can see the bottom at four metres.

The trumpet on the Königssee

The boatman stops mid-lake and plays into the cliff; the echo comes back a full phrase later.

The local pool, not the lagoon

Every district has one: hot pots at different temperatures, a strict pre-shower rule, and the day's arguments happening in them.

North Macedonia Ohrid

Norway Fredrikstad

The free ferry, all day

Slovenia Bled

The pletna, rowed standing

A boat design and a rowing licence handed down through the same families since 1740; there is no engine on the lake by law.

Slovenia Postojna & Škocjan

The bridge over the Škocjan gorge

A river 100 metres below you, inside a mountain, crossed on a suspended footbridge. Quieter and more astonishing than its famous neighbour.

Spain Ribeira Sacra

Heroic viticulture, from the river

The growers call it viticultura heroica without irony: everything is carried up and down by hand or cable, and the boat is the only way to see why.

Spain Valencia

Paella where the rice grows

The Albufera lagoon south of the city grows the rice and cooks it over orange wood; lunchtime only, and never in the evening.
