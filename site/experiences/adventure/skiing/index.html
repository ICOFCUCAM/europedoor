Adventure

Snow & ice

11 across 8 countries

Estonia · France · Georgia · Hungary · Iceland · Norway · Switzerland · United Kingdom

  1. Bog shoes in Lahemaa

    Snowshoe-like frames let you cross raised bog off the boardwalk — an hour from the capital and utterly silent.

    Tallinn, Estonia · Walk or hike · moderate

  2. A small Saint-Émilion cellar

    Skip the classified growths and visit a family making 20,000 bottles; you will taste from the barrel and be told the truth about the vintage.

    Bordeaux, France · Cellar & vineyard · moderate

  3. Down to the Mer de Glace

    The steps to the ice are signed with the year the glacier reached each level; the descent is a chronology as much as a walk.

    Chamonix, France · Walk or hike · moderate

  4. Mestia to Ushguli on foot

    Four days between villages with guesthouses each night, no need to carry a tent, and glaciers in view most of the way.

    Mestia, Svaneti, Georgia · Walk or hike · moderate

  5. An outdoor thermal bath in winter

    38°C water, snow on the stone, and old men playing chess on floating boards. Go early; go on a weekday.

    Budapest, Hungary · On the water · moderate

  6. Into a glacier, in winter only

    The blue caves form and collapse each year; go with a guide who checks them that morning, and only from November to March.

    Höfn & Jökulsárlón, Iceland · Walk or hike · high

  7. Lunch at a working fish counter

    Skip the tourist stalls; eat where the boats deliver, standing up, whatever came in that morning.

    Bergen, Norway · Food & table · moderate

  8. Dog sledding on the glacier

    Wheeled carts in summer, runners in winter, and the same dogs that supplied the mines before snowmobiles.

    Longyearbyen, Norway · Rail, road or ride · high

  9. An aurora night with a forecast, not a promise

    A guide who drives to clear sky — sometimes into Finland — is worth more than one who stays put; nobody can guarantee the lights, and anyone who does is selling something.

    Tromsø, Norway · Wildlife & nature · high

  10. The Bernina line to Tirano

    From glacier to palm trees in four hours, over the Landwasser viaduct and around the Brusio spiral — booked as a normal train, not a tour.

    St. Moritz & the Engadin, Switzerland · Rail, road or ride · moderate

  11. The Hampstead bathing ponds

    Three ponds, open every day of the year, including the January mornings when the ice is broken by hand.

    London, United Kingdom · On the water · low

Selected by name and description against ski, snow, ice, glacier, sled, snowshoe and aurora — matched on what we wrote about the experience, never on the name of the town. 1 more term is declared for this list and has matched nothing yet: bobsleigh.
