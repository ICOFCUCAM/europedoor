The Koman Lake ferry

Azerbaijan Baku

Petroglyphs and mud volcanoes

Rock carvings of boats and dancers from 10,000 years ago, then a field of cold mud volcanoes burping methane a few kilometres on.

Denmark Copenhagen

Swim in the harbour

Free, supervised in summer, and the result of thirty years of cleaning up a working port.

Denmark Roskilde

Row a reconstructed longship

Built with period tools from the excavated hulls; you supply the arms and learn quickly why Vikings were fit.

On the water moderate

Finland Savonlinna

Looking for the Saimaa seal

Fewer than four hundred exist. A quiet boat, an early morning, and no promises — which is how it should be sold.

Wildlife & nature moderate

France Annecy

Swim in Lake Annecy

Fed by mountain springs and cleaned up by a 1960s sewer scheme that is now the textbook example; you can see the bottom at four metres.

Germany Bacharach & the Rhine Gorge

Above the vineyards on the Rheinsteig

The high path gives you the whole gorge; the trains and boats below give you the way back.

Germany Berchtesgaden

The trumpet on the Königssee

The boatman stops mid-lake and plays into the cliff; the echo comes back a full phrase later.

Germany Hamburg

The 62 ferry, not the tour boat

A public transport ferry down the Elbe past the container terminals; same view, a fraction of the price.

Greece Chania, Crete

Down the Samaria gorge

Sixteen kilometres, one direction only, ending at a boat because there is no road out. Open May to October.

Walk or hike moderate

Greece Milos

Round the island by boat

Half the coast has no road to it. A day boat from Adamas is the only way to see Kleftiko and the southern coves.

Greece Nafplio

The acoustics at Epidaurus

Sit in the top row and have someone whisper on the orchestra floor. Built in the 4th century BC and still unexplained in full.

Iceland Húsavík

Whales from an old oak boat

The refitted fishing schooners cut their engines and drift; the silence is half of what you came for.

Iceland Þingvellir

Snorkel the Silfra fissure

Two degrees, a dry suit, and water filtered through lava for decades — the clearest you will ever swim in.

Ireland The Aran Islands (Inis Mór)

The island by bicycle

Hire at the pier, ride the length of it, and be back for the last ferry — the island's whole scale in one day.

Italy Lipari & the Aeolians

Stromboli, from the water

The volcano has erupted more or less continuously for two thousand years. Boats hold position off the Sciara del Fuoco after dark.

Wildlife & nature moderate

Malta Valletta

Across the Grand Harbour by dgħajsa

A rowed and now motorised water taxi in the old harbour shape; the cheapest and best view of the bastions.

Montenegro Žabljak

Rafting the Tara

The deepest canyon in Europe, drinkable water the whole way, and a season from May to October.

On the water moderate

Netherlands Amsterdam

The city the way it is used

Rent for a day, ride out to Amsterdam-Noord on the free ferry, and stop thinking of the bicycle as an activity.

North Macedonia Ohrid

Across the lake to Sveti Naum

An hour and a half by boat to a monastery at the springs on the Albanian border, past cliffs with nothing else on them.

On the water low

Norway Alta

Six thousand years of rock carving

Thousands of figures — boats, reindeer, bears, people — pecked into coastal rock over five millennia, and read in order along a boardwalk.

Museum or collection low

Norway Bergen

Lunch at a working fish counter

Skip the tourist stalls; eat where the boats deliver, standing up, whatever came in that morning.

Food & table moderate

Norway Fredrikstad

The free ferry, all day

Fredrikstad runs its river crossings free of charge as ordinary transport; ride them between the old town and the new for as long as you like.

On the water low

Norway Geiranger

The farms on the ledges

Skageflå was farmed until 1918 on a shelf 250 metres above the water, reached by ladder. The boat drops you at the bottom and you climb.

Walk or hike moderate

Norway Lofoten (Svolvær)

Cold-water surf at Unstad

Beach break inside the Arctic Circle, 6mm of neoprene, and a sauna afterwards that you will remember longer than the waves.

On the water moderate

Norway Oslo

The commuter ferry as a day out

Ride the public boats to Hovedøya and Gressholmen with a swim towel and a bakery bag; it is a city transport ticket, not a cruise.

On the water low

Norway Risør

The wooden boat festival

Every August the harbour fills with clinker-built boats and the people who maintain them; go for the sound of the hulls rather than the programme.

On the water moderate

Romania Tulcea & the Danube Delta

Into the delta by small boat

The big tour boats stay in the main channels; a local with a small engine gets you into the reed corridors where the birds are.

Wildlife & nature moderate

Slovenia Bled

The pletna, rowed standing

A boat design and a rowing licence handed down through the same families since 1740; there is no engine on the lake by law.

On the water moderate

Spain Ribeira Sacra

Heroic viticulture, from the river

The growers call it viticultura heroica without irony: everything is carried up and down by hand or cable, and the boat is the only way to see why.

Cellar & vineyard moderate

Sweden Stockholm

Archipelago on a commuter boat

The Waxholmsbolaget boats are public transport that happen to cross one of the world's great island landscapes.

On the water low

Türkiye Istanbul

Between continents on a commuter ferry

Twenty minutes, a glass of tea, gulls following the stern, and Europe and Asia both in view. The single best thing in the city and nearly free.
