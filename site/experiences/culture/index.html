How this list is built

Anything in a place tagged art, music, design, festivals, architecture, plus anything whose own name or description matches one of the 46 terms under the sub-categories below. The terms are matched against what we wrote about the experience and never against the name of the town, because a salt mine in Hallstatt is not a market.
