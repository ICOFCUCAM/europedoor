Where the ground is warm

Thermal Europe

Europe sits on hot water in more places than people realise, and every culture that found some built something around it: Roman baths, Ottoman hammams, Habsburg spa towns, Icelandic municipal pools and a Finnish sauna in every building. Bathing is the oldest continuous European leisure activity there is.
