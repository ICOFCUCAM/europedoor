Wine Europe

The interesting wine map of Europe is not the famous one. It includes clay vessels buried in Georgian courtyards, volcanic vines woven into baskets against Aegean wind, and slate slopes on the Rhine at sixty degrees. Every one of these places will let you taste in the cellar where it was made.
