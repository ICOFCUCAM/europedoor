A thousand years, and what remains of it

Jewish Europe

Jewish communities shaped European cities for a millennium before most of them were destroyed in the twentieth century. These places hold both halves of that: living communities and institutions, and the sites of what happened. They are visited best slowly, with the museums that were built by the communities themselves rather than about them.
