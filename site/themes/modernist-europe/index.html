The twentieth century, built

Modernist Europe

Between about 1920 and 1975 Europe rebuilt itself twice and argued about how the whole time. These are the places where the argument is still standing: social housing that worked, a manifesto in a house, a city rebuilt from nothing, and one that decided not to be rebuilt at all.
