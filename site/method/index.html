Europe Experience Score

The whole formula, on one page.

Six numbers appear on every city and country page. Here is exactly how each one is produced, because a score whose method is secret is a ranking, and rankings for sale are how travel sites lose their readers.

Nature

base 34; nature +26, mountains +22, coast +16, islands +12, winter +10, wild +6; +4 per matching experience (max 3), kinds: walk, wild, water

0–97

History

base 34; history +26, sacred +16, architecture +16, art +10, cities +4; +4 per matching experience (max 3), kinds: museum, sacred

0–97

Food & table

base 34; food +30, wine +22, festivals +6, cities +6; +4 per matching experience (max 3), kinds: table, cellar

0–97

Culture

base 34; art +22, architecture +18, music +16, design +14, festivals +10, sacred +8; +4 per matching experience (max 3), kinds: museum, stage, workshop

0–97

Adventure

base 34; mountains +24, nature +18, winter +16, coast +14, islands +10, rail +6; +4 per matching experience (max 3), kinds: walk, water, ride

0–97

Family

base 34; nature +14, coast +14, wild +12, history +8, islands +8, winter +6; music & nightlife −10; +5 per experience (max 3) that passes the family rule published at /experiences/family

0–97

Authenticity

base 34 + 10; quiet +22; not the capital +8; tagged big-city −8; +6 per experience (max 3) of kind workshop, table, wild, cellar; +2 per recorded place (max 4)

0–97

Value

110 − (midpoint of the country's daily cost band − €40) × 0.382

0–97

Two dimensions this refuses to compute

The specification this came from lists ten. Eight are computable from the dataset. These two are not, and an approximation would be worse than the gap.

Accessibility

We hold no step-free access, hearing-loop or accessible-toilet data for any place in the Atlas. Deriving a number from something else would be a guess about whether a disabled traveller can get in, and that is the worst thing on this list to guess about. The gap is stated on every place page and on /accessibility.

not computed

Romance

Not measurable from anything in this dataset. Any formula would be a proxy — sunsets, coastlines, small hotels — dressed up as evidence, and the number would be doing persuasion rather than description.

not computed

What the scores are not

  • Not measurements. They are computed from our own editorial tags. If we tag a city wrongly, its score is wrong, and the fix is to fix the tag — tell us.
  • Not quality. A 94 for Adventure means the place is about adventure, not that it does it better than a 71.
  • Not for sale. No business, tourism board or partner can change a score. There is no mechanism to; the numbers are recomputed from the dataset on every build.
  • Not capped at 100. The ceiling is 97 and the floor is 12, so nothing is ever perfect and nothing is ever worthless.

Why value is different

Five dimensions are structural — they come from tags. Value is the only one anchored to something outside our own judgement: the midpoint of the country's daily cost band, which is published on every country page and can be argued with directly.

Recomputed, never stored

No score is written into the data files. Every number on the site is derived at build time from the tags, so a score and its explanation cannot drift apart.
