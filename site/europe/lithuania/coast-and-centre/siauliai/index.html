Travel tips

Practical things about Lithuania that are not obvious from outside it.

  • The Hill of Crosses is an active devotional site, not a photo opportunity — leave one or leave it alone.
  • Užupis, in Vilnius, declared itself a republic in 1997 and has a constitution on a wall in twenty languages.
  • The Curonian Spit is shared with the Russian exclave of Kaliningrad; the border is real and closed to casual crossing.
