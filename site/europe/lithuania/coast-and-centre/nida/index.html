The Coast & Centre, Lithuania

Nida, Curonian Spit

Wooden fishermen's houses under a moving dune, on a sand spit between lagoon and sea.

≈ Coast & beaches♣ Wild nature○ Islands

Why visit

  • Parnidis dune and its sundial, with the Russian border visible south
  • Thomas Mann's summer house, where he wrote in 1930–32
  • Amber, smoked fish, and no cars to speak of
