Travel tips

Practical things about Armenia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • The borders with Türkiye and Azerbaijan are closed; overland routes go through Georgia or Iran.
  • The genocide of 1915 is central to national memory and the memorial is treated with real solemnity.
  • Monasteries are working churches; women are not required to cover but modest dress is expected.
