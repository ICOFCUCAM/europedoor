Places to see

Everything recorded across The North, in one list.

Speicherstadt

The largest warehouse district in the world, red brick on oak piles, built in the 1880s for coffee, tea, spices and carpets. Best walked at dusk when the canals go still.

Hamburg · Quarter

Elbphilharmonie

A glass wave on top of a 1960s cocoa warehouse. The public plaza between the two is free and gives the best view of the harbour in the city.

Hamburg · Theatre

Holstentor

The city gate that leans, because it was built on marshy ground in 1464 and has been settling ever since. It is on the back of the old fifty-mark note.

Lübeck · Monument
