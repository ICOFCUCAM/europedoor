Berlin & the East, Germany

Berlin

Poor, sexy, enormous and unfinished — five museums on one island and a wall memorial that refuses to be comfortable.

Why visit

  • Museum Island, especially the Pergamon's scale and the Neues Museum's Nefertiti
  • The Berlin Wall Memorial at Bernauer Straße — the honest one, not Checkpoint Charlie
  • Tempelhof: an airport turned into the largest urban park in the world
  • The Stasi Museum, in the ministry's own offices

Places to see

4 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Museumsinsel

Five museums on one island in the Spree, holding the Pergamon altar, the bust of Nefertiti and enough classical sculpture to make a day feel short.

Museum · a full day

East Side Gallery

The longest surviving stretch of the Wall, 1.3 km of it, painted by artists from twenty-one countries in 1990 and repainted since as the originals weathered.

Monument · 1 hour

Brandenburg Gate

Prussian triumphal arch, then a gate nobody could pass through for twenty-eight years, and now the place every photograph of the city is taken from.

Monument · 30 minutes

Tempelhofer Feld

A closed airport turned into a park, and the runways were left. Berliners cycle, skate and barbecue on a 300-hectare field where planes landed until 2008.

Park · 2 hours

Travel tips

Practical things about Germany that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Cash is still king in a surprising number of bakeries, bars and taxis.
  • Sunday closing is near-total; buy food on Saturday.
  • Jaywalking against a red man is genuinely disapproved of, especially with children present.
