Places to see

Everything recorded across Berlin &amp; the East, in one list.

Museumsinsel

Five museums on one island in the Spree, holding the Pergamon altar, the bust of Nefertiti and enough classical sculpture to make a day feel short.

Berlin · Museum

East Side Gallery

The longest surviving stretch of the Wall, 1.3 km of it, painted by artists from twenty-one countries in 1990 and repainted since as the originals weathered.

Berlin · Monument

Brandenburg Gate

Prussian triumphal arch, then a gate nobody could pass through for twenty-eight years, and now the place every photograph of the city is taken from.

Berlin · Monument

Tempelhofer Feld

A closed airport turned into a park, and the runways were left. Berliners cycle, skate and barbecue on a 300-hectare field where planes landed until 2008.

Berlin · Park

Frauenkirche

Rebuilt from its own rubble between 1994 and 2005, with the blackened original stones set back where they were found. The dark blocks in the pale facade are the point.

Dresden · Church

The Zwinger

A baroque pleasure palace built around a courtyard, now holding the Old Masters gallery and Raphael's Sistine Madonna — the one with the two bored cherubs at the bottom.

Dresden · Monument
