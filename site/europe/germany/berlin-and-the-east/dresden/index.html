Dresden

A baroque royal capital burned in 1945 and rebuilt stone by numbered stone, on a river bend.

Why visit

  • The Frauenkirche, reassembled from the rubble and finished in 2005
  • The Green Vault's treasury, booked in timed slots
  • Saxon Switzerland's sandstone towers, forty minutes upriver

Travel tips

Practical things about Germany that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Cash is still king in a surprising number of bakeries, bars and taxis.
  • Sunday closing is near-total; buy food on Saturday.
  • Jaywalking against a red man is genuinely disapproved of, especially with children present.
