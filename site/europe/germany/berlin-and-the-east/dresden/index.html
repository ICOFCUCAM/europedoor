Places to see

Frauenkirche

Rebuilt from its own rubble between 1994 and 2005, with the blackened original stones set back where they were found. The dark blocks in the pale facade are the point.

Church · 1 hour

The Zwinger

A baroque pleasure palace built around a courtyard, now holding the Old Masters gallery and Raphael's Sistine Madonna — the one with the two bored cherubs at the bottom.

Monument · 2–3 hours

2 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Travel tips

Practical things about Germany that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Cash is still king in a surprising number of bakeries, bars and taxis.
  • Sunday closing is near-total; buy food on Saturday.
  • Jaywalking against a red man is genuinely disapproved of, especially with children present.
