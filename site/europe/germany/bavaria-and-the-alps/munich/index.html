Bavaria & the Alps, Germany

Munich

A wealthy southern capital with the Alps an hour away and a river you can surf in the middle of a park.

● Food◈ Art & museums▦ Big cities▲ Mountains

Why visit

  • The Eisbach standing wave, surfed year-round in the English Garden
  • The Alte Pinakothek's Rubens and Dürer
  • A beer garden where you may legally bring your own food
  • Day trains to the mountains and the lakes
