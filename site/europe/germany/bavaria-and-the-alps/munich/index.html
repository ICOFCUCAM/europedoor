  • A beer garden where you may legally bring your own food
  • Day trains to the mountains and the lakes

Places to see

2 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Englischer Garten

Larger than Central Park, with a standing wave on the Eisbach where surfers queue in wetsuits all year, and beer gardens under chestnut trees at the far end.

Park · 2–3 hours

The Residenz

The Wittelsbach city palace: ten courtyards, 130 rooms open, and a treasury holding crowns from the tenth century onward.

Castle · 3 hours

Things to do

A beer garden, done correctly

Buy the beer, bring the food, sit at the unmarked communal tables — the marked ones are for served customers and the distinction matters.

Food & drink

Travel tips

Practical things about Germany that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Cash is still king in a surprising number of bakeries, bars and taxis.
  • Sunday closing is near-total; buy food on Saturday.
  • Jaywalking against a red man is genuinely disapproved of, especially with children present.
