Places to see

Everything recorded across Bavaria & the Alps, in one list.

Englischer Garten

Larger than Central Park, with a standing wave on the Eisbach where surfers queue in wetsuits all year, and beer gardens under chestnut trees at the far end.

Munich · Park

The Residenz

The Wittelsbach city palace: ten courtyards, 130 rooms open, and a treasury holding crowns from the tenth century onward.

Munich · Castle

Things to do

A beer garden, done correctly

Buy the beer, bring the food, sit at the unmarked communal tables — the marked ones are for served customers and the distinction matters.

Munich · Food & table
