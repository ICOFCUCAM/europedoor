Bavaria & the Alps, Germany

Berchtesgaden

An alpine corner surrounded on three sides by Austria, with a fjord-green lake below the Watzmann.

▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature❄ Snow & winter

Why visit

  • Königssee, crossed by electric boats since 1909 so the echo carries
  • The salt mine, worked since 1517, entered on a wooden slide
  • Steep, serious walking on the Watzmann
