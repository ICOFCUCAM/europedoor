Places to see

The Long Room, Trinity College

Sixty-five metres of oak barrel vault holding 200,000 of the library's oldest books, with the Book of Kells shown below it a page at a time.

Library · 1–2 hours

Kilmainham Gaol

Where the leaders of the 1916 Rising were held and shot. Guided entry only, and the tours sell out days ahead — which is the single most useful thing to know about visiting Dublin.

Monument · 2 hours

2 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Travel tips

Practical things about Ireland that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Ireland is in the EU but not Schengen; the Common Travel Area with the UK works separately.
  • Traditional sessions are for the musicians — sit, listen, and do not clap over the tune's end.
  • Rural addresses often have no street numbers; Eircodes work where satnav does not.
