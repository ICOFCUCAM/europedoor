Places to see

Everything recorded across Dublin & the East, in one list.

The Long Room, Trinity College

Sixty-five metres of oak barrel vault holding 200,000 of the library's oldest books, with the Book of Kells shown below it a page at a time.

Dublin · Library

Kilmainham Gaol

Where the leaders of the 1916 Rising were held and shot. Guided entry only, and the tours sell out days ahead — which is the single most useful thing to know about visiting Dublin.

Dublin · Monument

The Round Tower and monastic city

A sixth-century monastic settlement in a glacial valley, with a 30 m round tower whose door is four metres off the ground for reasons nobody has to explain twice.

Glendalough · Monastery
