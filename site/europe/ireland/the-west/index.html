Three limestone islands where Irish is the daily language and stone walls divide fields the size of rooms.

Slea Head

The western end of the peninsula, looking at the Blasket Islands, with beehive huts and an oratory built without mortar that has stayed dry for twelve hundred years.

Dingle · Viewpoint
