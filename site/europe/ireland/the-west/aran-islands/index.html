The West & the Atlantic, Ireland

The Aran Islands (Inis Mór)

Three limestone islands where Irish is the daily language and stone walls divide fields the size of rooms.

○ Islands⚑ History & ruins♣ Wild nature≈ Coast & beaches

Why visit

  • Dún Aonghasa, a prehistoric fort on an 87-metre cliff with no railing
  • 1,600 km of dry stone wall on an island 14 km long
  • Bicycles, because there is almost nothing else
