The portrait

Why Switzerland belongs in your Europe.

Switzerland is four language regions, twenty-six cantons and a public transport system that works so well it changes how you travel: a postbus meets the train, the train meets the boat, and all three are punctual to the minute. The Alps take up 60% of the land and are threaded with cable cars and mountain huts. Nothing is cheap and almost nothing is broken.

Where Switzerland is worth going

5 destinations across 3 travel regions.

Getting around

The rail network is the product. A Swiss Travel Pass covers trains, buses, boats and most mountain railways; without one, individual tickets are eye-watering. Timetables interlock, so a four-minute connection is a real connection.

When to come

High mountain trails open from late June and close with the first snow in October. September is the best month up high: stable weather, clear air, cows coming down. May and November are between seasons and many lifts simply stop.

Worth knowing

  • Not in the EU; in Schengen. The franc is the currency and euros are accepted at a poor rate.
  • Mountain weather turns fast and huts must be booked; the SAC app carries conditions.
  • Tap water in villages and from most fountains is drinkable unless marked otherwise.

At the table

  • Cheese in its own right — Gruyère, Appenzeller, raclette scraped off the wheel
  • Rösti, and the Röstigraben that divides German from French Switzerland
  • Chocolate, bought from a maker rather than an airport
