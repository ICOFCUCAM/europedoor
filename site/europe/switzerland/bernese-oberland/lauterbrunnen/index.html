The Bernese Oberland, Switzerland

Lauterbrunnen

A valley with vertical walls and seventy-two waterfalls, and car-free villages on the shelves above it.

▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature═ Slow travel by rail

Why visit

  • Mürren and Gimmelwald, reachable only by cable car or on foot
  • Trümmelbach: ten glacier waterfalls inside the mountain
  • The Schilthorn, and the Eiger north face opposite
