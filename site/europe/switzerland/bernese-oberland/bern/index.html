The Bernese Oberland, Switzerland

Bern

The federal capital: six kilometres of arcades, a river you swim down, and Einstein's flat above a café.

⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins▦ Big cities

Why visit

  • Swimming the Aare with the current, as the city does all summer
  • The Zytglogge clock's mechanism, from 1530
  • Paul Klee's collection in a Renzo Piano wave
