Ljubljana

A capital of 300,000 with a car-free centre, a castle on the hill and Plečnik's architecture holding it together.

Why visit

  • Plečnik's bridges, market colonnade and library — one architect's whole city
  • The riverside bars on summer evenings
  • Metelkova, a squatted former barracks turned art complex
