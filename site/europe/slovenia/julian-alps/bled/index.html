The Julian Alps, Slovenia

Bled

An island church in a glacial lake under a cliff-top castle — the most photographed thing in the country and still lovely at 7 am.

♣ Wild nature▲ Mountains● Food

What earns the time

  • Rowing to the island in a pletna, and ringing the wishing bell
  • The 6 km lakeside path, flat and swimmable at intervals
  • Vintgar gorge, on boardwalks over the water
