Plovdiv

One of Europe's oldest continuously inhabited cities, on six hills, with a Roman theatre still in use.

Why visit

  • The Roman theatre, hosting opera and rock concerts since restoration
  • The Old Town's 19th-century merchant houses with overhanging upper floors
  • Kapana, a craft district in the old artisans' quarter

Travel tips

Practical things about Bulgaria that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Shaking the head means yes and nodding can mean no — it catches everyone out.
  • In the EU but the lev is still the currency, pegged to the euro.
  • Mountain huts in Rila and Pirin are basic, cheap and often full at weekends.
