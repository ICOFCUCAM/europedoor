Sofia & the Mountains, Bulgaria

Bansko

A stone-built mountain town under Pirin, with cheap skiing in winter and hut-to-hut walking in summer.

❄ Snow & winter▲ Mountains● Food♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • Pirin national park's glacial lakes and the Vihren summit
  • Mehanas serving Bansko sausage and heavy red wine
  • Lift passes at a fraction of Alpine prices
