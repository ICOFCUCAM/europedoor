Sofia & the Mountains, Bulgaria

Sofia

A capital with Roman ruins under the metro stations and a mountain at the end of the tram line.

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places● Food▦ Big cities

Why visit

  • Alexander Nevsky cathedral and its icon crypt
  • The Roman Serdica ruins, excavated and left open in the middle of the city
  • Vitosha mountain, 2,290 metres, reached by public transport
