Sofia & the Mountains, Bulgaria

Rila Monastery

A fortified monastery in a forested valley, founded in the 10th century, with striped arcades and frescoes over every surface.

Historic site · 1 nights

Travel tips

Practical things about Bulgaria that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Shaking the head means yes and nodding can mean no — it catches everyone out.
  • In the EU but the lev is still the currency, pegged to the euro.
  • Mountain huts in Rila and Pirin are basic, cheap and often full at weekends.
