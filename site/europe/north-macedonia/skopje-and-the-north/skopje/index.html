Why visit

  • The Old Bazaar, trading continuously since the 12th century
  • The Stone Bridge, and the argument in stone on either side of it
  • Matka canyon, twenty minutes out, with caves and a kayak club

Travel tips

Practical things about North Macedonia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • The name changed in 2019 after a long dispute with Greece; both the old and new forms are still heard.
  • Wild Ohrid trout is protected and should not be ordered.
  • Cash is common outside the cities.
