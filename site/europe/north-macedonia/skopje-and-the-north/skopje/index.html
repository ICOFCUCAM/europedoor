Skopje & the North, North Macedonia

Skopje

A capital with the largest Ottoman bazaar in the Balkans on one bank and a marble-clad neoclassical rebuild on the other.

Capital city · 1–2 nights

⚑ History & ruins● Food⌂ Architecture▦ Big cities

Why go

  1. 01The Old Bazaar, trading continuously since the 12th century
  2. 02The Stone Bridge, and the argument in stone on either side of it
  3. 03Matka canyon, twenty minutes out, with caves and a kayak club

Travel tips

Practical things about North Macedonia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • The name changed in 2019 after a long dispute with Greece; both the old and new forms are still heard.
  • Wild Ohrid trout is protected and should not be ordered.
  • Cash is common outside the cities.
