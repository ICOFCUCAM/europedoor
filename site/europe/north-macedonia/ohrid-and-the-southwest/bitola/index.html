Ohrid & the Southwest, North Macedonia

Bitola

A former consular city with a wide pedestrian avenue, and the Roman ruins of Heraclea on its edge.

City · 1–2 nights

⚑ History & ruins● Food⌂ Architecture

Why go

  1. 01Širok Sokak, built for nineteenth-century diplomats and still promenaded
  2. 02Heraclea Lyncestis's mosaic floors
  3. 03Pelister national park's pines and glacial lakes above the town

Travel tips

Practical things about North Macedonia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • The name changed in 2019 after a long dispute with Greece; both the old and new forms are still heard.
  • Wild Ohrid trout is protected and should not be ordered.
  • Cash is common outside the cities.
