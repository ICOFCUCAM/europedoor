The portrait

Why North Macedonia belongs in your Europe.

North Macedonia is landlocked, mountainous, and centred on Lake Ohrid — between three and five million years old, with sixty endemic species and a town of churches on its shore. Skopje was flattened by an earthquake in 1963, rebuilt in concrete, and then rebuilt again in the 2010s in a neoclassical style that its own citizens argue about constantly.

Where North Macedonia is worth going

3 destinations across 2 travel regions.

Getting around

Buses connect Skopje, Ohrid and Bitola; the roads through the mountains are slow. Trains exist and are slower still. A car opens up the monasteries and Mavrovo.

When to come

Ohrid is warm and swimmable from June to September and the town is busy in August. Spring and early autumn are best for the mountains and the monasteries. Winter is cold and there is decent cheap skiing at Mavrovo.

Worth knowing

  • The name changed in 2019 after a long dispute with Greece; both the old and new forms are still heard.
  • Wild Ohrid trout is protected and should not be ordered.
  • Cash is common outside the cities.

At the table

  • Tavče gravče: baked beans in a clay dish, the national dish
  • Ajvar, made in vast quantities every September
  • Ohrid trout — check it is farmed, as the endemic wild one is protected
