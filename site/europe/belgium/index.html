The portrait

Why Belgium belongs in your Europe.

Belgium is small, divided and quietly excellent at the things that matter daily: bread, beer, chips, painting and comics. The medieval cloth towns of Flanders — Bruges, Ghent, Antwerp — got rich early and kept the buildings. Brussels is the accidental capital of a continent and much better company than its reputation.

Where Belgium is worth going

5 destinations across 2 travel regions.

Getting around

Everything is within ninety minutes by train and the network is dense and cheap. Flemish cities are flat and cycle well. Brussels is bilingual on every sign, which confuses satnavs more than people.

When to come

Spring and early autumn are best; Bruges in July is a queue. December brings serious Christmas markets and the beer suits the weather.

Worth knowing

  • Language boundaries are legally fixed: French in Wallonia, Dutch in Flanders, and Brussels officially both.
  • Trappist beer is brewed by monks in six Belgian abbeys and sold to fund them, not for profit.
  • Sunday is genuinely closed outside the tourist centres.

At the table

  • Chips, twice fried, from a frituur, with a sauce you choose badly the first time
  • Trappist and lambic beer — Westvleteren, Orval, gueuze that tastes of cider and horse
  • Waterzooi and moules, and chocolate bought where it is made
