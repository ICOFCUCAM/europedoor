Castle · Ghent, Belgium

Gravensteen

A moated twelfth-century castle in the middle of the city, complete with the counts' quarters and a distinctly unsentimental collection of judicial instruments.

Give it 1–2 hours · Open year-round · 51.057°N, 3.721°E
