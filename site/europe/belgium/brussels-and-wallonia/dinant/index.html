Travel tips

Practical things about Belgium that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Language boundaries are legally fixed: French in Wallonia, Dutch in Flanders, and Brussels officially both.
  • Trappist beer is brewed by monks in six Belgian abbeys and sold to fund them, not for profit.
  • Sunday is genuinely closed outside the tourist centres.
