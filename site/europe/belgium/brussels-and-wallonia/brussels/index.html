Places to see

Grand-Place

Rebuilt in five years after a French bombardment flattened it in 1695, which is why a medieval square is uniformly late baroque.

Square · 1 hour

Royal Museums of Fine Arts

Four museums in one, and the Magritte collection is the largest anywhere — 200-odd works, which is enough to see him stop being a joke and start being a painter.

Museum · 3 hours

2 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Travel tips

Practical things about Belgium that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Language boundaries are legally fixed: French in Wallonia, Dutch in Flanders, and Brussels officially both.
  • Trappist beer is brewed by monks in six Belgian abbeys and sold to fund them, not for profit.
  • Sunday is genuinely closed outside the tourist centres.
