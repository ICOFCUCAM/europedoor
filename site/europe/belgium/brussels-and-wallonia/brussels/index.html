Brussels & Wallonia, Belgium

Brussels

Art Nouveau streets, comic-strip walls, a gilded square, and the machinery of a continent in the eastern quarter.

What earns the time

  • The Grand-Place, best at night when it is lit and empty
  • Horta's houses, four of them UNESCO-listed
  • The comic strip route: fifty painted gables across the centre
  • Lambic breweries in Anderlecht, fermenting on wild yeast
