Green Spain

The Atlantic north: Basque food, Cantabrian mountains, and Galicia at the end of the pilgrimage.

5 destinations · 8 places recorded · about 11 nights to see it all

● Food≈ Coast & beaches▲ Mountains✝ Sacred places◑ Wine & drink

In the region

What Green Spain is made of

All five of them, the same set the drawing above plots.

Destinations

San Sebastián

A shell-shaped bay, a belle époque town, and more Michelin stars per head than anywhere on earth.

City
2–3 nights

Santiago de Compostela

The end of the Camino: a granite city that rains constantly and fills each afternoon with people who have walked there.

City
1–2 nights

Picos de Europa (Cangas de Onís)

Limestone peaks twenty kilometres from the sea, with a cable car, gorges and blue cheese aged in caves.

National park
2–3 nights

Bilbao

A steel city that commissioned one building in 1997 and changed what cities thought was possible.

City
2–3 nights

Ribeira Sacra

Vineyards on canyon walls above the Sil, worked from boats and by hand, with Romanesque monasteries in the woods.

Valley
2–3 nights

Places to see

Everything recorded across Green Spain, in one list.

La Concha

A shell-shaped bay with a promenade the whole way round and an island in the middle of it.

San Sebastián · Beach

Monte Igueldo

A funicular from 1912 to the headland, with the bay laid out exactly as the postcards have it.

San Sebastián · Viewpoint

Santiago Cathedral

The end of the Camino, and the botafumeiro swinging across the transept when it is scheduled.

Santiago de Compostela · Church

Mercado de Abastos

Galician seafood bought at one counter and cooked at another, which is the correct system.

Santiago de Compostela · Market

Ruta del Cares

Twelve kilometres cut into a cliff face for a hydro channel, now the most walked path in northern Spain.

Picos de Europa (Cangas de Onís) · Mountain

Lagos de Covadonga

Two glacial lakes at 1,100 metres above a shrine, with the road closed to cars in high season.

Picos de Europa (Cangas de Onís) · Mountain

Guggenheim Bilbao

Titanium on a riverbank, and the single building most often credited with changing a city’s mind about itself.

Bilbao · Museum

Vizcaya Bridge

The world’s oldest transporter bridge, from 1893, still carrying cars across in a hanging gondola.

Bilbao · Bridge

Things to do

A pintxo crawl, done properly

One drink and one signature dish per bar, standing, then move. Sitting down is how visitors get it wrong.

San Sebastián · Food & table

The last hundred kilometres

From Sarria, five days, and the minimum distance for a compostela. Busy, sociable, and still the real thing.

Santiago de Compostela · Walk or hike

Across on the hanging gondola

The world’s oldest transporter bridge, on the UNESCO list, still running every eight minutes as a local crossing.

Bilbao · Rail, road or ride

Heroic viticulture, from the river

The growers call it viticultura heroica without irony: everything is carried up and down by hand or cable, and the boat is the only way to see why.

Ribeira Sacra · Cellar & vineyard
