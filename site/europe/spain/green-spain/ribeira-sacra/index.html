Green Spain, Spain

Ribeira Sacra

Vineyards on canyon walls above the Sil, worked from boats and by hand, with Romanesque monasteries in the woods.

◑ Wine & drink♣ Wild nature✝ Sacred places● Food

Why visit

  • Terraces at gradients that need a winch, above a 500-metre gorge
  • Mencía reds from vines nobody can mechanise
  • Catamarans up the canyon, and eighteen Romanesque churches within an hour

Things to do

Heroic viticulture, from the river

The growers call it viticultura heroica without irony: everything is carried up and down by hand or cable, and the boat is the only way to see why.
