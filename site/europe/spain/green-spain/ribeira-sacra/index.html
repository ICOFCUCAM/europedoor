Green Spain, Spain

Ribeira Sacra

Vineyards on canyon walls above the Sil, worked from boats and by hand, with Romanesque monasteries in the woods.

Valley · 2–3 nights

◑ Wine & drink♣ Wild nature✝ Sacred places● Food

Why go

  1. 01Terraces at gradients that need a winch, above a 500-metre gorge
  2. 02Mencía reds from vines nobody can mechanise
  3. 03Catamarans up the canyon, and eighteen Romanesque churches within an hour

Travel tips

Practical things about Spain that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch runs 14:00–16:00 and kitchens genuinely close; dinner starts at 21:00.
  • Many museums are free in the last two hours of the day.
  • The Camino de Santiago is a working pilgrimage with its own etiquette, not a long-distance hiking route.
