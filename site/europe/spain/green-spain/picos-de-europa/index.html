Picos de Europa (Cangas de Onís)

Limestone peaks twenty kilometres from the sea, with a cable car, gorges and blue cheese aged in caves.

Why visit

  • The Cares gorge, twelve kilometres cut into a cliff
  • Cabrales cheese, matured in mountain caves
  • Covadonga's lakes and the shrine below them
