Torla & Ordesa

A stone village at the mouth of a glacial canyon, under the third-highest peak in the Pyrenees.

Why visit

  • Ordesa canyon and the Cola de Caballo, a full day on a good path
  • Monte Perdido, 3,355 metres, and the bearded vultures over it
  • A shuttle bus in summer because the valley road is closed to cars

Travel tips

Practical things about Spain that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch runs 14:00–16:00 and kitchens genuinely close; dinner starts at 21:00.
  • Many museums are free in the last two hours of the day.
  • The Camino de Santiago is a working pilgrimage with its own etiquette, not a long-distance hiking route.
