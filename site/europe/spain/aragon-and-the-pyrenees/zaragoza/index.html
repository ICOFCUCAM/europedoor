Zaragoza

Roman, Moorish, Christian and industrial in the same city, with a basilica the size of a cathedral on the Ebro.

⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins● Food▦ Big cities

What earns the time

  • The Aljafería, an 11th-century Moorish palace, now the regional parliament
  • Basílica del Pilar's eleven domes on the river
  • Mudéjar brickwork, on the UNESCO list, all over the old town
