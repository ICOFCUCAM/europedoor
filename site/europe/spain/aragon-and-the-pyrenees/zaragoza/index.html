Zaragoza

Roman, Moorish, Christian and industrial in the same city, with a basilica the size of a cathedral on the Ebro.

Why visit

  • The Aljafería, an 11th-century Moorish palace, now the regional parliament
  • Basílica del Pilar's eleven domes on the river
  • Mudéjar brickwork, on the UNESCO list, all over the old town

Travel tips

Practical things about Spain that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch runs 14:00–16:00 and kitchens genuinely close; dinner starts at 21:00.
  • Many museums are free in the last two hours of the day.
  • The Camino de Santiago is a working pilgrimage with its own etiquette, not a long-distance hiking route.
