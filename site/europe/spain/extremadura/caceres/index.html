Extremadura, Spain

Cáceres

A walled old town of Moorish towers and conquistador palaces, with storks nesting on most of them.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture● Food

What earns the time

  • A UNESCO old town with no modern buildings inside the walls at all
  • Storks on every tower from February
  • Ibérico ham from acorn-fed pigs in the dehesa outside
