Extremadura, Spain

Mérida

The best Roman ruins in Spain, including a theatre that still stages classical drama every summer.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture✝ Sacred places

Why visit

  • The theatre, from 15 BC, with a summer festival on its stage
  • A 792-metre Roman bridge, still walked across
  • The national museum of Roman art, in a Rafael Moneo building

Roman Theatre

Built in 15 BC, buried for centuries, and staging Greek and Roman drama every summer since 1933.

Theatre · 90 minutes

Things to do

Classical drama in a Roman theatre

Greek and Roman plays staged every July and August in the theatre they were written for, which is a different experience from reading them.

Music & performance · moderate
