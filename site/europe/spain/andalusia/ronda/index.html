Andalusia, Spain

Ronda

A town split by a 120-metre gorge with an 18th-century bridge across it, in the middle of white-village country.

⚑ History & ruins♣ Wild nature◑ Wine & drink⌂ Architecture

Why visit

  • The Puente Nuevo, and the path down into the gorge below it
  • One of Spain's oldest bullrings, whether or not you approve
  • Serranía de Ronda wine, which nobody exports
