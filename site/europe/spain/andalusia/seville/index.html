Andalusia, Spain

Seville

Orange trees, the world's largest gothic cathedral, and flamenco that belongs to specific families.

⌂ Architecture♫ Music & nightlife● Food✝ Sacred places

Why visit

  • The Real Alcázar, still a working royal palace
  • The Giralda, a minaret with a cathedral built round it
  • A flamenco peña rather than a tablao — the difference is everything

Places to see

3 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Real Alcázar

A working royal palace with Mudéjar ceilings and gardens that have been continuously planted since the 1100s.

Castle · 2 hours

Seville Cathedral & the Giralda

The largest Gothic cathedral in the world, with a minaret for a bell tower and a ramp instead of stairs.

Church · 2 hours

Plaza de España

A 1929 half-circle of tiled bridges and alcoves, one for each province, around a canal.

Square · 45 minutes

Things to do

Flamenco in a peña

Members' clubs that admit visitors quietly; no castanets, no stage, and the singing is the part that matters.
