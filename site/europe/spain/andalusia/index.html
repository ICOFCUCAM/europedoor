Places to see

Everything recorded across Andalusia, in one list.

The Alhambra

Nasrid palaces, a fortress and a summer garden on one hill, entered in half-hour slots booked weeks ahead.

Granada · Castle

Albaicín

White lanes on the hill facing the Alhambra, best walked upward and finished at San Nicolás.

Granada · Quarter

Capilla Real

Where Ferdinand and Isabella are buried, next to the cathedral that was built to outshine it.

Granada · Church

Real Alcázar

A working royal palace with Mudéjar ceilings and gardens that have been continuously planted since the 1100s.

Seville · Castle

Seville Cathedral & the Giralda

The largest Gothic cathedral in the world, with a minaret for a bell tower and a ramp instead of stairs.

Seville · Church

Plaza de España

A 1929 half-circle of tiled bridges and alcoves, one for each province, around a canal.

Seville · Square

Mezquita-Catedral

856 columns of a 10th-century mosque with a cathedral inserted into the middle, and both left standing.

Córdoba · Church

Roman Bridge

Sixteen arches across the Guadalquivir, Roman in origin and rebuilt many times, best at dawn.

Córdoba · Bridge

Puente Nuevo

An 18th-century bridge across a 120-metre gorge, with a path down to see it from beneath.

Ronda · Bridge

Things to do

The Nasrid palaces, on a timed ticket

Half-hour entry slots, sold out weeks ahead. Take the earliest of the day — the courtyards were designed for low light and few people.

Granada · Sacred site

Tapas, the Granada way

Every drink comes with a plate you did not order and cannot choose; four bars is a dinner.

Granada · Food & table

Flamenco in a peña

Members' clubs that admit visitors quietly; no castanets, no stage, and the singing is the part that matters.
