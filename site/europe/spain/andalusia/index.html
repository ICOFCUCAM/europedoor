Andalusia

The Moorish south: the Alhambra, three great cities, white villages and the mountains behind them.

6 destinations · 9 places recorded · about 11 nights to see it all

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture● Food✝ Sacred places♫ Music & nightlife

In the region

What Andalusia is made of

All six of them, the same set the drawing above plots.

Destinations

Granada

The last Moorish city in Spain, under the Sierra Nevada, with the Alhambra above it.

City
2–3 nights

Seville

Orange trees, the world’s largest gothic cathedral, and flamenco that belongs to specific families.

City
2–3 nights

Córdoba

The largest city in the world in the 10th century, with a mosque-cathedral of 856 columns at its centre.

City
1–2 nights

Ronda

A town split by a 120-metre gorge with an 18th-century bridge across it, in the middle of white-village country.

Town
1–2 nights

Cádiz

Possibly the oldest continuously inhabited city in western Europe, on a spit, surrounded by water on three sides.

City
1–2 nights

Málaga

A port that spent a decade turning itself into a museum city, with Picasso’s birthplace and a Moorish fortress above.

City
1–2 nights

Places to see

Everything recorded across Andalusia, in one list.

The Alhambra

Nasrid palaces, a fortress and a summer garden on one hill, entered in half-hour slots booked weeks ahead.

Granada · Castle

Albaicín

White lanes on the hill facing the Alhambra, best walked upward and finished at San Nicolás.

Granada · Quarter

Capilla Real

Where Ferdinand and Isabella are buried, next to the cathedral that was built to outshine it.

Granada · Church

Real Alcázar

A working royal palace with Mudéjar ceilings and gardens that have been continuously planted since the 1100s.

Seville · Castle

Seville Cathedral & the Giralda

The largest Gothic cathedral in the world, with a minaret for a bell tower and a ramp instead of stairs.

Seville · Church

Plaza de España

A 1929 half-circle of tiled bridges and alcoves, one for each province, around a canal.

Seville · Square

Mezquita-Catedral

856 columns of a 10th-century mosque with a cathedral inserted into the middle, and both left standing.

Córdoba · Church

Roman Bridge

Sixteen arches across the Guadalquivir, Roman in origin and rebuilt many times, best at dawn.

Córdoba · Bridge

Puente Nuevo

An 18th-century bridge across a 120-metre gorge, with a path down to see it from beneath.

Ronda · Bridge

Things to do

The Nasrid palaces, on a timed ticket

Half-hour entry slots, sold out weeks ahead. Take the earliest of the day — the courtyards were designed for low light and few people.

Granada · Sacred site

Tapas, the Granada way

Every drink comes with a plate you did not order and cannot choose; four bars is a dinner.

Granada · Food & table

Flamenco in a peña

Members' clubs that admit visitors quietly; no castanets, no stage, and the singing is the part that matters.

Seville · Music & performance
