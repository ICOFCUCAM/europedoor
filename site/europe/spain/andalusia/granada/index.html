Andalusia, Spain

Granada

The last Moorish city in Spain, under the Sierra Nevada, with the Alhambra above it.

City · 2–3 nights

⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins● Food♫ Music & nightlife▲ Mountains

Why go

  1. 01The Alhambra's Nasrid palaces, booked months ahead and worth every minute
  2. 02The Albaicín's white lanes and the view from San Nicolás at sunset
  3. 03Free tapas with every drink, which is a real local law of physics
  4. 04Skiing and the beach within ninety minutes of each other in March

Granada is at 37.180°N, 3.600°W.

Places to see

The Alhambra

Nasrid palaces, a fortress and a summer garden on one hill, entered in half-hour slots booked weeks ahead.

Castle · half a day

Albaicín

White lanes on the hill facing the Alhambra, best walked upward and finished at San Nicolás.

Quarter · 2 hours

Capilla Real

Where Ferdinand and Isabella are buried, next to the cathedral that was built to outshine it.

Things to do

The Nasrid palaces, on a timed ticket

Half-hour entry slots, sold out weeks ahead. Take the earliest of the day — the courtyards were designed for low light and few people.

Sacred site · moderate

Tapas, the Granada way

Every drink comes with a plate you did not order and cannot choose; four bars is a dinner.

Travel tips

Practical things about Spain that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch runs 14:00–16:00 and kitchens genuinely close; dinner starts at 21:00.
  • Many museums are free in the last two hours of the day.
  • The Camino de Santiago is a working pilgrimage with its own etiquette, not a long-distance hiking route.
