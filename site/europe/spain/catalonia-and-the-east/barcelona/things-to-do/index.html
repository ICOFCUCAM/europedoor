Things to do in Barcelona

Everything in the Atlas for Barcelona: places to see and things to do, in one list.

Sagrada Família

The interior light is the point; the facades are the argument. Under construction since 1882.

Church · 2 hours

Park Güell

A failed housing development turned public park, with the serpentine bench and the city below.

Park · 2 hours

Mercat de Santa Caterina

A wavy tiled roof over the market Barcelona actually shops in, rather than the one on the Rambla.

Market · 45 minutes

Hospital de Sant Pau

Twelve modernista pavilions built as a hospital because Domènech thought patients recover faster in beauty.

Monument · 90 minutes

The grid, read as a plan

Cerdà's 1859 blocks were designed with gardens inside; a few have been reopened, and finding them changes how the city reads.
