Catalonia & the East, Spain

Barcelona

A Roman city inside a medieval city inside a 19th-century grid, with Gaudí's work scattered across all three.

⌂ Architecture◈ Art & museums● Food≈ Coast & beaches▦ Big cities

Why visit

  • The Sagrada Família's interior light, which is the actual point
  • The Eixample grid and its chamfered corners, a 19th-century plan that worked
  • Santa Caterina and Sant Antoni markets, where the city shops
  • Montjuïc's Fundació Miró and the view back over the port

Travel tips

Practical things about Spain that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch runs 14:00–16:00 and kitchens genuinely close; dinner starts at 21:00.
  • Many museums are free in the last two hours of the day.
  • The Camino de Santiago is a working pilgrimage with its own etiquette, not a long-distance hiking route.
