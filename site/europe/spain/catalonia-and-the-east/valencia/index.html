Valencia

A river turned into a nine-kilometre park, a gothic silk exchange, and the actual home of paella.

● Food⌂ Architecture≈ Coast & beaches◧ Design & making

Why visit

  • Turia gardens: a flood-diverted riverbed, now the city's spine
  • La Lonja, a gothic trading hall with twisted stone columns
  • Paella valenciana, with rabbit and beans and no seafood at all
