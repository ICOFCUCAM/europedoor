Park Güell

A failed housing development turned public park, with the serpentine bench and the city below.

Barcelona · Park

Mercat de Santa Caterina

A wavy tiled roof over the market Barcelona actually shops in, rather than the one on the Rambla.

Barcelona · Market

Hospital de Sant Pau

Twelve modernista pavilions built as a hospital because Domènech thought patients recover faster in beauty.

Barcelona · Monument

Girona Cathedral

The widest Gothic nave in the world, at the top of 91 steps, with a Romanesque tapestry inside.

Girona & the Empordà · Church

El Call

One of Europe’s best-preserved medieval Jewish quarters, with a museum in the middle of it.

Girona & the Empordà · Quarter

La Lonja de la Seda

A Gothic silk exchange with twisted stone columns, built to show that trade was worth a cathedral.

Valencia · Monument

Jardín del Turia

Nine kilometres of park in a flood-diverted riverbed, which is the best civic decision Valencia ever made.

Things to do

The grid, read as a plan

Cerdà’s 1859 blocks were designed with gardens inside; a few have been reopened, and finding them changes how the city reads.

Barcelona · Walk or hike

The Camí de Ronda coastal path

A smugglers' and coastguards' path linking coves between Begur and Palamós; it reaches beaches no road does.

Girona & the Empordà · Walk or hike

Paella where the rice grows

The Albufera lagoon south of the city grows the rice and cooks it over orange wood; lunchtime only, and never in the evening.
