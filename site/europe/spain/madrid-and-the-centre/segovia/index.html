Madrid & the Centre, Spain

Segovia

A Roman aqueduct of 167 arches, built without mortar, running straight through the middle of a working town.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture● Food

What earns the time

  • The aqueduct, 28 metres high, dry-stone, still standing after 2,000 years
  • The Alcázar on its rock, which Disney is said to have borrowed
  • Cochinillo, roast suckling pig, carved with a plate
