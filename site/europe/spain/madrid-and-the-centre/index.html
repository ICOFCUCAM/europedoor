Places to see

Everything recorded across Madrid &amp; the Centre, in one list.

Museo del Prado

Velázquez, Goya and Bosch in a building designed as a natural history cabinet.

Madrid · Museum

Reina Sofía

Guernica, in a former hospital, with the room it needs around it.

Madrid · Museum

Parque del Retiro

125 hectares of former royal garden with a glass palace in the middle and rowing boats on the lake.

Madrid · Park

El Rastro

Sunday morning flea market down a hillside, unchanged in character for four centuries.

Madrid · Market

Toledo Cathedral

A Gothic pile with a Baroque hole cut in the roof to light the altar behind the altar.

Toledo · Church

Santo Tomé

El Greco's Burial of the Count of Orgaz, still in the church it was painted for in 1586.

Toledo · Church

Sinagoga del Tránsito

A 14th-century synagogue built by Muslim craftsmen for a Jewish congregation under a Christian king.

Toledo · Monument

Roman Aqueduct

167 arches, 28 metres high, built without mortar and standing in the middle of a working town.

Segovia · Monument

Alcázar of Segovia

A castle on a rock at the confluence of two rivers, rebuilt after an 1862 fire into something even more improbable.

Segovia · Castle

Plaza Mayor

The best square in Spain, used as a living room, and gold at sunset because of the stone.

University facade

Casas Colgadas
