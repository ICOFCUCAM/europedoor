Madrid & the Centre, Spain

Madrid

A high plateau capital with three of the world's great galleries in one kilometre and a nightlife that starts at midnight.

◈ Art & museums● Food▦ Big cities♫ Music & nightlife

Why visit

  • The Prado's Velázquez and Goya, and the Reina Sofía's Guernica
  • El Rastro flea market on Sunday morning
  • Retiro park, and the crystal palace in it
  • Vermouth at noon, which is a Madrid institution

Museo del Prado

Velázquez, Goya and Bosch in a building designed as a natural history cabinet.

Museum · 3 hours

Reina Sofía

Guernica, in a former hospital, with the room it needs around it.

Museum · 2 hours

Parque del Retiro

125 hectares of former royal garden with a glass palace in the middle and rowing boats on the lake.

Park · 2 hours

El Rastro

Sunday morning flea market down a hillside, unchanged in character for four centuries.

Market · 2 hours

Things to do

La hora del vermut

Sunday, around one, house vermouth on tap with an olive and a siphon of soda — the most Madrid ninety minutes there is.
