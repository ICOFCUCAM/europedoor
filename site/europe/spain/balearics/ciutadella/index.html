The Balearic Islands, Spain

Ciutadella, Menorca

Menorca's old capital: a sandstone harbour town on an island that is a UNESCO biosphere reserve and mostly farmland.

Town · 2–3 nights

○ Islands≈ Coast & beaches⚑ History & ruins● Food

Why go

  1. 01Camí de Cavalls: 185 km of bridle path round the whole coast
  2. 02Talayotic stone monuments, on the UNESCO list since 2023
  3. 03Beaches on the north coast reached only on foot

Travel tips

Practical things about Spain that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch runs 14:00–16:00 and kitchens genuinely close; dinner starts at 21:00.
  • Many museums are free in the last two hours of the day.
  • The Camino de Santiago is a working pilgrimage with its own etiquette, not a long-distance hiking route.
