Palma de Mallorca

A cathedral with a Gaudí interior on the harbour, and the Tramuntana mountains behind it for the other three seasons.

Why visit

  • La Seu, whose canopy Gaudí redesigned and whose chapel Barceló repainted in 2007
  • The Serra de Tramuntana, UNESCO-listed, and best from October to May
  • The wooden train to Sóller, running since 1912

Travel tips

Practical things about Spain that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch runs 14:00–16:00 and kitchens genuinely close; dinner starts at 21:00.
  • Many museums are free in the last two hours of the day.
  • The Camino de Santiago is a working pilgrimage with its own etiquette, not a long-distance hiking route.
