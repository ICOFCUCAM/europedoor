Travel tips

Practical things about Albania that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Cash is still king outside Tirana; the lek is not widely obtainable abroad.
  • Hospitality (besa) is a genuine cultural obligation and refusing food can offend.
  • Mountain paths in the north are marked but rescue is limited — go with a guide or a good map.
