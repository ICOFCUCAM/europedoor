Travel tips

Practical things about Liechtenstein that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Passport stamps are available as a souvenir at the tourist office; the border itself is unmanned.
  • Swiss francs everywhere; euros usually accepted at a poor rate.
  • The Liechtenstein Trail runs 75 km through all eleven municipalities.
