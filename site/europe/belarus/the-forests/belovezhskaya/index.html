The Forests, Belarus

Belovezhskaya Pushcha

The Belarusian half of Europe's last primeval lowland forest, with bison and trees that have never been cut.

❦ Wildlife watching♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • European bison, reintroduced from captive stock after the species died out here
  • Oaks over 500 years old
  • A UNESCO site shared across the Polish border
