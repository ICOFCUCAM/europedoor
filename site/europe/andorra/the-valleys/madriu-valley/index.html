Travel tips

Practical things about Andorra that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Not in the EU or Schengen, but there are no routine border controls with France or Spain; customs limits are enforced.
  • Fuel, alcohol and electronics are cheaper here, which is why the main road is a queue on Saturdays.
  • The Madriu-Perafita-Claror valley has no road at all and is entered on foot.
