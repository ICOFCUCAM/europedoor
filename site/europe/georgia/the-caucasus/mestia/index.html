The Greater Caucasus, Georgia

Mestia, Svaneti

A mountain region of defensive stone towers built by families against each other, under 5,000-metre peaks.

▲ Mountains⚑ History & ruins❦ Wildlife watching

Why visit

  • Koshki towers, 9th to 13th century, one per family
  • Ushguli, among the highest permanently inhabited villages in Europe
  • The four-day Mestia to Ushguli trek

Mestia to Ushguli on foot
