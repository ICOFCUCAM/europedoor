Helsinki & the Southern Coast, Finland

Helsinki

Neoclassical centre, brutal-modern edges, and a design district you can walk in an afternoon.

Why visit

  • Suomenlinna: a UNESCO sea fortress on six islands, twenty minutes by ferry
  • Temppeliaukio, a church blasted into bedrock
  • Löyly and the return of the public sauna to the waterfront
  • The Oodi library, which lends power tools
