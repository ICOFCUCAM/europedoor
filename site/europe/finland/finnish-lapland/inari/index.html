Finnish Lapland, Finland

Inari

The centre of Sámi Finland, on a lake with three thousand islands.

❄ Snow & winter♣ Wild nature⚑ History & ruins

Why visit

  • Siida: the Sámi museum, written by Sámi people
  • Ukonsaari, a sacred island out on the lake
  • Aurora with almost no light pollution in any direction
