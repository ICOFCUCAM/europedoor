The Lakeland, Finland

Savonlinna

A 15th-century castle on an island in the middle of Saimaa, which hosts opera in its courtyard each July.

⚑ History & ruins♣ Wild nature♫ Music & nightlife

Why visit

  • Olavinlinna castle, built to face east and never taken by storm
  • Opera in the courtyard, one month a year
  • Saimaa ringed seals — a freshwater seal found nowhere else
