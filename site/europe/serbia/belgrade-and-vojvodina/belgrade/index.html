Belgrade & Vojvodina, Serbia

Belgrade

A fortress above the confluence of the Sava and the Danube, with floating clubs below it and brutalist New Belgrade opposite.

Why visit

  • Kalemegdan fortress at sunset over the two rivers
  • Splavovi — river barges that are bars and clubs, all summer
  • Skadarlija's bohemian street and the kafanas along it
  • New Belgrade's socialist modernism, taken seriously by architects

Travel tips

Practical things about Serbia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Not in the EU; the dinar is the currency and cash is more useful outside cities.
  • Positions on Kosovo are politically charged here — visitors are best served by listening rather than opining.
  • Smoking indoors is more common than elsewhere in Europe.
