Western & Southern Serbia, Serbia

Mokra Gora

A mountain village on the Šargan Eight, a narrow-gauge line that climbs a figure of eight to gain height.

═ Slow travel by rail♣ Wild nature▲ Mountains

Why visit

  • The Šargan Eight, 22 tunnels in 15 kilometres
  • Drvengrad, a wooden village built as a film set and kept
  • The Tara national park and the Drina canyon

Things to do

The Šargan Eight

A 760 mm gauge line that loops over itself to climb 300 metres; two and a half hours and a great deal of leaning out of windows.
