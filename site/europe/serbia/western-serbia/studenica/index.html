Travel tips

Practical things about Serbia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Not in the EU; the dinar is the currency and cash is more useful outside cities.
  • Positions on Kosovo are politically charged here — visitors are best served by listening rather than opining.
  • Smoking indoors is more common than elsewhere in Europe.
