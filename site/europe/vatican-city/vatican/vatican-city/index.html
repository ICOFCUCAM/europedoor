Vatican City

A state the size of a large park, holding Michelangelo's ceiling, his dome and his Pietà.

Travel tips

Practical things about Vatican City that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Covered shoulders and knees are required and enforced at the door, in any weather.
  • The basilica is free; the museums are ticketed and should be booked online.
  • Photography is banned inside the Sistine Chapel, which is stated repeatedly and ignored constantly.
