Vatican City, Vatican City

Vatican City

A state the size of a large park, holding Michelangelo's ceiling, his dome and his Pietà.

✝ Sacred places◈ Art & museums⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins

Why visit

  • The Sistine Chapel, best at the first entry slot of the day
  • St Peter's dome, 551 steps, with the last stretch leaning inward
  • Raphael's School of Athens, on the way to the chapel and often rushed past
  • The necropolis under the basilica, booked separately and months ahead

Up the dome of St Peter's

The staircase follows the curve of the dome and tilts with it; the reward is the whole of Rome and the gardens nobody else sees.

Sacred site · low

The scavi, under the basilica

A Roman street of tombs beneath the church, ending at what may be St Peter's grave. Twelve people at a time, booked by email, months ahead.

Travel tips

Practical things about Vatican City that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Covered shoulders and knees are required and enforced at the door, in any weather.
  • The basilica is free; the museums are ticketed and should be booked online.
  • Photography is banned inside the Sistine Chapel, which is stated repeatedly and ignored constantly.
