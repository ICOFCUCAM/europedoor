Monte Titano, San Marino

City of San Marino

A walled town at 750 metres with three towers along the crest and the Adriatic visible on a clear day.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture▲ Mountains

Why visit

  • The Passo delle Streghe, the path linking the first two towers along the cliff
  • Guaita, the oldest tower, from the 11th century
  • The view to Rimini and the sea, 20 kilometres away and 750 metres down

Travel tips

Practical things about San Marino that are not obvious from outside it.

  • No border control with Italy; the euro is used but San Marino mints its own collectable coins.
  • Passport stamps are available for a fee at the tourist office.
  • The historic centre and Monte Titano are UNESCO-listed.
