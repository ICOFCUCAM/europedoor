Travel tips

Practical things about Kosovo that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Entering from Serbia and leaving to a third country is straightforward; the reverse can create problems. Check current rules before routing.
  • Uses the euro unilaterally.
  • Orthodox monasteries are protected sites, sometimes with a police presence; visits are welcome and photography rules are strict.
