Tallinn & the North Coast, Estonia

Tallinn

A walled hanseatic town where the merchants' houses, the guild halls and the city wall all survived, and a startup district grew up beside them.

Places to see

Toompea and the old town

One of the most complete medieval town cores in northern Europe, still walled, with the upper town of the nobility and the lower town of the merchants kept deliberately separate.

Quarter · 3 hours

Kumu Art Museum

Estonian art from the eighteenth century to now, including the Soviet decades presented without either nostalgia or dismissal — which is rarer than it should be.

Museum · 2–3 hours

2 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Things to do

Walk the wall, tower to tower

Two kilometres of it still stand; the sections you can climb give the roofline the postcards use.

Walk or hike · low

Bog shoes in Lahemaa

Snowshoe-like frames let you cross raised bog off the boardwalk — an hour from the capital and utterly silent.

Walk or hike · moderate

Travel tips

Practical things about Estonia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Almost every public service is digital, including e-residency for non-citizens.
  • Bogs are a national landscape: boardwalks make them accessible without wrecking them.
  • The Russian border area requires care and current advice.
