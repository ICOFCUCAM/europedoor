The Islands & the South, Estonia

Kuressaare, Saaremaa

An island town around a moated bishop's castle, on Estonia's largest and slowest island.

○ Islands⚑ History & ruins♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • The 14th-century castle, complete with its moat
  • Kaali meteorite crater, formed within human memory
  • Juniper, windmills, and roads with no traffic on them
