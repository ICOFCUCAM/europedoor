Balaton & the Wine Country, Hungary

Tihany, Lake Balaton

A lavender peninsula with an abbey on a cliff, sticking into a shallow warm lake.

≈ Coast & beaches◑ Wine & drink✝ Sacred places♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • The abbey and its 1055 founding charter — the oldest written Hungarian
  • Lavender fields in late June
  • Water warm enough to stand in for hours, and rarely deeper than four metres

Travel tips

Practical things about Hungary that are not obvious from outside it.

  • The forint, not the euro; exchange rates at street bureaux are usually terrible.
  • In the baths, bring flip-flops and a towel or hire both; some pools require a cap.
  • Ruin bars occupy derelict courtyards in the old Jewish quarter, which is also a memorial district — the two coexist awkwardly.
