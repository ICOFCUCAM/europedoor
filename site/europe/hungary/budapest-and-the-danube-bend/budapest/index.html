Budapest & the Danube Bend, Hungary

Budapest

Hilly Buda and flat Pest, joined by bridges, with a parliament on the water and a hundred hot springs underneath.

⌂ Architecture● Food♫ Music & nightlife▦ Big cities⚑ History & ruins

Why visit

  • Széchenyi and Gellért baths — one grand and outdoor, one Art Nouveau and indoor
  • The parliament, seen from the Buda side at night
  • The Great Market Hall, and lángos on the upper floor
  • The House of Terror, in the building both secret polices used

Things to do

An outdoor thermal bath in winter

38°C water, snow on the stone, and old men playing chess on floating boards. Go early; go on a weekday.

On the water · moderate

A ruin bar, before it fills

Derelict courtyard buildings filled with salvaged furniture; Szimpla was the first and is still worth an early evening.
