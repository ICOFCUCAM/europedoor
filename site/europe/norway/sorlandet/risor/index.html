Sørlandet & the South Coast, Norway

Risør

'The white town by the Skagerrak' — 300 wooden houses round a harbour, and a wooden boat festival each August.

≈ Coast & beaches◧ Design & making○ Islands

Why visit

  • The wooden boat festival, the largest in the Nordics
  • Stangholmen lighthouse island, twenty minutes by boat
  • Craft workshops in the old warehouses

Things to do

The wooden boat festival

Every August the harbour fills with clinker-built boats and the people who maintain them; go for the sound of the hulls rather than the programme.
