Fjord Norway, Norway

Flåm

A hamlet at the end of a fjord arm, famous for a railway that drops 865 metres in twenty kilometres.

═ Slow travel by rail▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • The Flåm Railway, best taken uphill and walked or cycled back down
  • Nærøyfjord: a UNESCO-listed fjord arm 250 metres wide at its narrowest
  • Aurlandsdalen, a day-long valley walk between old summer farms
