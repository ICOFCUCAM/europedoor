Fjord Norway, Norway

Odda & Trolltunga

A smelter town at the end of a fjord arm, and a slab of rock that sticks 700 metres out over a lake.

▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature⚑ History & ruins

Why visit

  • Trolltunga: 28 km round, ten hours, and not a stroll
  • Folgefonna glacier above the valley
  • The smelter town itself, which is on the UNESCO tentative list and is not pretty

Trolltunga, with the day it needs
