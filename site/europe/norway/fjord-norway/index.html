Fjord Norway

The west coast: Bergen and its rain, the deep fjord arms, and the roads that only open when the snow lets them.

8 destinations · 13 places recorded · about 15 nights to see it all

▲ Mountains≈ Coast & beaches♣ Wild nature═ Slow travel by rail

What Fjord Norway is made of

All eight of them, the same set the drawing above plots.

Destinations

Bergen

A hanseatic trading port wedged between seven mountains, wet 200 days a year and entirely unbothered about it.

City
2–3 nights

Flåm

A hamlet at the end of a fjord arm, famous for a railway that drops 865 metres in twenty kilometres.

Village
1–2 nights

Ålesund

Burned to the ground in 1904 and rebuilt, all at once, in Art Nouveau — so the whole town matches.

Town
1–2 nights

Stavanger

Oil capital with a preserved wooden old town, and the most photographed cliff in Norway an hour away.

City
2–3 nights

Odda & Trolltunga

A smelter town at the end of a fjord arm, and a slab of rock that sticks 700 metres out over a lake.

Town
2–3 nights

Voss

A lake town between two fjords that decided to become Norway’s outdoor sports capital and succeeded.

City
1–2 nights

Geiranger

A UNESCO fjord arm 15 km long with waterfalls falling straight into it and abandoned farms on ledges above.

Valley
1–2 nights

Balestrand

A Sognefjord village of dragon-style timber villas, where 19th-century painters came and then stayed.

Village

Places to see

Everything recorded across Fjord Norway, in one list.

Bryggen

The leaning wooden trading houses of the Hanseatic wharf, rebuilt after each fire on the same foundations since the 1100s.

Bergen · Quarter

Fløyen

320 metres above the city by funicular, with a path down through the woods that most people forget to take.

Bergen · Viewpoint

Fish Market

Outdoor from May, indoors year-round, and better one street back where the boats actually deliver.

Bergen · Market

KODE

Four buildings on one street holding Munch, Astrup and the largest Norwegian art collection outside Oslo.

Bergen · Museum

Flåm Railway

Twenty kilometres and 865 metres of descent through twenty tunnels, most of them dug by hand.

Flåm · Monument

Nærøyfjord

A UNESCO fjord arm 250 metres wide at its narrowest, with waterfalls straight off the wall above the boat.

Flåm · Waterfall

Aksla

418 steps up from the town park, with the Art Nouveau roofs and the archipelago laid out below.

Ålesund · Viewpoint

Jugendstilsenteret

The Art Nouveau centre in the old pharmacy, explaining why an entire town matches.

Ålesund · Museum

Preikestolen

A flat slab 604 metres above the Lysefjord with no railing, reached by an eight-kilometre path.

Stavanger · Mountain

Gamle Stavanger

173 white wooden houses from the 18th and 19th centuries, on cobbled lanes above the harbour.

Stavanger · Quarter

Norwegian Petroleum Museum

The industry that paid for modern Norway, explained more frankly than a national museum usually manages.

Stavanger · Museum

The Seven Sisters

Seven falls dropping 250 metres into the fjord, opposite a suitor who never got anywhere.

Geiranger · Waterfall

Skageflå

A farm worked until 1918 on a ledge 250 metres above the water, reached by boat and then by climbing.

Geiranger · Ruin

Things to do

Fløyen to Ulriken ridge walk

Five hours along the tops between the two cable cars, with the city on one side and nothing but bog and rock on the other.

Bergen · Walk or hike

Lunch at a working fish counter

Skip the tourist stalls; eat where the boats deliver, standing up, whatever came in that morning.

Bergen · Food & table

Ride the Rallarvegen down

The old navvies' road from Myrdal, gravel all the way, downhill nearly all the way — a bike, a train ticket and a whole afternoon.

Flåm · Rail, road or ride

The town that burned and matched

A short walk that only works once you know the date: every façade you pass was drawn in the same three years.

Ålesund · Walk or hike

Preikestolen, early

Eight kilometres round on rock and boardwalk. Start before seven; by eleven there is a queue for the photograph and no railing to queue behind.

Stavanger · Walk or hike

Trolltunga, with the day it needs

Ten to twelve hours, a 1,200-metre climb, and mountain rescue callouts every season from people who read it as a viewpoint with a car park.

Odda & Trolltunga · Walk or hike

The farms on the ledges

Skageflå was farmed until 1918 on a shelf 250 metres above the water, reached by ladder. The boat drops you at the bottom and you climb.

Geiranger · Walk or hike
