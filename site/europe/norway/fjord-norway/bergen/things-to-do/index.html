Bergen, Norway

Things to do in Bergen

Everything in the Atlas for Bergen: places to see and things to do, in one list.

Bryggen

The leaning wooden trading houses of the Hanseatic wharf, rebuilt after each fire on the same foundations since the 1100s.

Quarter · 1 hour

Fløyen

320 metres above the city by funicular, with a path down through the woods that most people forget to take.

Viewpoint · 2 hours

Fish Market

Outdoor from May, indoors year-round, and better one street back where the boats actually deliver.

Market · 45 minutes

KODE

Four buildings on one street holding Munch, Astrup and the largest Norwegian art collection outside Oslo.

Museum · 2 hours

Fløyen to Ulriken ridge walk

Five hours along the tops between the two cable cars, with the city on one side and nothing but bog and rock on the other.

Walk or hike · low

Lunch at a working fish counter

Skip the tourist stalls; eat where the boats deliver, standing up, whatever came in that morning.

Food & table · moderate
