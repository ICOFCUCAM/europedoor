Fjord Norway, Norway

Geiranger

A UNESCO fjord arm 15 km long with waterfalls falling straight into it and abandoned farms on ledges above.

♣ Wild nature▲ Mountains≈ Coast & beaches

Why visit

  • The Seven Sisters falls, and the farm ruins on shelves opposite
  • Dalsnibba, 1,500 metres, with the fjord directly below
  • The Trollstigen road north, eleven hairpins with a wall on one side
