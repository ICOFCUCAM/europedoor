Norway

Østlandet & the East

The populated, farmed, forested east: the capital, the big lakes, and the valleys the ski world takes seriously.

4 destinations · 8 places recorded · about 7 nights to see it all

Capital city

Oslo

A capital that spent its oil money on architecture and then put a forest and a fjord within a tram ride of the result.

2–3 nights

City

Lillehammer

Olympic town on a long lake, with an open-air museum that is really a village of 200 relocated buildings.

1–2 nights

Town

Fredrikstad

Scandinavia's best-preserved fortress town, on an island in a river mouth, reached by a free ferry.

1–2 nights

Town

Rjukan

A company town in a gorge so deep the sun does not reach it for six months, so the town installed mirrors on the mountain.

1–2 nights

Places to see

Everything recorded across Østlandet &amp; the East, in one list.

MUNCH

Thirteen floors on the waterfront holding the artist's own bequest — 26,000 works, including the versions of The Scream worth arguing about.

Oslo · Museum

Fram Museum

The polar ship Fram kept whole indoors, with her hull open so you can walk the decks that drifted across the Arctic for three years.

Oslo · Museum

Vigeland Sculpture Park

212 bronze and granite figures by one sculptor in one park, free and open at every hour, including four in the morning.

Oslo · Park

Akershus Fortress

A medieval castle on the harbour that was never taken by siege, with the government quarter and the fjord on either side of it.

Oslo · Castle

Oslo Opera House

A roof that comes down to the water so the public can walk up it, which is the building's actual argument.

Oslo · Monument

Maihaugen

Two hundred buildings moved log by log from across Gudbrandsdalen, including a stave church.

Lillehammer · Museum

Vemork

The heavy water plant sabotaged in 1943, with the pipeline the saboteurs climbed still on the cliff.

Rjukan · Museum

The Sun Mirrors

Three heliostats on the mountain throwing daylight into a square the sun cannot reach for six months.

Rjukan · Monument

Things to do

The commuter ferry as a day out

Ride the public boats to Hovedøya and Gressholmen with a swim towel and a bakery bag; it is a city transport ticket, not a cruise.

Oslo · On the water

Waterfront architecture walk

Opera roof to Munch to the library at Bjørvika — twenty years of a city deciding what it wanted to look like, in ninety minutes.

Oslo · Walk or hike

Maihaugen open-air museum

Two hundred buildings moved log by log from across Gudbrandsdalen; the point is the carpentry, not the costumes.

Lillehammer · Museum or collection

The free ferry, all day

Fredrikstad runs its river crossings free of charge as ordinary transport; ride them between the old town and the new for as long as you like.

Fredrikstad · On the water

Vemork and the heavy water raid

The plant, the pipeline the saboteurs climbed, and an account that does not tidy up how close it came to failing.
