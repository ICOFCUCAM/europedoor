Northern Norway, Norway

Alta

Finnmark's largest town, with 6,000-year-old rock carvings and a cathedral shaped like the aurora.

⚑ History & ruins❄ Snow & winter♣ Wild nature◧ Design & making

Why visit

  • Hjemmeluft's rock art, on the UNESCO list, walked on boardwalks
  • The Northern Lights Cathedral, a titanium spiral
  • The Finnmarksløpet, Europe's longest sled dog race, in March

Six thousand years of rock carving

Thousands of figures — boats, reindeer, bears, people — pecked into coastal rock over five millennia, and read in order along a boardwalk.
