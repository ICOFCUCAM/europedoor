Northern Norway, Norway

Tromsø

A university town at 69°N with more bars per head than anywhere else in Norway, and the aurora directly overhead.

❄ Snow & winter♣ Wild nature♫ Music & nightlife▦ Big cities

Why visit

  • The Arctic Cathedral and the cable car above the strait
  • Aurora hunting between late September and late March, inland where the sky is clearer
  • Whale watching off Skjervøy in November and December

Places to see

3 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Arctic Cathedral

Eleven concrete triangles and a glass mosaic 23 metres high, finished in 1965 and visible from the whole city.

Church · 30 minutes

Polaria

A small aquarium and Arctic research centre with bearded seals, built as five ice floes tipped against a shore.

Museum · 1 hour

Storsteinen

421 metres by cable car, with the island city below and the midnight sun above it from late May.

Viewpoint · 90 minutes

Things to do

An aurora night with a forecast, not a promise

A guide who drives to clear sky — sometimes into Finland — is worth more than one who stays put; nobody can guarantee the lights, and anyone who does is selling something.

Wildlife & nature · high

A Sámi reindeer herding afternoon

Book with a Sámi-owned operator; the difference between a working herd and a photo backdrop is obvious within ten minutes.
