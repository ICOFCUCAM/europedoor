Northern Norway, Norway

Bodø

A rebuilt northern port with the strongest tidal current in the world at its door and Lofoten across the water.

≈ Coast & beaches♣ Wild nature◧ Design & making♫ Music & nightlife

Why visit

  • Saltstraumen: 400 million cubic metres of water through a 150-metre strait, four times a day
  • The coastal ferry and the Lofoten crossing
  • Sea eagles, which are ordinary here
