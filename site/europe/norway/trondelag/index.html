Norway

Trøndelag

The old seat of Norwegian kings, plus a copper town preserved by being too remote to modernise.

2 destinations · 6 places recorded · about 4 nights to see it all

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places● Food

Destinations

City

Trondheim

A student city built around the northernmost medieval cathedral in the world, and the end of a thousand-year pilgrim path.

2–3 nights

Town

Røros

A 17th-century copper-mining town of black timber houses, at -30°C for weeks at a stretch and listed by UNESCO for surviving intact.

1–2 nights

Places to see

Everything recorded across Trøndelag, in one list.

Nidaros Cathedral

The northernmost medieval cathedral in the world, the end of the St Olav Ways, and a stoneyard that has cut replacement blocks continuously for a century.

Trondheim · Church

Gamle Bybro

The old town bridge, red-painted, with the wooden wharves of the Nidelva on both sides of it.

Trondheim · Bridge

Ringve Music Museum

A national collection of musical instruments in a manor house above the fjord, played rather than displayed.

Trondheim · Museum

Røros Church

A 1784 stone church seating 1,600 in a town of 3,700, built by a copper company for its workers.

Røros · Church

Olavsgruva Mine

Fifty metres underground at five degrees year-round, in a copper mine worked from 1650.

Røros · Cave

Slaggveien

Turf-roofed miners' cottages built on the slag heaps, which is the whole social history in one lane.

Røros · Street

Things to do

The cathedral, from the workshop side

The stoneyard has been cutting replacement blocks continuously for a century; see that before you see the nave.

Trondheim · Sacred site

Down the Olav mine

Five degrees underground year-round, hard hats, and the noise a hand drill made before compressed air.

Røros · Museum or collection
