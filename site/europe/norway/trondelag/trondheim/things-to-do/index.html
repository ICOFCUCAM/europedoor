Trondheim, Norway

Things to do in Trondheim

Everything in the Atlas for Trondheim: places to see and things to do, in one list.

Nidaros Cathedral

The northernmost medieval cathedral in the world, the end of the St Olav Ways, and a stoneyard that has cut replacement blocks continuously for a century.

Church · 90 minutes

Gamle Bybro

The old town bridge, red-painted, with the wooden wharves of the Nidelva on both sides of it.

Bridge · 15 minutes

Ringve Music Museum

A national collection of musical instruments in a manor house above the fjord, played rather than displayed.

Museum · 2 hours

The cathedral, from the workshop side

The stoneyard has been cutting replacement blocks continuously for a century; see that before you see the nave.

Sacred site · low
