Røros, Norway

Things to do in Røros

Everything in the Atlas for Røros: places to see and things to do, in one list.

Røros Church

A 1784 stone church seating 1,600 in a town of 3,700, built by a copper company for its workers.

Church · 45 minutes

Olavsgruva Mine

Fifty metres underground at five degrees year-round, in a copper mine worked from 1650.

Cave · 90 minutes

Slaggveien

Turf-roofed miners' cottages built on the slag heaps, which is the whole social history in one lane.

Street · 30 minutes

Down the Olav mine

Five degrees underground year-round, hard hats, and the noise a hand drill made before compressed air.

Museum or collection · moderate
