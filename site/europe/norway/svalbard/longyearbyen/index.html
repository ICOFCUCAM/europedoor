Svalbard, Norway

Longyearbyen

The world's northernmost town of any size: a coal settlement turned research station, where leaving the settlement requires a rifle.

Town · 3–4 nights

Places to see

Svalbard Museum

Whaling, mining, trapping and the ecology of an archipelago where nothing decomposes, in one well-made room.

Museum · 90 minutes

Global Seed Vault

A concrete wedge in a mountainside holding a million seed samples. Nobody goes inside; the point is that it is there.

Monument · 20 minutes, from outside

Things to do

Out of town, legally

Any trip beyond the settlement needs a guide carrying a rifle and trained to avoid using it. That is the law and it is also the honest reading of the risk.

Travel tips

Practical things about Norway that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Card and phone payment work everywhere, including on ferries and in mountain huts. Cash is close to useless.
  • Allemannsretten — the right to roam — lets you walk and camp on uncultivated land, with obligations attached: no fires in summer, no trace, 150 metres from any house.
  • Alcohol above 4.7% is sold only in state Vinmonopolet shops, which close early and shut on Sundays.
