Longyearbyen, Norway

Things to do in Longyearbyen

Everything in the Atlas for Longyearbyen: places to see and things to do, in one list.

Svalbard Museum

Whaling, mining, trapping and the ecology of an archipelago where nothing decomposes, in one well-made room.

Museum · 90 minutes

Global Seed Vault

A concrete wedge in a mountainside holding a million seed samples. Nobody goes inside; the point is that it is there.

Monument · 20 minutes, from outside

Out of town, legally

Any trip beyond the settlement needs a guide carrying a rifle and trained to avoid using it. That is the law and it is also the honest reading of the risk.

Wildlife & nature · high

Dog sledding on the glacier

Wheeled carts in summer, runners in winter, and the same dogs that supplied the mines before snowmobiles.

Rail, road or ride · high
