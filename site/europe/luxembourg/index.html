The portrait

Why Luxembourg belongs in your Europe.

Luxembourg is 2,586 square kilometres, trilingual by default, and richer per head than almost anywhere. The capital sits on a rock split by two river valleys, with casemates tunnelled through it. In 2020 it made all public transport free nationwide, which turns out to be the single best reason to travel around it slowly.

Where Luxembourg is worth going

4 destinations across 2 travel regions.

Getting around

All public transport — train, tram, bus — is free for everyone, resident or not. The country is crossable in ninety minutes.

When to come

The Moselle harvest in September and October is the highlight of the year. Summer is green and walkable in the Mullerthal. The capital empties at weekends, which makes it pleasant.

Worth knowing

  • Free public transport applies to second class on every line in the country.
  • Luxembourgish is the national language; French runs administration and German runs the press.
  • Cross-border commuters nearly double the capital’s population on weekdays.

At the table

  • Judd mat Gaardebounen: smoked pork collar with broad beans
  • Moselle Riesling and Crémant, both underrated because nobody exports them
  • Gromperekichelcher, potato fritters, from a market stall
