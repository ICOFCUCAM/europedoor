Umbria & the Marches, Italy

Urbino

A hilltop ducal palace that was the model Renaissance court, in a town that has been a university since 1506.

◈ Art & museums⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins

Why visit

  • Piero della Francesca's Flagellation, a small painting people travel for
  • The duke's studiolo, panelled in trompe-l'œil marquetry
  • Raphael's birthplace, on a steep street

Travel tips

Practical things about Italy that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Coperto is a per-person cover charge and is legal and normal; a tip on top is not expected.
  • ZTL zones in historic centres are camera-enforced — never drive in without a hotel permit.
  • Churches enforce covered shoulders and knees, including in high summer.
