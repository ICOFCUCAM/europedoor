Umbria & the Marches, Italy

Assisi

A pink stone town on Monte Subasio, built around the basilica of a man who rejected everything it is made of.

✝ Sacred places◈ Art & museums⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins

What earns the time

  • Giotto's fresco cycle in the upper basilica, restored after the 1997 earthquake
  • The Eremo delle Carceri, the hermitage in the woods above town
  • Evening, when the day pilgrims leave and the town is a town
