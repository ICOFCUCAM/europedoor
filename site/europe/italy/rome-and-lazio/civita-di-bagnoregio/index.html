Rome & Lazio, Italy

Civita di Bagnoregio

A village on an eroding tufa pillar, reached by a footbridge, with about a dozen permanent residents left.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture♣ Wild nature

What earns the time

  • The bridge, which is the only way in and takes ten minutes
  • Etruscan tunnels cut under the rock
  • Watching the cliff edge, which loses a little every winter — the town is called 'the dying city' locally
