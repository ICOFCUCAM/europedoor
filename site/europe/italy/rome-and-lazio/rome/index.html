Why visit

  • The Pantheon, entered free, still roofed by the largest unreinforced concrete dome ever built
  • The Forum and Palatine early, before the heat and the groups
  • Testaccio market and the quinto quarto cooking around it
  • Borghese Gallery, two hours, booked, and worth restructuring a day for

Places to see

5 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Pantheon

The largest unreinforced concrete dome ever built, finished around 126 AD and still holding up a working church.

Monument · 45 minutes

Roman Forum & Palatine

The civic centre of an empire, walked at ground level, with the hill the emperors lived on above it.

Archaeological site · 3 hours

Galleria Borghese

Bernini's marble that behaves like flesh, in a villa that admits 360 people at a time by booking.

Museum · 2 hours

Via Appia Antica

Basalt paving from 312 BC, closed to traffic on Sundays, with catacombs and aqueducts either side.

Street · half a day

Mercato Testaccio

Where Roman offal cooking survives, in a purpose-built modern hall the traders actually wanted.

Market · 1 hour

Travel tips

Practical things about Italy that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Coperto is a per-person cover charge and is legal and normal; a tip on top is not expected.
  • ZTL zones in historic centres are camera-enforced — never drive in without a hotel permit.
  • Churches enforce covered shoulders and knees, including in high summer.
