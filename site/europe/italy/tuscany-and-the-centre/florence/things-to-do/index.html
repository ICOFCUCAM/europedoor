Florence, Italy

Things to do in Florence

Everything in the Atlas for Florence: places to see and things to do, in one list.

Brunelleschi's Dome

Four million bricks laid without a centring frame, climbed between its two shells by 463 steps.

Church · 90 minutes

Uffizi

The Medici collection in the offices Vasari built for them, and the reason people book Florence months ahead.

Museum · 3 hours

Bargello

Donatello, Michelangelo and the sculpture the Uffizi queue never sees, in a 13th-century prison.

Museum · 90 minutes

San Miniato al Monte

An 11th-century facade above the city, with Gregorian chant at vespers and almost no crowd.

Church · 1 hour

Vespers at San Miniato

Gregorian chant in an 11th-century church above the city, at 17:30, free, and almost always uncrowded.

Sacred site · low
