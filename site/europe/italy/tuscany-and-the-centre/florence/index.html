Places to see

Brunelleschi's Dome

Four million bricks laid without a centring frame, climbed between its two shells by 463 steps.

Church · 90 minutes

Uffizi

The Medici collection in the offices Vasari built for them, and the reason people book Florence months ahead.

Museum · 3 hours

Bargello

Donatello, Michelangelo and the sculpture the Uffizi queue never sees, in a 13th-century prison.

Museum · 90 minutes

San Miniato al Monte

An 11th-century facade above the city, with Gregorian chant at vespers and almost no crowd.

Church · 1 hour

Travel tips

Practical things about Italy that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Coperto is a per-person cover charge and is legal and normal; a tip on top is not expected.
  • ZTL zones in historic centres are camera-enforced — never drive in without a hotel permit.
  • Churches enforce covered shoulders and knees, including in high summer.
