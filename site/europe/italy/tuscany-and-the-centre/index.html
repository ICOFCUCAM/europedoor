Places to see

Everything recorded across Tuscany &amp; the Centre, in one list.

Brunelleschi's Dome

Four million bricks laid without a centring frame, climbed between its two shells by 463 steps.

Florence · Church

Uffizi

The Medici collection in the offices Vasari built for them, and the reason people book Florence months ahead.

Florence · Museum

Bargello

Donatello, Michelangelo and the sculpture the Uffizi queue never sees, in a 13th-century prison.

Florence · Museum

San Miniato al Monte

An 11th-century facade above the city, with Gregorian chant at vespers and almost no crowd.

Florence · Church

Piazza del Campo

A shell-shaped square sloping to the town hall, with the Palio run round its edge twice each summer.

Siena · Square

Siena Cathedral

Striped marble inside and out, over an inlaid floor uncovered only a few weeks a year.

Siena · Church

Portico di San Luca

666 arches climbing four kilometres out of the city so a procession would never get wet.

Bologna · Monument

Il Quadrilatero

The medieval trades quarter, still laid out by guild, still selling food at seven in the morning.

Bologna · Market

The Walls

Four kilometres of intact Renaissance rampart, planted with plane trees in 1818 and used as a park ever since.

Lucca · Monument

Piazza dell'Anfiteatro

The oval of a Roman amphitheatre, built over in houses, which is why the square is that shape.

Lucca · Square
