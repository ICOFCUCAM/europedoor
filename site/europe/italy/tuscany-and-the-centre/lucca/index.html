Tuscany & the Centre, Italy

Lucca

An intact Renaissance wall, four kilometres round, wide enough on top to be a park, with a Roman amphitheatre turned into a square inside it.

⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins● Food▦ Big cities

What earns the time

  • Cycling the walls, which is what the whole city does at six
  • Piazza dell'Anfiteatro: the amphitheatre's oval, built over in houses
  • Puccini's birthplace, and concerts most evenings
