Tuscany & the Centre, Italy

Bologna

Forty kilometres of porticoes, the oldest university in the world, and the best eating in Italy.

● Food⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture♫ Music & nightlife

Why visit

  • The Quadrilatero's food shops, unchanged in layout since the Middle Ages
  • Portico di San Luca: 666 arches uphill to a basilica
  • Tagliatelle al ragù, which is what the world calls bolognese and is not the same thing
