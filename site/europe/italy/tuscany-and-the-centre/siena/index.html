Siena

A medieval city that lost to Florence and was preserved by losing, arranged around a shell-shaped square.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture◈ Art & museums● Food

Why visit

  • The Campo, and the Palio run around it twice each summer
  • The cathedral's inlaid marble floor, uncovered a few weeks a year
  • The contrade — seventeen districts with their own churches, museums and feuds
