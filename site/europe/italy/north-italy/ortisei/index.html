The North & the Dolomites, Italy

Ortisei & the Dolomites

Pale limestone towers over alpine meadows, in a valley where Ladin is still the first language.

▲ Mountains❄ Snow & winter♣ Wild nature● Food

Why visit

  • Alpe di Siusi, the largest high-altitude meadow in Europe
  • Via ferrata routes built by soldiers in 1915–18
  • Rifugi serving South Tyrolean food at 2,000 metres
