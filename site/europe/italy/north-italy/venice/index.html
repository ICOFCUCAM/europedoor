The North & the Dolomites, Italy

Venice

A city on wooden piles in a lagoon, with no roads, fewer than 50,000 residents left, and no equivalent anywhere.

⌂ Architecture◈ Art & museums⚑ History & ruins≈ Coast & beaches

Why visit

  • Cannaregio and Castello, where people still live
  • The Accademia and the Punta della Dogana at opposite ends of taste
  • A traghetto across the Grand Canal, standing, for two euros
  • Torcello and the mosaics that predate the city

Places to see

3 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Basilica di San Marco

Eight thousand square metres of gold mosaic over a Byzantine plan, on a floor that undulates with the subsidence.

Church · 1 hour

Gallerie dell'Accademia

Five centuries of Venetian painting where it was painted, including Giorgione's Tempest.

Museum · 2 hours

Torcello

The lagoon's first settlement, now nine residents and a cathedral with mosaics older than Venice itself.

Island · half a day

Things to do

Cicchetti and an ombra

Small plates and a small glass of wine at a bàcaro, standing, moving on after one — the Venetian version of an aperitivo.
