The North & the Dolomites, Italy

Trieste

Austro-Hungarian, Italian, Slovene and Jewish at once, on a limestone edge above the Adriatic, with the best coffee culture in Italy.

⌂ Architecture● Food⚑ History & ruins≈ Coast & beaches

What earns the time

  • Piazza Unità d'Italia, the largest sea-facing square in Europe
  • The Risiera di San Sabba, the only Nazi camp with a crematorium on Italian soil
  • Coffee ordered in a local dialect that confuses every other Italian
