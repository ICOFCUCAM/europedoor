Places to see

Everything recorded across The North &amp; the Dolomites, in one list.

Basilica di San Marco

Eight thousand square metres of gold mosaic over a Byzantine plan, on a floor that undulates with the subsidence.

Venice · Church

Gallerie dell'Accademia

Five centuries of Venetian painting where it was painted, including Giorgione's Tempest.

Venice · Museum

Torcello

The lagoon's first settlement, now nine residents and a cathedral with mosaics older than Venice itself.

Venice · Island

Alpe di Siusi

The largest high-altitude alpine meadow in Europe, 57 square kilometres at 1,800 metres.

Ortisei & the Dolomites · Park

Seceda

A tilted limestone ridge above Val Gardena, reached by cable car and walked along the top.

Ortisei & the Dolomites · Mountain

The Last Supper

Fifteen minutes with Leonardo's wall, twenty-five people at a time, booked months ahead.

Milan · Monument

Duomo di Milano

Six centuries in the building, with roof terraces you walk among the spires and statues.

Milan · Church

Museo Egizio

The oldest Egyptian museum in the world and, by collection, second only to Cairo.

Turin · Museum

Mole Antonelliana

A 167-metre brick tower holding the national cinema museum, with a glass lift up the middle.

Turin · Monument

Arena di Verona

A 1st-century amphitheatre seating 15,000, hosting opera without amplification since 1913.

Verona · Theatre

Basilica di San Zeno

Bronze doors of 48 panels and a Mantegna altarpiece, in the quietest great church in the city.

Verona · Church

Sentiero Azzurro

The blue path between the five villages, ticketed, and closed in sections after each landslip.

Vernazza & the Cinque Terre · Monument

Risiera di San Sabba

A rice mill used as a Nazi camp with a crematorium — the only one on Italian soil — kept as a memorial.

Trieste · Monument

Piazza Unità d'Italia

The largest sea-facing square in Europe, Habsburg on three sides and open on the fourth.

Trieste · Square

Things to do

Cicchetti and an ombra

Small plates and a small glass of wine at a bàcaro, standing, moving on after one — the Venetian version of an aperitivo.

Venice · Food & table

A night in a rifugio

Dinner at seven, lights out at ten, and the Enrosadira — the pink glow on the rock at dawn — from the door.

Ortisei & the Dolomites · Walk or hike

Vermouth where it was invented

Carpano started here in 1786. The historic cafés still serve it the way it was drunk: cold, alone, with a small plate.

Turin · Cellar & vineyard

Opera in a Roman arena

Fifteen thousand people, no amplification, and the cheap seats on the stone steps are the ones with the acoustics.

Verona · Music & performance

The terraces, from above

The high paths behind the villages are free, empty and better; the coastal one is the queue.
