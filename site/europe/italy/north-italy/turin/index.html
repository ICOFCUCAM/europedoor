The North & the Dolomites, Italy

Turin

The first capital of Italy: baroque arcades, an Egyptian museum second only to Cairo's, and vermouth invented in a bar here.

Why visit

  • The Museo Egizio, which has more than most Egyptian cities
  • Eighteen kilometres of arcades, so the court could walk in the rain
  • Aperitivo, which Turin claims and Milan monetised

Travel tips

Practical things about Italy that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Coperto is a per-person cover charge and is legal and normal; a tip on top is not expected.
  • ZTL zones in historic centres are camera-enforced — never drive in without a hotel permit.
  • Churches enforce covered shoulders and knees, including in high summer.
