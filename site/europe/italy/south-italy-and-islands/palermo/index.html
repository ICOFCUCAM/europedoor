Palermo

Norman, Arab and Byzantine in the same building, with the best street food in Italy underneath.

● Food⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture✝ Sacred places

Why visit

  • The Cappella Palatina: Islamic ceiling, Byzantine mosaics, Norman king
  • Ballarò and Capo markets, shouted rather than sold
  • Monreale's mosaics, twenty minutes uphill

Ballarò

Monreale

6,500 square metres of gold mosaic twenty minutes uphill, which most Palermo visitors skip.

Church · 2 hours

Palermo by street food

Panelle, arancine, sfincione, and — if you are brave — pani ca meusa. A thousand years of occupation in six snacks.
