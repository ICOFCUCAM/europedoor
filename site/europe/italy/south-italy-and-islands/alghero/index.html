Alghero, Sardinia

A Catalan-speaking walled port on Sardinia's northwest coast, with sea caves and Bronze Age towers inland.

Why visit

  • Catalan street signs, and a dialect spoken since the 14th century
  • Neptune's Grotto, reached by 654 steps down a cliff
  • Nuraghi: 7,000 stone towers across the island, from 1800 BC

Travel tips

Practical things about Italy that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Coperto is a per-person cover charge and is legal and normal; a tip on top is not expected.
  • ZTL zones in historic centres are camera-enforced — never drive in without a hotel permit.
  • Churches enforce covered shoulders and knees, including in high summer.
