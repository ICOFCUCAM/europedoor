Places to see

Everything recorded across The South &amp; the Islands, in one list.

National Archaeological Museum

Everything good from Pompeii and Herculaneum, including the Farnese marbles and the secret cabinet.

Naples · Museum

Pompeii

A Roman town stopped in 79 AD: streets, bakeries, election graffiti and the casts of people who did not leave.

Naples · Archaeological site

Napoli Sotterranea

Greek quarries turned aqueduct turned air-raid shelter, forty metres under the old town.

Naples · Cave

The Sassi

Cave dwellings inhabited for nine thousand years, evacuated in 1952 as a national disgrace and now UNESCO-listed.

Matera · Quarter

Santa Maria de Idris

A rock church cut into a spur above the ravine, frescoed inside and reached by steps.

Matera · Church

Cappella Palatina

An Islamic muqarnas ceiling over Byzantine mosaics, commissioned by a Norman king, in one room.

Palermo · Church

Ballarò

A thousand years of shouted commerce, and the best street food in Italy under the awnings.

Palermo · Market

Monreale

6,500 square metres of gold mosaic twenty minutes uphill, which most Palermo visitors skip.

Palermo · Church

Duomo di Siracusa

A 5th-century BC Doric temple with a cathedral built around it; the columns are in the walls.

Syracuse & Ortigia · Church

Greek Theatre

Carved into the rock in the 5th century BC and still staging Aeschylus each summer.

Syracuse & Ortigia · Theatre

Basilica di Santa Croce

A century of carving in soft golden limestone, which is why the facade looks like it is melting upward.

Lecce · Church

Grotta di Nettuno

A sea cave under a 110-metre cliff, entered by 654 steps down or by boat in calm weather.

Alghero, Sardinia · Cave

Stromboli

A volcano that has erupted every twenty minutes or so for two thousand years, watched from the water at dusk.

Lipari & the Aeolians · Mountain

Things to do

Fried pizza on the street

Older and poorer than the baked kind, folded into a wallet and eaten walking; the sign to look for is a queue of locals.

Naples · Food & table

Palermo by street food

Panelle, arancine, sfincione, and — if you are brave — pani ca meusa. A thousand years of occupation in six snacks.

Palermo · Food & table

Inside a nuraghe

Su Nuraxi at Barumini is the UNESCO one, but there are seven thousand of these dry-stone towers and you can walk into many of them alone.

Alghero, Sardinia · Museum or collection

Stromboli, from the water

The volcano has erupted more or less continuously for two thousand years. Boats hold position off the Sciara del Fuoco after dark.
