The South & the Islands, Italy

Lipari & the Aeolians

Seven volcanic islands off Sicily, one of them erupting every twenty minutes, all of them on the UNESCO list.

○ Islands♣ Wild nature≈ Coast & beaches❦ Wildlife watching

Why visit

  • Stromboli's explosions, watched from a boat at dusk
  • Pumice and obsidian quarries on Lipari, white against black
  • Hydrofoils between islands as ordinary transport
