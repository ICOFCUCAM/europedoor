Travel tips

Practical things about Sweden that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Card everywhere; many places take no cash at all, including some museums and buses.
  • Systembolaget holds the monopoly on strong alcohol and closes on Sundays.
  • The right to roam comes with the duty not to disturb — never within sight of a house, never a fire in a dry summer.
