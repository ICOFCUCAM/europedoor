The Northwest & the Mountains, Azerbaijan

Xınalıq

A village at 2,300 metres whose people speak a language related to nothing else, reached by a single mountain road.

▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature⚑ History & ruins

What earns the time

  • Flat-roofed stone houses stacked up the slope
  • The Ketsh language, spoken by a few thousand people and nobody else
  • Shepherd routes into the high Caucasus
