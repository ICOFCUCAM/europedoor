Baku & the Caspian, Azerbaijan

Baku

A UNESCO-listed walled city with a mysterious stone tower, ringed by oil-boom mansions and three flame-shaped skyscrapers.

Capital city · 2–3 nights

Travel tips

Practical things about Azerbaijan that are not obvious from outside it.

  • The border with Armenia is closed; an Armenian stamp can complicate entry.
  • Baku's old city and the Gobustan petroglyphs are both UNESCO-listed.
  • Tea houses are a social institution and usually male-dominated outside the capital.
