Victoria, Gozo

A citadel town in the middle of an island where the pace drops noticeably the moment the ferry docks.

Why visit

  • The Ċittadella walls, and the whole island visible from them
  • Ġgantija: temples older than Ħaġar Qim, and older than the pyramids
  • Salt pans at Xwejni, cut into the rock and still worked by one family

Travel tips

Practical things about Malta that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Driving is on the left, a British legacy along with the letterboxes.
  • The temples require timed tickets and Ħaġar Qim is roofed for protection.
  • Swimming is off rocks and platforms more often than sand; the Blue Lagoon is beautiful and mobbed.
