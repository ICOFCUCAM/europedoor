Malta, Malta

Mdina & Rabat

The old walled capital on a hill, with about 250 residents and no traffic — called the silent city for a reason.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture✝ Sacred places

Why visit

  • The bastion walls over the whole island at dusk
  • St Paul's catacombs, cut in the 3rd century
  • Evening, when the day visitors leave and it earns its name
