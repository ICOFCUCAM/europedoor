Travel tips

Practical things about Malta that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Driving is on the left, a British legacy along with the letterboxes.
  • The temples require timed tickets and Ħaġar Qim is roofed for protection.
  • Swimming is off rocks and platforms more often than sand; the Blue Lagoon is beautiful and mobbed.
