Malta, Malta

Valletta

A planned fortress city of 1566, on a peninsula between two harbours, with Caravaggio's largest painting in its cathedral.

Why visit

  • St John's Co-Cathedral, plain outside and overwhelming inside
  • Caravaggio's Beheading of St John, the only work he signed
  • The Upper Barrakka gardens and the noon gun over the Grand Harbour
  • The Three Cities across the water, by traditional dgħajsa boat

Travel tips

Practical things about Malta that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Driving is on the left, a British legacy along with the letterboxes.
  • The temples require timed tickets and Ħaġar Qim is roofed for protection.
  • Swimming is off rocks and platforms more often than sand; the Blue Lagoon is beautiful and mobbed.
