Travel tips

Practical things about Cyprus that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Driving is on the left.
  • Crossing points between south and north exist and rules change — check current guidance before planning around one.
  • The Troodos churches are small, locked and often opened by a neighbour with a key; a donation is expected.
