Nicosia & the South Coast, Cyprus

Nicosia

A Venetian star-shaped fortress city with a UN buffer zone through the middle and a crossing on the main shopping street.

⚑ History & ruins● Food▦ Big cities

Why visit

  • The Venetian walls, eleven bastions, still tracing the city
  • The Cyprus Museum's Neolithic and Bronze Age collections
  • The Ledra Street crossing, and the shuttered street either side of it

Travel tips

Practical things about Cyprus that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Driving is on the left.
  • Crossing points between south and north exist and rules change — check current guidance before planning around one.
  • The Troodos churches are small, locked and often opened by a neighbour with a key; a donation is expected.
