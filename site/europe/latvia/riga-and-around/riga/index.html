Riga & Around, Latvia

Riga

A hanseatic old town wrapped in a boulevard ring, wrapped in the best Art Nouveau streets in Europe.

⌂ Architecture◈ Art & museums▦ Big cities● Food♫ Music & nightlife

Why visit

  • Alberta iela, where Mikhail Eisenstein put faces on every facade
  • The Central Market in five Zeppelin hangars
  • The wooden architecture of Kalnciema across the river
  • The Latvian National Museum of Art, sharp and well-lit
