Travel tips

Practical things about Latvia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Riga's Central Market occupies five former Zeppelin hangars.
  • The Latvian language is one of only two surviving Baltic languages, and older in structure than most of Europe's.
  • Forest covers more than half the country and mushrooming in autumn is a national activity.
