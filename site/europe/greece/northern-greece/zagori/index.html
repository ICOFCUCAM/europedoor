Northern Greece, Greece

Zagori (Monodendri)

Forty-six stone villages in the Pindus mountains, linked by packhorse bridges over the deepest gorge in the world by ratio.

▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature● Food❦ Wildlife watching

What earns the time

  • The Vikos gorge, from the Oxya viewpoint or from inside it
  • Arched stone bridges built by 18th-century merchant guilds
  • Bears, wolves and 1,800 plant species in the national park
