Why visit

  • The Kamares aqueduct, striding across the middle of town
  • Philippi: a Roman city, a battlefield, and where Paul first preached in Europe
  • Ferries to Thasos, which is green and largely undeveloped

Travel tips

Practical things about Greece that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Ferry cancellations for wind are common and not negotiable; leave a buffer day before a flight home.
  • Many older buildings ask you to bin paper rather than flush it — follow the sign.
  • Archaeological sites close early in winter and midday shade is scarce; go at opening.
