Northern Greece, Greece

Kavala

An Ottoman aqueduct over a harbour town, with Philippi's ruins inland and Thasos across the water.

⚑ History & ruins≈ Coast & beaches● Food✝ Sacred places

What earns the time

  • The Kamares aqueduct, striding across the middle of town
  • Philippi: a Roman city, a battlefield, and where Paul first preached in Europe
  • Ferries to Thasos, which is green and largely undeveloped
