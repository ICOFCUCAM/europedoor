Northern Greece, Greece

Ioannina

An Ottoman lake city with an island in it, a silversmithing tradition and the gateway to Zagori.

⚑ History & ruins● Food✝ Sacred places♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • The walled kastro, and two mosques still standing inside it
  • The island, reached in ten minutes, with five monasteries and no cars
  • Silverwork, which the city has done for four centuries
