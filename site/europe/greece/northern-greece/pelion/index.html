Pelion (Makrinitsa)

Stone villages with slate roofs on a wooded mountain that drops to beaches on both sides.

▲ Mountains≈ Coast & beaches♣ Wild nature● Food

Why visit

  • Kalderimia: cobbled mule paths between villages, still the best way around
  • The narrow-gauge Moutzouris railway, built by Evaristo de Chirico
  • Apples, chestnuts and firs on one side of the ridge, the Aegean on the other
