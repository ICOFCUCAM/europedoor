Mystras

A Byzantine city on a spur of Taygetos, abandoned in the 1950s and left standing: palaces, churches and frescoes.

Why visit

  • A whole late-Byzantine city, walked from the castle down
  • Frescoes in six churches, most of them unrestored
  • Sparta below, which has almost nothing left
