Places to see

Everything recorded across Athens &amp; the Peloponnese, in one list.

The Acropolis

The Parthenon, the Erechtheion and the Propylaea on one rock, best at opening or in the last hour.

Athens · Archaeological site

Acropolis Museum

The Parthenon frieze laid out at actual scale and orientation, with casts where the originals are in London.

Athens · Museum

National Archaeological Museum

The Mask of Agamemnon, the Antikythera mechanism, and the best classical collection anywhere.

Athens · Museum

Anafiotika

A Cycladic village built inside Athens by island masons in the 1840s, and still lived in.

Athens · Quarter

Palamidi

A Venetian fortress of eight bastions above the town, reached by 999 steps or a road.

Nafplio · Castle

Epidaurus Theatre

A 4th-century BC theatre whose acoustics carry a whisper to the back row, and still nobody fully knows why.

Nafplio · Theatre

Mycenae

The Lion Gate and the shaft graves of a civilisation that collapsed three thousand years ago.

Nafplio · Archaeological site

The Upper Town

A Byzantine town on top of the rock, with Hagia Sophia on the edge and almost nobody up there.

Monemvasia · Ruin

Sanctuary of Apollo

The sacred way, the temple, the treasuries and the stadium above them all, on a terrace of Parnassus.

Delphi · Archaeological site

Delphi Museum

The bronze Charioteer, which survived because an earthquake buried it.

Delphi · Museum

The Stadium

Entered through a vaulted tunnel, with the original start line still in place.

Olympia · Archaeological site

Mystras

A whole late-Byzantine city on a spur of Taygetos: palaces, churches, frescoes, walked downhill.

Mystras · Archaeological site

Viros Gorge

A dry riverbed walked from the sea up into Taygetos, past chapels cut into the rock.

Kardamyli & the Mani · Mountain

Hydra Harbour

Shipowners' stone mansions round a working port with no cars, no bicycles and mules on the quay.

Hydra · Monument

Things to do

The Acropolis Museum, third floor

The Parthenon frieze laid out at actual scale and orientation, with plaster casts where the British Museum holds the originals — the gaps are the argument.

Athens · Museum or collection

The acoustics at Epidaurus

Sit in the top row and have someone whisper on the orchestra floor. Built in the 4th century BC and still unexplained in full.

Nafplio · Sacred site

The sacred way at opening

The site opens at eight and the coaches arrive at ten. Two hours of Delphi alone is a different place from Delphi at noon.
