Athens & the Peloponnese, Greece

Kardamyli & the Mani

Stone tower houses on a wild peninsula where blood feuds ran into the 20th century, above a clear sea.

Why visit

  • Tower villages built for feuding families, still standing empty
  • The Viros gorge, walked from the sea up into Taygetos
  • Patrick Leigh Fermor's house, now open by appointment
