Athens & the Peloponnese, Greece

Delphi

The navel of the ancient world, on a terrace of Mount Parnassus above a sea of olive trees.

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • The sacred way up to the temple of Apollo, where the oracle spoke
  • The bronze Charioteer, which survived because an earthquake buried it
  • The stadium at the top, above everything, and usually empty
