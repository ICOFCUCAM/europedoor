Athens & the Peloponnese, Greece

Athens

A sprawling modern capital with a 2,500-year-old temple on a rock in the middle and a food scene that got very good, quietly.

Why visit

  • The Acropolis at opening, and the Acropolis Museum afterwards, in that order
  • The National Archaeological Museum, which is the real prize
  • Rooftop bars looking at a floodlit Parthenon
  • Anafiotika, a Cycladic village accidentally inside the city

Travel tips

Practical things about Greece that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Ferry cancellations for wind are common and not negotiable; leave a buffer day before a flight home.
  • Many older buildings ask you to bin paper rather than flush it — follow the sign.
  • Archaeological sites close early in winter and midday shade is scarce; go at opening.
