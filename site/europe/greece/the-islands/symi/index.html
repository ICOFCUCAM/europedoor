The Islands, Greece

Symi

Neoclassical mansions in ochre and blue stacked round a horseshoe harbour, an hour from Rhodes.

○ Islands≈ Coast & beaches⌂ Architecture✝ Sacred places

Why visit

  • The harbour at Gialos, which is the reason to come
  • Panormitis monastery at the southern end, reached by boat
  • Sponge diving history, and the shops that still sell them
