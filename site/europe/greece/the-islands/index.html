Places to see

Everything recorded across The Islands, in one list.

Portara

A temple doorway on an islet at the harbour, left standing because the marble was too big to steal.

Naxos · Monument

The unfinished kouros

A ten-metre marble figure lying where it cracked in the quarry 2,600 years ago.

Naxos · Archaeological site

Akrotiri

A Bronze Age town buried in ash and preserved whole, with two-storey houses and drains that still work.

Santorini · Archaeological site

The caldera path

Ten kilometres along the rim from Fira to Oia, walked early rather than at sunset.

Santorini · Mountain

Samaria Gorge

Sixteen kilometres downhill through the White Mountains to a village with no road out.

Chania, Crete · Mountain

Venetian Harbour

A curved quay, a lighthouse rebuilt by Egyptians in the 1830s, and a mosque at the near end.

Chania, Crete · Monument

Palace of the Grand Master

A crusader fortress rebuilt by Italy in the 1930s as a holiday residence for Mussolini, who never used it.

Rhodes · Castle

Lindos Acropolis

A Doric stoa on a rock above a white village and a bay, climbed on foot or by donkey.

Sarakiniko

Knossos

Things to do

The interior, not the beach

Half the island is farmland at altitude — cheese, potatoes and Venetian towers, twenty minutes from the sand.

Naxos · Rail, road or ride

Vines grown in baskets

No trellises: the vines are woven into circles on the ground to shelter the fruit from wind and catch night moisture.

Santorini · Cellar & vineyard

Down the Samaria gorge

Sixteen kilometres, one direction only, ending at a boat because there is no road out. Open May to October.

Chania, Crete · Walk or hike

Round the island by boat

Half the coast has no road to it. A day boat from Adamas is the only way to see Kleftiko and the southern coves.

Milos · On the water

A panigiri that ends at dawn

A village saint's day: one long table, live violin and lute, dancing in a circle, and food priced to cover the church roof.
