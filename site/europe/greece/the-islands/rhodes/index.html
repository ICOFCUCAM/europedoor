Rhodes

A walled medieval city built by crusader knights, on an island of Ottoman villages and butterfly valleys.

Why visit

  • The largest inhabited medieval town in Europe, inside four kilometres of wall
  • Lindos's acropolis above a white village and a bay
  • Ottoman baths and mosques the guidebooks skip
