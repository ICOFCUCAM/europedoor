The Islands, Greece

Corfu

Venetian for four centuries and British for fifty, green all year, with an old town that looks nothing like the Aegean.

○ Islands⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins● Food

Why visit

  • The UNESCO old town, Venetian streets and two fortresses
  • Cricket on the Spianada, a British hangover
  • Mount Pantokrator, and Albania visible across the strait
