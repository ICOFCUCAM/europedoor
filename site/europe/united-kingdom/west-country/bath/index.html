The West Country, United Kingdom

Bath

A Roman bathing complex under a Georgian city built in one honey-coloured stone.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture● Food

Why visit

  • The Roman Baths, still fed by the only hot spring in Britain
  • The Royal Crescent and the Circus, built as speculation and now the argument for it
  • Bathing in the thermal water again, at the modern spa
