Scotland, United Kingdom

Islay

A low green island of nine working distilleries, 3,000 people and 50,000 wintering geese.

◑ Wine & drink≈ Coast & beaches❦ Wildlife watching♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • Peated whisky at the distillery that made it, on the shore
  • Barnacle geese arriving from Greenland each October
  • Machir Bay, empty in any weather
