Travel tips

Practical things about United Kingdom that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Not in Schengen: your Schengen days do not apply here and the border check is real.
  • Driving is on the left, and rural roads in Wales and the Highlands are single-track with passing places.
  • Right to roam exists in Scotland and barely exists in England — footpaths there are rights of way across private land, which is a different thing.
