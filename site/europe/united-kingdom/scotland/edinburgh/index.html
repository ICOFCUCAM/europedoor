Edinburgh

A medieval spine running down from a castle on a plug of volcanic rock, with a Georgian grid alongside.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture♫ Music & nightlife▦ Big cities✵ Festivals

Why visit

  • The Royal Mile at seven in the morning, before anyone else
  • Arthur's Seat, a full hill inside the city
  • The National Museum of Scotland's free, excellent everything
  • The Fringe in August, which triples the population
