Wales, United Kingdom

Eryri / Snowdonia (Betws-y-Coed)

Slate, lakes and the highest mountains in England and Wales, in a national park with a Welsh-speaking heart.

▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature═ Slow travel by rail

Why visit

  • Yr Wyddfa by the Watkin or Rhyd Ddu path rather than the crowded one
  • The slate landscape of Blaenau Ffestiniog, now UNESCO-listed
  • Narrow-gauge railways built for slate, now for the view

The slate railway to the coast
