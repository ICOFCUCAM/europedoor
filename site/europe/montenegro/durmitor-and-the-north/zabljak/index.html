Durmitor & the North, Montenegro

Žabljak

The highest town in the Balkans at 1,450 metres, at the edge of Durmitor national park.

Town · 2–3 nights

▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature❄ Snow & winter❦ Wildlife watching

Why go

  1. 01The Black Lake, twenty minutes' walk from the town
  2. 02Bobotov Kuk, 2,523 metres, a long day on rock
  3. 03The Tara canyon, 1,300 metres deep, rafted in spring

Travel tips

Practical things about Montenegro that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Uses the euro unilaterally; not in the EU.
  • The coast road is single carriageway and slow in summer; the Sozina tunnel saves an hour.
  • Mountain weather in Durmitor turns fast and rescue is limited.
