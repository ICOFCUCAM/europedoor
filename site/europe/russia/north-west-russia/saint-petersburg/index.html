The Northwest, Russia

Saint Petersburg

A planned imperial capital of canals and neoclassical facades, built on piles in a delta from 1703.

◈ Art & museums⌂ Architecture♫ Music & nightlife⚑ History & ruins

Why visit

  • The Hermitage, in the Winter Palace
  • The Church of the Saviour on Spilled Blood and its mosaic interior
  • White Nights in June, when the bridges lift at night
  • Peterhof's fountains, which run without pumps
