The Northwest, Russia

Saint Petersburg

A planned imperial capital of canals and neoclassical facades, built on piles in a delta from 1703.

City · 3–4 nights

◈ Art & museums⌂ Architecture♫ Music & nightlife⚑ History & ruins

Why go

  1. 01The Hermitage, in the Winter Palace
  2. 02The Church of the Saviour on Spilled Blood and its mosaic interior
  3. 03White Nights in June, when the bridges lift at night
  4. 04Peterhof's fountains, which run without pumps

Saint Petersburg is at 59.940°N, 30.310°E.

Travel tips

Practical things about Russia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Check your own government's current travel advice; most advise against travel.
  • This country is excluded from the Journey Planner.
  • Western bank cards do not function and consular assistance is limited.
