Check government travel advice before planning anything here

European governments advise against all or all but essential travel to Russia, citing the security situation, the risk of arbitrary detention, limited consular assistance and severely restricted flight and payment options. This page is a record of the European part of the country and is not a recommendation to travel there.

EuropeDoor keeps a page for every country in Europe, including the ones nobody should be travelling to right now. A page here is a record, not a recommendation, and countries at this level are excluded from the Journey Planner.

The portrait

Why Russia belongs in your Europe.

Russia west of the Urals is geographically European and holds Moscow, St Petersburg and the Golden Ring of medieval towns. St Petersburg was built from nothing on a marsh in 1703 as a deliberate window on Europe, and the Hermitage is among the largest art collections in the world. This page records that; the advisory above governs whether any of it is currently reachable.

Where Russia is worth going

2 destinations across 2 travel regions.

Getting around

Historically an extensive rail network including the overnight Moscow–St Petersburg services. Current air links to Europe are severely limited and Western cards do not work.

When to come

Historically the White Nights in June for St Petersburg. This is context, not a recommendation.

Worth knowing

  • Check your own government’s current travel advice; most advise against travel.
  • This country is excluded from the Journey Planner.
  • Western bank cards do not function and consular assistance is limited.
