The portrait

Why Austria belongs in your Europe.

Austria spent six centuries as the seat of an empire and then lost it in an afternoon, which is why Vienna is a capital built for twice the country. Everything west of it is mountain: ski resorts in winter, high pasture in summer, and lakes cold enough to hurt. The coffee house, the opera and the Sunday hike are all still institutions rather than attractions.

Where Austria is worth going

5 destinations across 3 travel regions.

Getting around

ÖBB trains are punctual and the Railjet crosses the country in four hours. Mountain valleys have postbuses timed to the trains. Vienna’s transport is cheap and the annual pass is famously a euro a day.

When to come

Vienna is a year-round city and best in the shoulder months. The Alps have two seasons and a gap between them: lifts and mountain huts close in April–May and again in November.

Worth knowing

  • In a coffee house you are buying the table, not the coffee; nobody will rush you.
  • Mountain huts (Hütten) need booking and cash, and lights go out early.
  • Sunday closing is near-total outside stations and tourist areas.

At the table

  • Wiener Schnitzel, veal and beaten thin, with potato salad
  • Kaffee und Kuchen, an afternoon institution with its own vocabulary
  • Wine from the Wachau and Burgenland, which nobody outside expects
