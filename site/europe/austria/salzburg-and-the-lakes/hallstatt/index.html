Salzburg & the Salzkammergut, Austria

Hallstatt

A lakeside village under a salt mountain, with 7,000 years of continuous mining and more visitors than it can hold.

♣ Wild nature⚑ History & ruins

What earns the time

  • The oldest salt mine in the world, and a wooden staircase from 1344 inside it
  • The bone house, with painted skulls stacked by family
  • Early morning or evening — the day-trip window is 10 to 4 and it is unbearable
