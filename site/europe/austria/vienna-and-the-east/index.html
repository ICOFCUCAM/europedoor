Austria

Vienna & the East

The imperial capital, the Danube valley's vineyards, and Hungary's old border country.

2 destinations · 4 places recorded · about 5 nights to see it all

Destinations

Vienna

Habsburg on the outside, Freud and Klimt and social housing on the inside, with the world's best-funded music scene.

Capital city
3–4 nights

Dürnstein & the Wachau

A blue baroque tower over the Danube, terraced vineyards behind, and the castle where Richard the Lionheart was held.

Village
1–2 nights

Places to see

Everything recorded across Vienna & the East, in one list.

Schönbrunn Palace

1,441 rooms, a park that is free to walk, and a zoo founded in 1752 that is the oldest still operating anywhere.

Vienna · Castle

Kunsthistorisches Museum

The Habsburg collection, which means the largest group of Bruegels in existence and a room of Vermeer, Velázquez and Caravaggio that would be a national museum anywhere else.

Vienna · Museum

Stephansdom

The tiled roof is the thing: 230,000 glazed tiles in a chevron pattern, laid as a double eagle on one side and the city arms on the other.

Vienna · Church

Naschmarkt

A kilometre and a half of stalls that began as a milk market in the 1770s. Cheaper and less performed at the far end from the underground station.

Vienna · Market

Things to do

Standing room at the Staatsoper

A few euros, queued for on the night, in one of the great opera houses. Bring a scarf to tie to the rail and hold your place.

Vienna · Music & performance

A heuriger on the city edge

Wine taverns in Vienna's own vineyards, open only when the grower has wine to sell — a pine branch over the door means open.

Vienna · Cellar & vineyard
