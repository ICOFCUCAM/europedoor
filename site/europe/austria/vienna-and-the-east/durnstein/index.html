Travel tips

Practical things about Austria that are not obvious from outside it.

  • In a coffee house you are buying the table, not the coffee; nobody will rush you.
  • Mountain huts (Hütten) need booking and cash, and lights go out early.
  • Sunday closing is near-total outside stations and tourist areas.
