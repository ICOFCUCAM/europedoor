Nordkette

A cable railway from the middle of the city to 2,300 m in twenty minutes. Few places let you go from a coffee to an alpine ridge that fast.

Mountain · half a day

Goldenes Dachl

A balcony roofed with 2,657 fire-gilded copper tiles, built in 1500 so Maximilian I could watch tournaments in the square below.

Monument · 20 minutes

Things to do

City to summit in twenty minutes

Tram, funicular and two cable cars take you from a café table to 2,256 metres of exposed ridge; take a jacket you would not think you needed.

Travel tips

Practical things about Austria that are not obvious from outside it.

  • In a coffee house you are buying the table, not the coffee; nobody will rush you.
  • Mountain huts (Hütten) need booking and cash, and lights go out early.
  • Sunday closing is near-total outside stations and tourist areas.
