Travel tips

Practical things about Iceland that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Weather closes roads with little warning; a rental booked in July can still be stuck in a storm.
  • Off-road driving is illegal and damages moss that takes decades to regrow.
  • Tap water is glacial and free; buying it bottled is the most conspicuous tourist error in the country.
