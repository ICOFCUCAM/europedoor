Places to see

Everything recorded across The South Coast & East, in one list.

Reynisfjara

Black sand, basalt columns and sneaker waves that have killed people who turned their backs on the sea. Watch the water; it is not a beach for paddling.

Vík í Mýrdal · Beach

Jökulsárlón

A lagoon where the glacier calves icebergs that drift out to sea and wash back onto the black beach opposite, which is why it is called Diamond Beach.

Höfn & Jökulsárlón · Viewpoint

Things to do

Into a glacier, in winter only

The blue caves form and collapse each year; go with a guide who checks them that morning, and only from November to March.
