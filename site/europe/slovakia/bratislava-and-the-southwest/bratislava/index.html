Travel tips

Practical things about Slovakia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Mountain rescue in the Tatras is charged to the rescued; insurance is essential.
  • Wooden churches in the northeast are UNESCO-listed and often need a key from a neighbour.
  • Euro since 2009, unlike its neighbours.
