The Tatras & the North, Slovakia

Levoča & the Spiš

A walled medieval town with the tallest wooden altar in the world, under the ruins of Spiš castle.

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places⌂ Architecture

Why visit

  • Master Pavol's altar, 18.6 metres of carved lime wood
  • Spiš castle, four hectares of ruin on a limestone hill
  • The town walls and the wooden cage of shame in the square
