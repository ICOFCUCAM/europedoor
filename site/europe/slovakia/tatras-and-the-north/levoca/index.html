The Tatras & the North, Slovakia

Levoča & the Spiš

A walled medieval town with the tallest wooden altar in the world, under the ruins of Spiš castle.

Town · 1–2 nights

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places⌂ Architecture

Why go

  1. 01Master Pavol's altar, 18.6 metres of carved lime wood
  2. 02Spiš castle, four hectares of ruin on a limestone hill
  3. 03The town walls and the wooden cage of shame in the square

Travel tips

Practical things about Slovakia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Mountain rescue in the Tatras is charged to the rescued; insurance is essential.
  • Wooden churches in the northeast are UNESCO-listed and often need a key from a neighbour.
  • Euro since 2009, unlike its neighbours.
