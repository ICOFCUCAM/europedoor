Bucharest & the Danube Delta, Romania

Tulcea & the Danube Delta

The gateway to 5,800 square kilometres of reed, channel and lake, with 300 bird species and villages reachable only by boat.

Why visit

  • Pelican colonies, the largest outside Africa
  • Fishing villages with no road access at all
  • Fish soup cooked on the boat, in the delta, by the person who caught it

Travel tips

Practical things about Romania that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Bears are genuinely present in the Carpathians; do not feed them from a car, which is both illegal and how people get hurt.
  • The leu, not the euro.
  • Village guesthouses (pensiuni) are the best accommodation in the country and cost very little.
