Bucharest

Belle Époque boulevards, communist megastructures, and the second-largest administrative building on earth.

Why visit

  • The Palace of the Parliament, which required demolishing a fifth of the old city
  • The Village Museum: 300 relocated buildings in a park
  • Lipscani's bars, in the old merchant quarter
