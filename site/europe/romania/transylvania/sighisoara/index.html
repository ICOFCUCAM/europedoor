Transylvania, Romania

Sighișoara

A walled Saxon citadel still lived in, with a clock tower and a covered stairway to a hilltop school.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture

Why visit

  • The clock tower's figures, turning since 1648
  • The covered stairway, 175 steps, built so children could get to school in snow
  • Painted guild houses inside the walls
