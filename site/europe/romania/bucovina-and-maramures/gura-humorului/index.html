Travel tips

Practical things about Romania that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Bears are genuinely present in the Carpathians; do not feed them from a car, which is both illegal and how people get hurt.
  • The leu, not the euro.
  • Village guesthouses (pensiuni) are the best accommodation in the country and cost very little.
