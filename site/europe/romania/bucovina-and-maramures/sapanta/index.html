Bucovina & Maramureș, Romania

Săpânța & Maramureș

Wooden churches with tall shingled spires, and a cemetery where every headstone is painted, rhymed and often rude.

✝ Sacred places⚑ History & ruins● Food

What earns the time

  • The Merry Cemetery: 800 carved oak crosses with verses about the dead
  • Wooden churches on the UNESCO list, built without nails
  • Horse carts, which are transport rather than heritage
