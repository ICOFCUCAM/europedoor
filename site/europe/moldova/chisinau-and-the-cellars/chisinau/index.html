Travel tips

Practical things about Moldova that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Transnistria is a de facto separate administration; entry involves its own controls and your government's advice may differ for it.
  • Village guesthouse networks are well organised and startlingly cheap.
  • Cellar visits are usually by car through the tunnels, which is exactly as strange as it sounds.
