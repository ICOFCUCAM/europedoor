Orheiul Vechi & the North, Moldova

Orheiul Vechi

A monastery dug into a limestone cliff over a loop of the Răut, inhabited on and off since the 13th century.

✝ Sacred places♣ Wild nature⚑ History & ruins

Why visit

  • The cave church, reached along the cliff edge
  • Butuceni village below, with guesthouses in stone houses
  • Getic and Tatar remains in the same valley
