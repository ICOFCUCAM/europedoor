The Auvergne & the Massif Central, France

Le Puy-en-Velay

Chapels on top of volcanic plugs, green lentils with their own appellation, and the start of the oldest road to Santiago.

Things to do

The first day of the Via Podiensis

The oldest of the Santiago routes leaves from the cathedral steps after the 7 am pilgrim mass. The first day to Saint-Privat is twenty-three kilometres and sets the tone.

Travel tips

Practical things about France that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch is 12 to 2 and a rural kitchen closes at 13:45 regardless of your plans.
  • Bonjour on entering a shop is not optional politeness; skipping it reads as rudeness.
  • Many museums are free on the first Sunday of the month, and unbearable on it.
