Mont-Saint-Michel

An abbey on a rock in a bay with the fastest tides in Europe, and a village winding up to it.

✝ Sacred places≈ Coast & beaches⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture

Why visit

  • The abbey at opening or on a night visit, not at noon
  • Walking the bay barefoot with a guide, which is the only safe way
  • The tide, which moves faster than a person walks on spring highs

Travel tips

Practical things about France that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch is 12 to 2 and a rural kitchen closes at 13:45 regardless of your plans.
  • Bonjour on entering a shop is not optional politeness; skipping it reads as rudeness.
  • Many museums are free on the first Sunday of the month, and unbearable on it.
