Normandy & Brittany, France

Saint-Malo

A corsair city inside granite ramparts, rebuilt stone by stone after 1944, with a tidal range of twelve metres.

Why visit

  • The ramparts circuit at high tide, when spray comes over
  • Walking to the Grand Bé island at low water — and watching the clock
  • Oysters at Cancale, ten kilometres east

Travel tips

Practical things about France that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch is 12 to 2 and a rural kitchen closes at 13:45 regardless of your plans.
  • Bonjour on entering a shop is not optional politeness; skipping it reads as rudeness.
  • Many museums are free on the first Sunday of the month, and unbearable on it.
