Champagne & the North, France

Amiens

The largest gothic cathedral in France by volume, and market gardens on canals behind it.

✝ Sacred places⌂ Architecture● Food♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • The cathedral, twice the volume of Notre-Dame de Paris
  • Hortillonnages: 300 hectares of floating market gardens, seen from a punt
  • Jules Verne's house, where he wrote most of it
