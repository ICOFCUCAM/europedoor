Champagne & the North, France

Reims

Where thirty-three French kings were crowned, above twenty kilometres of Roman chalk cellars full of champagne.

Why visit

  • The cathedral's smiling angel, and Chagall's windows behind the altar
  • Crayères: Gallo-Roman chalk pits, now cellars, at 10°C year-round
  • The Montagne de Reims villages, where the grapes actually come from

Travel tips

Practical things about France that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch is 12 to 2 and a rural kitchen closes at 13:45 regardless of your plans.
  • Bonjour on entering a shop is not optional politeness; skipping it reads as rudeness.
  • Many museums are free on the first Sunday of the month, and unbearable on it.
