Travel tips

Practical things about France that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch is 12 to 2 and a rural kitchen closes at 13:45 regardless of your plans.
  • Bonjour on entering a shop is not optional politeness; skipping it reads as rudeness.
  • Many museums are free on the first Sunday of the month, and unbearable on it.
