Corsica, France

Bonifacio

A citadel on white limestone cliffs undercut by the sea, with Sardinia visible across twelve kilometres of strait.

Why visit

  • The King of Aragon's staircase, 187 steps cut into the cliff face
  • Boat trips into the sea caves under the town
  • Lavezzi islands, granite and empty, half an hour offshore

Travel tips

Practical things about France that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Lunch is 12 to 2 and a rural kitchen closes at 13:45 regardless of your plans.
  • Bonjour on entering a shop is not optional politeness; skipping it reads as rudeness.
  • Many museums are free on the first Sunday of the month, and unbearable on it.
