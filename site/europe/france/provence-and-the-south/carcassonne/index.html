Carcassonne

A double-walled citadel of 52 towers, heavily restored in the 1850s and argued about ever since.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture◑ Wine & drink

Why visit

  • The walls at night, when the day visitors have gone and it is floodlit
  • Viollet-le-Duc's restoration, and the debate about whether it invented as much as it saved
  • Corbières and Minervois wine in the plain below
