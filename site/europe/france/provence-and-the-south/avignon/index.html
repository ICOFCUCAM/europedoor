Avignon

Seven popes ruled from here in the 14th century and left the largest gothic palace in the world behind them.

Why visit

  • The Palais des Papes, more fortress than palace
  • The famous half-bridge, which stops in the Rhône
  • The July festival, when the whole city becomes a stage
