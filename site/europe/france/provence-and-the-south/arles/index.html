Arles

A Roman arena still in use, the Rhône delta at the door, and the year Van Gogh painted two hundred canvases here.

⚑ History & ruins◈ Art & museums● Food

Why visit

  • The amphitheatre, built in 90 AD and still hosting events
  • The Camargue: salt, white horses, flamingos, and a rice crop
  • Les Rencontres, Europe's oldest photography festival, in July
