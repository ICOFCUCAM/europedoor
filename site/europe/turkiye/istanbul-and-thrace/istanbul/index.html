Istanbul & Thrace, Türkiye

Istanbul

Capital of two empires, on two continents, with a 1,500-year-old church-mosque at the centre of it.

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places● Food⌂ Architecture▦ Big cities

Why visit

  • Hagia Sophia, and the Basilica Cistern under the street outside
  • The Süleymaniye mosque, quieter and better proportioned than the Blue Mosque
  • A commuter ferry to Kadıköy at dusk, with tea, for the price of a bus ride
  • Breakfast in Beşiktaş, which will take the whole morning

Things to do

Between continents on a commuter ferry

Twenty minutes, a glass of tea, gulls following the stern, and Europe and Asia both in view. The single best thing in the city and nearly free.

On the water · low

A two-hour Turkish breakfast

Fifteen small dishes, endless tea, and no expectation that you leave. Order for the table and clear the morning.
