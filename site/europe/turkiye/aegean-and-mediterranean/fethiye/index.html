Fethiye & the Lycian coast

Rock-cut tombs in the cliffs above a harbour, and a 540-kilometre coastal path starting nearby.

Why visit

  • The Lycian Way, hugging the coast for 540 km
  • Sunken ruins in the shallows off Kekova, seen from a boat
  • Butterfly Valley and the beaches reachable only by sea
