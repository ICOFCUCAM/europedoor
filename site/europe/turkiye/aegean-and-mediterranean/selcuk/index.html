Selçuk & Ephesus

A market town beside one of the best-preserved classical cities in the Mediterranean.

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places● Food

Why visit

  • Ephesus's library facade and marble street
  • The terrace houses, roofed and separately ticketed, with intact mosaics
  • Şirince, a hill village of former Greek houses making fruit wine
