Göreme, Cappadocia

Soft volcanic rock eroded into cones and carved out for a thousand years into churches, houses and underground cities.

Why visit

  • Rock-cut churches with Byzantine frescoes in the open-air museum
  • Derinkuyu, an underground city eighteen storeys deep
  • Balloons at dawn, weather permitting, which it often does not
  • The Rose and Red valleys, walked at sunset

Travel tips

Practical things about Türkiye that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Only East Thrace and part of Istanbul are geographically in Europe; the rest is Asia.
  • Mosques welcome visitors outside prayer times; shoes off, shoulders and knees covered, heads covered for women.
  • Bargaining is expected in bazaars and not in shops.
