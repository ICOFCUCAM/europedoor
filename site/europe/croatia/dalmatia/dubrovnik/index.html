Places to see

The City Walls

Two kilometres of unbroken circuit, up to 25 m high, walked in one direction only. Go at opening or an hour before closing; the middle of the day is a queue with a view.

Monument · 2 hours

Rector's Palace

Where the elected head of the Ragusan republic lived for exactly one month at a time, forbidden to leave the building — a constitution designed around distrust of power.

Travel tips

Practical things about Croatia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Euro and Schengen since 2023 — the old kuna is gone and the Slovenian border is open.
  • Water shoes are genuinely useful; most beaches are pebble or rock.
  • Dubrovnik's old town has cruise-ship peaks; check the arrivals calendar and go on a light day.
