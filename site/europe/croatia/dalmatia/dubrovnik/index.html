Dalmatia, Croatia

Dubrovnik

A maritime republic in near-perfect limestone, walled, car-free, and under real pressure from its own popularity.

⚑ History & ruins≈ Coast & beaches⌂ Architecture

What earns the time

  • The walls, walked at opening or in the last hour
  • Lokrum island, ten minutes by boat, with a monastery and peacocks
  • The Rector's Palace and the 1317 pharmacy still trading
