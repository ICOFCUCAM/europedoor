Places to see

Everything recorded across Dalmatia, in one list.

Diocletian's Palace

Not a ruin: a Roman emperor's retirement palace that three thousand people still live inside. The cathedral is his mausoleum, repurposed by the people he persecuted.

Split · Archaeological site

Marjan Hill

A pine-covered headland walked by everyone in Split at some point on a Sunday, with swimming coves on the south side and the whole coast from the top.

Split · Park

The City Walls

Two kilometres of unbroken circuit, up to 25 m high, walked in one direction only. Go at opening or an hour before closing; the middle of the day is a queue with a view.

Dubrovnik · Monument

Rector's Palace

Where the elected head of the Ragusan republic lived for exactly one month at a time, forbidden to leave the building — a constitution designed around distrust of power.

Dubrovnik · Monument

Things to do

The palace substructures

The cellars survive because they were filled with rubbish for centuries; they map the imperial floor plan above exactly.
