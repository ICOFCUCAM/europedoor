Places to see

Diocletian's Palace

Not a ruin: a Roman emperor's retirement palace that three thousand people still live inside. The cathedral is his mausoleum, repurposed by the people he persecuted.

Archaeological site · 3 hours

Marjan Hill

A pine-covered headland walked by everyone in Split at some point on a Sunday, with swimming coves on the south side and the whole coast from the top.

Park · 2–3 hours

2 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Things to do

The palace substructures

The cellars survive because they were filled with rubbish for centuries; they map the imperial floor plan above exactly.

Travel tips

Practical things about Croatia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Euro and Schengen since 2023 — the old kuna is gone and the Slovenian border is open.
  • Water shoes are genuinely useful; most beaches are pebble or rock.
  • Dubrovnik's old town has cruise-ship peaks; check the arrivals calendar and go on a light day.
