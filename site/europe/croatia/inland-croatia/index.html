Places to see

Everything recorded across Inland Croatia, in one list.

Gornji Grad

The medieval hill town, with a cannon fired at noon every day since 1877 and a tiled church roof carrying the arms of Croatia, Dalmatia and Slavonia.

Zagreb · Quarter

The Upper Lakes

Twelve terraced lakes joined by travertine barriers that are still growing about a centimetre a year. The boardwalks have no railings, which is part of it.

Plitvice Lakes · Waterfall

Veliki Slap

78 m, the tallest in Croatia, at the foot of the lower canyon. Heaviest after the snowmelt and reduced to a thread by late August.

Plitvice Lakes · Waterfall
