Inland Croatia, Croatia

Plitvice Lakes

Sixteen terraced lakes falling into each other over travertine barriers that are still growing.

♣ Wild nature▲ Mountains

Why visit

  • Boardwalks directly over the water — swimming is banned and enforced
  • The upper lakes, which most day visitors miss
  • First entry of the day, in autumn, with the mist on the water

Travel tips

Practical things about Croatia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Euro and Schengen since 2023 — the old kuna is gone and the Slovenian border is open.
  • Water shoes are genuinely useful; most beaches are pebble or rock.
  • Dubrovnik's old town has cruise-ship peaks; check the arrivals calendar and go on a light day.
