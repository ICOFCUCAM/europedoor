The Upper Lakes

Twelve terraced lakes joined by travertine barriers that are still growing about a centimetre a year. The boardwalks have no railings, which is part of it.

Waterfall · 3–4 hours

Veliki Slap

78 m, the tallest in Croatia, at the foot of the lower canyon. Heaviest after the snowmelt and reduced to a thread by late August.

Waterfall · 1 hour

Things to do

Plitvice at opening

Timed entry tickets sell out; the 7 am slot gives you an hour of empty boardwalk before the coaches from the coast arrive.

Walk or hike · moderate

Travel tips

Practical things about Croatia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Euro and Schengen since 2023 — the old kuna is gone and the Slovenian border is open.
  • Water shoes are genuinely useful; most beaches are pebble or rock.
  • Dubrovnik's old town has cruise-ship peaks; check the arrivals calendar and go on a light day.
