The portrait

Why Croatia belongs in your Europe.

Croatia is shaped like a pair of tongs around Bosnia, with a long limestone coast and a flat continental interior that visitors skip. The Adriatic islands — 1,244 of them, 48 inhabited — are the draw, along with two Roman-founded cities that never stopped being used. It joined Schengen and the euro in 2023, which changed the practicalities considerably.

Where Croatia is worth going

7 destinations across 3 travel regions.

Getting around

Jadrolinija ferries and catamarans link the islands and run reduced schedules out of season. Buses beat trains everywhere. The coastal road is beautiful and slow; the inland motorway is fast.

When to come

June and September are the best months: warm sea, open ferries, tolerable Dubrovnik. August is the Adriatic at capacity. Plitvice is at its best in October colour or under May snowmelt. Winter closes most island infrastructure.

Worth knowing

  • Euro and Schengen since 2023 — the old kuna is gone and the Slovenian border is open.
  • Water shoes are genuinely useful; most beaches are pebble or rock.
  • Dubrovnik’s old town has cruise-ship peaks; check the arrivals calendar and go on a light day.

At the table

  • Peka: meat or octopus baked under a bell with embers on top, ordered hours ahead
  • Istrian truffles and olive oil, both world class and cheaper here
  • Pošip and plavac mali — white and red from the Dalmatian islands
