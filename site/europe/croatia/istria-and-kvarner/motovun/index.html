Istria & Kvarner, Croatia

Motovun

A walled hill town over a truffle forest, with a walkable rampart and a view of the whole Mirna valley.

● Food◑ Wine & drink⚑ History & ruins

Why visit

  • Truffle hunting in the Motovun forest with dogs, October and November
  • The Parenzana, a disused railway turned cycle path
  • Istrian malvazija from cellars in the villages below
