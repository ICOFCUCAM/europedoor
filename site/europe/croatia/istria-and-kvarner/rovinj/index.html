Istria & Kvarner, Croatia

Rovinj

A Venetian fishing town on a former island, with a campanile copied from San Marco and houses straight into the sea.

≈ Coast & beaches● Food⌂ Architecture

Why visit

  • The old town's stepped lanes, all of them ending at water
  • Golden Cape forest park, cycled or walked
  • Istrian tables — truffle, prosciutto, malvazija — at half Italian prices
