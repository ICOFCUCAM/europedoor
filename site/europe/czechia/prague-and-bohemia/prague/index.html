Prague & Bohemia, Czechia

Prague

Romanesque, gothic, baroque and cubist stacked on top of each other, with a castle above and a bridge in between.

⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins♫ Music & nightlife● Food▦ Big cities

Why visit

  • Charles Bridge at six in the morning — genuinely worth the alarm
  • The castle complex and St Vitus's stained glass by Mucha
  • Vyšehrad, the other castle, with almost nobody on it
  • A pub where they draw a mlíko, a glass of pure foam, and mean it
