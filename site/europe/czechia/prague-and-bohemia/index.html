Places to see

Everything recorded across Prague & Bohemia, in one list.

Charles Bridge

Fourteenth-century, thirty baroque statues, and so crowded by ten in the morning that the only honest advice is to walk it at dawn or after midnight.

Prague · Bridge

Prague Castle

Not a castle so much as a walled hill town that has been the seat of Bohemian kings, Holy Roman emperors and Czech presidents in turn. St Vitus Cathedral is inside it.

Prague · Castle

Josefov, the Jewish Quarter

Six synagogues and a cemetery where twelve thousand headstones lean against each other because the ground was reused for three hundred years and there was nowhere else to bury.

Prague · Quarter

Vyšehrad and the river bend

The other castle rock, south along the Vltava, with a neo-gothic basilica and the national cemetery. Locals come here to avoid the crowds at the first one.

Prague · Ruin

Český Krumlov Castle

The second-largest castle complex in the country, wrapped in a river meander, with a baroque theatre that still has its original stage machinery and painted scenery.

Český Krumlov · Castle

The Cloak Bridge

A three-tiered covered viaduct joining the castle to its theatre across a ravine, built so the household could reach a performance without going outside.

Český Krumlov · Bridge
