Places to see

Český Krumlov Castle

The second-largest castle complex in the country, wrapped in a river meander, with a baroque theatre that still has its original stage machinery and painted scenery.

Castle · 2–3 hours

The Cloak Bridge

A three-tiered covered viaduct joining the castle to its theatre across a ravine, built so the household could reach a performance without going outside.

Travel tips

Practical things about Czechia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Beer is often cheaper than water in a pub and ordering is done by putting a mat on the table.
  • The koruna, not the euro; card acceptance is near-universal in cities.
  • Prague's centre has aggressive currency-exchange scams — use a bank or an ATM from a real bank.
