Prague & Bohemia, Czechia

Český Krumlov

A castle town in a tight loop of the Vltava, essentially unchanged since the 18th century.

⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins♣ Wild nature

Why visit

  • The baroque theatre, with original 1766 machinery still working
  • The bear moat, which has had bears since the 1700s
  • Canoeing the river loop around the town
