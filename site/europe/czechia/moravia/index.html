Places to see

Everything recorded across Moravia, in one list.

Villa Tugendhat

Mies van der Rohe, 1930, and the building where modernism stopped being a manifesto and became a house somebody lived in. Entry is by timed tour and books out weeks ahead.

Brno · Monument

Špilberk Castle

A hilltop fortress that spent the nineteenth century as the Habsburg empire's most feared prison, and now holds the city museum and the best view over the roofs.

Brno · Castle
