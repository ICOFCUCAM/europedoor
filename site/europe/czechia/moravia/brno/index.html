Czechia's second city: student, modernist and self-deprecating, with an ossuary of 50,000 under the square.

Places to see

Villa Tugendhat

Mies van der Rohe, 1930, and the building where modernism stopped being a manifesto and became a house somebody lived in. Entry is by timed tour and books out weeks ahead.

Monument · 1–2 hours

Špilberk Castle

A hilltop fortress that spent the nineteenth century as the Habsburg empire's most feared prison, and now holds the city museum and the best view over the roofs.

Travel tips

Practical things about Czechia that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Beer is often cheaper than water in a pub and ordering is done by putting a mat on the table.
  • The koruna, not the euro; card acceptance is near-universal in cities.
  • Prague's centre has aggressive currency-exchange scams — use a bank or an ATM from a real bank.
