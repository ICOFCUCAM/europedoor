Masovia & the Centre, Poland

Warsaw

Eighty-five per cent destroyed in 1944 and rebuilt from Canaletto's paintings, with a Stalinist tower and glass towers around it.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture♫ Music & nightlife● Food▦ Big cities

Why visit

  • POLIN, the museum of a thousand years of Polish Jews
  • The Warsaw Rising Museum, loud and unsparing
  • The reconstructed Old Town, itself a UNESCO site for how it was rebuilt
  • Chopin concerts in Łazienki park on summer Sundays
