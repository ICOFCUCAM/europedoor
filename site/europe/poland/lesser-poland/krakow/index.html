Why visit

  • The Rynek and the hourly trumpet call that stops mid-note
  • Wawel castle and cathedral, where Polish kings are buried
  • Kazimierz: synagogues, bars and Schindler's factory museum
  • Wieliczka's salt mine, carved into chapels 135 metres down

Travel tips

Practical things about Poland that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Auschwitz-Birkenau is a memorial and a cemetery; visits are free, timed, and behaviour there matters.
  • Milk bars (bar mleczny) are subsidised canteens serving a full meal for a few euros.
  • The złoty, not the euro, though cards work nearly everywhere.
