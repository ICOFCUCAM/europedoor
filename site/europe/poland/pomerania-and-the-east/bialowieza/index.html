Pomerania & the East, Poland

Białowieża

The last primeval lowland forest in Europe, with wild bison in it, on the Belarusian border.

National park · 1–2 nights

❦ Wildlife watching♣ Wild nature⚑ History & ruins

Why go

  1. 01The strict reserve, entered only with a licensed guide
  2. 02European bison — some 600 of them, wild
  3. 03Trees that have never been felled, some 400 years old

Travel tips

Practical things about Poland that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Auschwitz-Birkenau is a memorial and a cemetery; visits are free, timed, and behaviour there matters.
  • Milk bars (bar mleczny) are subsidised canteens serving a full meal for a few euros.
  • The złoty, not the euro, though cards work nearly everywhere.
