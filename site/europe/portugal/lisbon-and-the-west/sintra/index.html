Travel tips

Practical things about Portugal that are not obvious from outside it.

  • The couvert — bread, olives, cheese put on your table — is charged unless you send it back.
  • Fado has two schools, Lisbon's and Coimbra's, and neither is background music.
  • Atlantic beaches have real currents; the flag system is enforced and worth obeying.
