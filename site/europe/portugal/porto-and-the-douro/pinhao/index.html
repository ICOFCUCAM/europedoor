Porto & the Douro, Portugal

Pinhão & the Douro valley

Terraced schist vineyards falling into a dammed river, worked by hand because machines cannot stand up on them.

◑ Wine & drink♣ Wild nature═ Slow travel by rail● Food

Why visit

  • The Douro railway along the water from Peso da Régua
  • Quintas that take guests and let you pick during harvest
  • Foot-treading in granite lagares, still done for the top wines
