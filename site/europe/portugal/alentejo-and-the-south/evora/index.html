The Alentejo & the South, Portugal

Évora

A walled town with a Roman temple in the middle, a chapel of bones, and megaliths in the fields around it.

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places◑ Wine & drink● Food

Why visit

  • The Temple of Diana, standing since the 1st century
  • The Capela dos Ossos and its unambiguous doorway inscription
  • Almendres cromlech, older than Stonehenge, and usually empty
