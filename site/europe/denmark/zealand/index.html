Places to see

Everything recorded across Zealand &amp; Copenhagen, in one list.

Tivoli Gardens

Opened 1843 and the second-oldest amusement park still running. Walt Disney visited twice and took notes; the wooden roller coaster still has a brakeman riding in it.

Copenhagen · Park

Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek

A brewer's collection: Mediterranean antiquity downstairs, French impressionism upstairs, and a winter garden with a palm house in the middle of both.

Copenhagen · Museum

Rosenborg Castle

A Renaissance summer house in the middle of town, holding the crown jewels in a basement that feels far too casual for what is in it.

Copenhagen · Castle

Viking Ship Museum

Five ships deliberately sunk in 1070 to block the fjord, raised in 1962 and reassembled from fragments. Reconstructions are sailed from the harbour outside.

Roskilde · Museum

Roskilde Cathedral

The burial place of forty Danish kings and queens across nine hundred years, so the building is also a walk through changing ideas about how to be remembered.

Roskilde · Church
