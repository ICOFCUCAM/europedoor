Zealand & Copenhagen, Denmark

Roskilde

Viking ships raised from the fjord, thirty-nine Danish monarchs in one cathedral, and a festival in a field.

⚑ History & ruins✝ Sacred places♫ Music & nightlife

Why visit

  • Five 11th-century ships, scuttled to block the fjord and dug out in 1962
  • The cathedral: Denmark's royal burial church since 1413
  • Reconstructed longships you can row on the fjord
