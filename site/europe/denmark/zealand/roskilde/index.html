Viking Ship Museum

Five ships deliberately sunk in 1070 to block the fjord, raised in 1962 and reassembled from fragments. Reconstructions are sailed from the harbour outside.

Museum · 2–3 hours

Roskilde Cathedral

The burial place of forty Danish kings and queens across nine hundred years, so the building is also a walk through changing ideas about how to be remembered.

Church · 1–2 hours

Things to do

Row a reconstructed longship

Built with period tools from the excavated hulls; you supply the arms and learn quickly why Vikings were fit.

On the water · moderate

Travel tips

Practical things about Denmark that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Cash is nearly extinct; MobilePay and cards run the country.
  • Cycle lanes have their own rules and Danes enforce them by glare — signal, and keep right.
  • Almost everything closes on Sundays outside the big cities.
