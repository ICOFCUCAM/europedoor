Places to see

3 recorded so far. We hold what each one is and how long to give it, and deliberately not its opening hours or price.

Tivoli Gardens

Opened 1843 and the second-oldest amusement park still running. Walt Disney visited twice and took notes; the wooden roller coaster still has a brakeman riding in it.

Park · half a day

Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek

A brewer's collection: Mediterranean antiquity downstairs, French impressionism upstairs, and a winter garden with a palm house in the middle of both.

Museum · 2–3 hours

Rosenborg Castle

A Renaissance summer house in the middle of town, holding the crown jewels in a basement that feels far too casual for what is in it.

Castle · 2 hours

Travel tips

Practical things about Denmark that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Cash is nearly extinct; MobilePay and cards run the country.
  • Cycle lanes have their own rules and Danes enforce them by glare — signal, and keep right.
  • Almost everything closes on Sundays outside the big cities.
