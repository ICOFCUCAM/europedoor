Kyiv & the Centre, Ukraine

Kyiv

A city founded in the 5th century on hills above a wide river, with a cave monastery from 1051 beneath it.

✝ Sacred places⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture♫ Music & nightlife

Why visit

  • Saint Sophia's cathedral, with 11th-century mosaics
  • The Lavra caves, and the monks buried in them
  • Andriivskyi descent, the old artists' street
  • The deepest metro station in the world at Arsenalna, 105 metres down
