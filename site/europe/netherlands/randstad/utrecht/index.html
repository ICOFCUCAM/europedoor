Travel tips

Practical things about Netherlands that are not obvious from outside it.

  • Do not walk in the bicycle lane. This is the single most reliable way to annoy a Dutch person.
  • Amsterdam's centre is heavily regulated now — tours, rentals and behaviour all more restricted than the reputation suggests.
  • Almost everyone speaks excellent English and will switch before you finish your first Dutch sentence.
