The Randstad, Netherlands

Rotterdam

Flattened in 1940 and rebuilt as an argument for modern architecture, on Europe's largest port.

⌂ Architecture◧ Design & making▦ Big cities● Food

Why visit

  • The Markthal, a food market inside a housing block
  • Depot Boijmans, the first publicly accessible art storage building anywhere
  • Harbour tours through a port that runs for forty kilometres
