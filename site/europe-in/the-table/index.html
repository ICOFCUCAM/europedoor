Themes that run through it

Theme

Wine Europe

The interesting wine map of Europe is not the famous one. It includes clay vessels buried in Georgian courtyards, volcanic vines woven into baskets against Aegean wind, and slate slopes on the Rhine at sixty degrees. Every one of these places will let you taste in the cellar where it was made.

Theme

Island Europe

An island changes the pace before you arrive, because you have to wait for a boat. These eight are chosen for being genuinely themselves rather than resort annexes — a Baltic sand spit, a Hebridean distillery island, an Adriatic island closed to outsiders until 1989, and a Cycladic island that farms.

Theme

The Grand Tour

For two hundred years a wealthy young northern European was sent south to learn taste, and the itinerary barely changed: Paris, the Alps, the Italian cities, ideally Greece. The route survives, the coaching inns are hotels, and the trains do in hours what took them weeks.
