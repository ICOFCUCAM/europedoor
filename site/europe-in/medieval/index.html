Themes that run through it

Theme

Medieval Europe

A medieval town survives for one of two reasons: it stayed poor, or it stayed useful. These did one or the other. Look for the shape rather than the set pieces — a market square where the roads meet, a wall that a rich century paid for, and a church larger than the population ever justified.

Theme

Sacred Europe

Pilgrimage built more of Europe than any king did: the roads, the bridges, the hostels, the towns that grew where walkers stopped. These places span four Christian traditions and are all still in use — which means visiting them means visiting somebody's church rather than a monument.

Theme

Viking Europe

The Norse were traders and settlers more often than raiders, and the archaeology says so: ships built for cargo, parliaments held in the open air, and towns founded from Dublin to Kyiv. These are the places where you can see the evidence rather than the reputation.
