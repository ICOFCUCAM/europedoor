Europe's mountains

Europe has more mountain range than its reputation suggests, and most of it is not the Alps. These are the places where the mountain is the reason to go.

Themes that run through it

Theme

Mountain Europe

Europe's mountains are not one thing. The Alps are high and serviced; the Caucasus is higher and barely; the Tatras are compressed; the Picos end in the sea; the Accursed Mountains have a blood-feud memory and a trail through three countries. This is the case for treating them as different places rather than one activity.

Theme

Rail Europe

Some lines were built to move slate, ore or salt and are now worth the fare on their own. Others were engineering arguments. All of these are scheduled public services rather than tourist excursions, which means you can use them as transport and get the spectacle for the price of a ticket.
