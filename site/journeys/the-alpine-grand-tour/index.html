The Alpine Grand Tour

Six valleys, four countries, one mountain range

17 days · 6 stops · 4 countries · 509 km in a straight line

▲ Mountains═ Slow travel by rail♣ Wild nature❄ Snow & winter● Food

The Alps are a single range and five nationalities, and the way to feel that is to cross them slowly by train and cable car rather than fly over. This route runs west to east from Mont Blanc to the Dolomites, staying high, and every leg is on a scheduled public service. Book the mountain huts before you book the hotels.

The route, in order

Every hop is a straight-line distance between two coordinates. This atlas holds no road and no rail geometry, so that number is a floor on the leg and never the leg — Chamonix to Zermatt is 69 km here and about 170 on the ground, round a mountain range. It is here for the scale of the thing, not for planning a day.

Experiences along the way

Down to the Mer de Glace

The steps to the ice are signed with the year the glacier reached each level; the descent is a chronology as much as a walk.

Chamonix · Walk or hike

The North Face trail from Mürren

Two hours on a balcony path with the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau directly across the valley the whole way.

Lauterbrunnen · Walk or hike

The Bernina line to Tirano

From glacier to palm trees in four hours, over the Landwasser viaduct and around the Brusio spiral — booked as a normal train, not a tour.

St. Moritz & the Engadin · Rail, road or ride

City to summit in twenty minutes

Tram, funicular and two cable cars take you from a café table to 2,256 metres of exposed ridge; take a jacket you would not think you needed.

Innsbruck · Rail, road or ride

A night in a rifugio

Dinner at seven, lights out at ten, and the Enrosadira — the pink glow on the rock at dawn — from the door.

Ortisei & the Dolomites · Walk or hike

What you will be eating

  • France — Bread bought twice a day, because it is worth nothing by evening
  • Switzerland — Cheese in its own right — Gruyère, Appenzeller, raclette scraped off the wheel
  • Austria — Wiener Schnitzel, veal and beaten thin, with potato salad
  • Italy — Pasta by region — trofie in Liguria, orecchiette in Puglia, carbonara only in Rome

What to pack

  • Boots with ankle support and a broken-in sole
  • Layers, and a hat, even in August
  • Cash for mountain huts, which often take nothing else
  • Sun cream — snow at altitude burns

What this estimate covers

About €4,510 per person: 16 nights at the high daily band for each country on the route, plus a distance-based transport figure between stops, plus 12%.
