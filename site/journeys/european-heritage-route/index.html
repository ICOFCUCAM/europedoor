London to Budapest by rail

The European Heritage Route

The specification's own suggestion, and a good one: the cathedral-and-capital spine of western and central Europe, taken entirely by train. It crosses seven countries and four architectural centuries without ever needing a flight, and every stop on it is a city that once thought of itself as the centre of something.

The route

  • Days 1–3

    London · United Kingdom

    Begin with the free national collections and a play for the price of a cinema ticket.

  • Day 4

    Canterbury · United Kingdom

    An hour out, and the mother church of a global communion.

    ↳ 88 km — a local train or a short drive from London

  • Days 5–6

    Bruges · Belgium

    Under the Channel and into Flanders: a city preserved by going broke in 1500.

    ↳ 149 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Canterbury

  • Days 7–8

    Amsterdam · Netherlands

    The canal ring, the Rijksmuseum, and a bicycle rather than a tram.

    ↳ 173 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Bruges

  • Days 9–10

    Cologne · Germany

    Up the Rhine: a cathedral that took 632 years and a Roman city under the streets.

    ↳ 213 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Amsterdam

  • Days 11–13

    Prague · Czechia

    East into Bohemia, and the largest intact gothic and baroque centre north of the Alps.

    ↳ 536 km — a long rail day, or a short flight if the days are tight from Cologne

  • Days 14–16

    Vienna · Austria

    South to the imperial capital: standing tickets at the opera, coffee houses in the afternoon.

    ↳ 253 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Prague

  • Days 17–19

    Budapest · Hungary

    Down the Danube to end in a thermal bath, ideally in the snow.

    ↳ 214 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Vienna

Experiences along the way

The Hampstead bathing ponds

Three ponds, open every day of the year, including the January mornings when the ice is broken by hand.

London · On the water

One free national collection a day

Britain's museums charge nothing for the permanent collections; treat one as an hour's visit rather than a day's endurance.

London · Museum or collection

The last day of the Pilgrims' Way

Twenty miles from Chilham along the North Downs, arriving on foot the way it was meant to be done.

Canterbury · Walk or hike

Bruges before the coaches

Day-trippers arrive from ten. Between six and nine the canals belong to you and the swans.

Bruges · Walk or hike

The city the way it is used

Rent for a day, ride out to Amsterdam-Noord on the free ferry, and stop thinking of the bicycle as an activity.

Amsterdam · Rail, road or ride

A pub with a tapster, not a menu

The good places pour continuously and mark your tab in pencil; you say when to stop, and the pour type is a real choice.

Prague · Food & table

Vyšehrad and the river bend

The city's first castle, a cemetery of composers, and the whole of Prague from the ramparts without a crowd.

Prague · Walk or hike

Standing room at the Staatsoper

A few euros, queued for on the night, in one of the great opera houses. Bring a scarf to tie to the rail and hold your place.

Vienna · Music & performance

A heuriger on the city edge

Wine taverns in Vienna's own vineyards, open only when the grower has wine to sell — a pine branch over the door means open.

Vienna · Cellar & vineyard

An outdoor thermal bath in winter

38°C water, snow on the stone, and old men playing chess on floating boards. Go early; go on a weekday.

Budapest · On the water

What you will be eating

  • United Kingdom — A Sunday roast, which is a meal and an institution
  • Belgium — Chips, twice fried, from a frituur, with a sauce you choose badly the first time
  • Netherlands — Raw herring, held by the tail, with onion — in season from June
  • Germany — Bread: 3,000 registered varieties, and the bakery is the first thing to find
  • Czechia — Roast pork, dumplings and cabbage — vepřo knedlo zelo, the national plate
  • Austria — Wiener Schnitzel, veal and beaten thin, with potato salad
  • Hungary — Paprika, in everything, and not interchangeable with chilli

What to pack

  • A rail pass, or advance singles
  • One smart layer for a concert
  • An umbrella
  • Shoulders covered for churches

What this estimate covers

About €2,710 per person: 19 nights at the moderate daily band for each country on the route, plus a distance-based transport figure between stops, plus 12%. It excludes getting to the start and home from the end, and it is planning arithmetic from published bands rather than a quote. How these numbers are made →
