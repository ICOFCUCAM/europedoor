Winter, treated as the reason rather than the problem

The Cold North

Most of Europe's north is sold in summer and is more itself in February. This is a winter route: aurora, snow, saunas, ice and four hours of daylight that are better than most places get in twelve. It needs proper clothing and repays it.

The route

  • Days 1–2

    Helsinki · Finland

    Start with a sauna on the frozen waterfront.

  • Days 3–5

    Rovaniemi · Finland

    North by night train, into Lapland proper.

    ↳ 705 km — a long rail day, or a short flight if the days are tight from Helsinki

  • Days 6–8

    Inari · Finland

    Sámi Finland, a lake with three thousand islands, and no light pollution in any direction.

    ↳ 274 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Rovaniemi

  • Days 9–12

    Tromsø · Norway

    Across into Norway for whales in the dark and the aurora directly overhead.

    ↳ 328 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Inari

  • Days 13–15

    Abisko · Sweden

    South-east into the driest winter sky in Lapland, and the chairlift into it.

    ↳ 145 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Tromsø

  • Days 16–17

    Kiruna · Sweden

    End in the iron town that is being physically moved off the hole it dug.

    ↳ 80 km — a local train or a short drive from Abisko

Experiences along the way

A public sauna on the sea

Hot, then the Baltic, then hot again. The rule is that nobody performs and nobody talks much.

Helsinki · On the water

The Design District on foot

Twenty-five streets, two hundred shops and studios, and the through-line from Aalto to whoever graduated last year.

Helsinki · Walk or hike

Siida, on Sámi terms

A national minority telling its own story, including the parts about the Finnish state — worth an afternoon rather than an hour.

Inari · Museum or collection

An aurora night with a forecast, not a promise

A guide who drives to clear sky — sometimes into Finland — is worth more than one who stays put; nobody can guarantee the lights, and anyone who does is selling something.

Tromsø · Wildlife & nature

A Sámi reindeer herding afternoon

Book with a Sámi-owned operator; the difference between a working herd and a photo backdrop is obvious within ten minutes.

Tromsø · Wildlife & nature

The first hut on the Kungsleden

One day out and one back gets you the landscape without the fortnight; the huts sell chocolate and take card.

Abisko · Walk or hike

What you will be eating

  • Finland — Rye bread, dense and sour, eaten with everything
  • Norway — Brown cheese (brunost) — caramelised whey, sliced thin, an acquired and worthwhile taste
  • Sweden — Fika: coffee and a cardamom bun, treated as an appointment rather than a snack

What to pack

  • Proper insulated boots, not fashion boots
  • Mittens, not gloves
  • A merino base layer
  • Something to cover your face at −25°C
  • Patience: the aurora is a forecast, not a booking
