Four countries down one river

The Danube Line

The Danube passes through more capitals than any river in the world, and this follows the interesting half of it. Cycle path, train and river boat all run alongside each other for most of the way, so the transport is a choice rather than a constraint.

The route

  • Days 1–2

    Dürnstein & the Wachau · Austria

    Start in the Wachau: terraced vineyards and a blue baroque tower over the water.

  • Days 3–5

    Vienna · Austria

    Downstream to the imperial capital.

    ↳ 66 km — a local train or a short drive from Dürnstein & the Wachau

  • Days 6–7

    Bratislava · Slovakia

    An hour further, and a different country with a castle on the bluff.

    ↳ 55 km — a local train or a short drive from Vienna

  • Days 8–10

    Budapest · Hungary

    The river's best city, and its widest.

    ↳ 161 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Bratislava

  • Days 11–12

    Novi Sad · Serbia

    Into Serbia: a Habsburg river town under a fortress with sixteen kilometres of tunnels.

    ↳ 258 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Budapest

  • Days 13–15

    Belgrade · Serbia

    End where the Sava joins it, on a fortress above both.

    ↳ 70 km — a local train or a short drive from Novi Sad

Experiences along the way

Standing room at the Staatsoper

A few euros, queued for on the night, in one of the great opera houses. Bring a scarf to tie to the rail and hold your place.

Vienna · Music & performance

A heuriger on the city edge

Wine taverns in Vienna's own vineyards, open only when the grower has wine to sell — a pine branch over the door means open.

Vienna · Cellar & vineyard

The Small Carpathian wine road

Twenty minutes from the capital: family cellars in Pezinok and Modra that sell almost nothing beyond the region.

Bratislava · Cellar & vineyard

An outdoor thermal bath in winter

38°C water, snow on the stone, and old men playing chess on floating boards. Go early; go on a weekday.

Budapest · On the water

A ruin bar, before it fills

Derelict courtyard buildings filled with salvaged furniture; Szimpla was the first and is still worth an early evening.

Budapest · Music & performance

A kafana with live music

Long tables, a small band moving between them, rakija, and no fixed end time.

Belgrade · Music & performance

What you will be eating

  • Austria — Wiener Schnitzel, veal and beaten thin, with potato salad
  • Slovakia — Bryndzové halušky: potato dumplings with sheep cheese and bacon
  • Hungary — Paprika, in everything, and not interchangeable with chilli
  • Serbia — Ćevapi and pljeskavica, grilled and enormous

What to pack

  • A bicycle, if you want one — the path is flat the whole way
  • Sun protection on the water
  • One smart layer for Vienna
