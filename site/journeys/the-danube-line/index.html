Four countries down one river

The Danube Line

The Danube passes through more capitals than any river in the world, and this follows the interesting half of it. Cycle path, train and river boat all run alongside each other for most of the way, so the transport is a choice rather than a constraint.

The route

  • Days 1–2

    Dürnstein & the Wachau · Austria

    Start in the Wachau: terraced vineyards and a blue baroque tower over the water.

  • Days 3–5

    Vienna · Austria

    Downstream to the imperial capital.

    ↳ 66 km — a local train or a short drive from Dürnstein & the Wachau

  • Days 6–7

    Bratislava · Slovakia

    An hour further, and a different country with a castle on the bluff.

    ↳ 55 km — a local train or a short drive from Vienna

  • Days 8–10

    Budapest · Hungary

    The river's best city, and its widest.

    ↳ 161 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Bratislava

  • Days 11–12

    Novi Sad · Serbia

    Into Serbia: a Habsburg river town under a fortress with sixteen kilometres of tunnels.

    ↳ 258 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Budapest

  • Days 13–15

    Belgrade · Serbia

    End where the Sava joins it, on a fortress above both.

    ↳ 70 km — a local train or a short drive from Novi Sad

What to pack

  • A bicycle, if you want one — the path is flat the whole way
  • Sun protection on the water
  • One smart layer for Vienna

What this estimate covers
