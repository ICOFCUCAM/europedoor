Atlantic to the Mediterranean

The classic west-to-east traverse, taken slowly enough to notice the change: Atlantic light and salt cod in the northwest, the Moorish south in the middle, then the Mediterranean proper from Barcelona onward. Every leg on this route can be done by train, which is unusual for a journey of this length and is half the pleasure of it.

The route

  • Days 1–3

    Porto · Portugal

    Granite, port lodges and the Douro. Begin on the Atlantic, facing the wrong way on purpose.

  • Days 4–6

    Lisbon · Portugal

    Down the coast to tile, fado and the Gulbenkian. Three days is the minimum here.

    ↳ 274 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Porto

  • Days 7–8

    Évora · Portugal

    Inland into the Alentejo: a Roman temple, a chapel of bones, and cork oaks in every direction.

    ↳ 108 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Lisbon

  • Days 9–11

    Seville · Spain

    Across the border into Andalusia. Orange trees, the Alcázar, and flamenco in a room of thirty.

    ↳ 213 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Évora

  • Days 12–14

    Granada · Spain

    East to the Alhambra, with the Sierra Nevada behind it and free tapas beneath it.

    ↳ 213 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Seville

  • Days 15–17

    Barcelona · Spain

    The long leg north by high-speed rail, into Gaudí's grid and the Mediterranean.

    ↳ 682 km — a long rail day, or a short flight if the days are tight from Granada

  • Days 18–19

    Nice · France

    Over the border along the coast, for the light the painters came for.

    ↳ 490 km — a long rail day, or a short flight if the days are tight from Barcelona

  • Days 20–22

    Rome · Italy

    The last hop east. Three thousand years, stacked, and the best street food of the trip.

    ↳ 471 km — a long rail day, or a short flight if the days are tight from Nice

What to pack

  • Comfortable shoes for cobbles and hills
  • Sun protection from April onwards
  • Something with sleeves for churches
  • A rail pass, or advance singles booked the day they open

What this estimate covers

About €2,860 per person: 22 nights at the moderate daily band for each country on the route, plus a distance-based transport figure between stops, plus 12%.
