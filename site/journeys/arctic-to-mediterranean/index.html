The full length of Europe, 69°N to 38°N

Arctic to Mediterranean

The specification's flagship, and the longest thing on this site: the whole north-to-south axis of Europe, from the midnight sun to the Aegean. Thirteen stops, eight countries, and roughly 5,000 kilometres of ground. Almost all of it can be done by train, which is the argument for taking it slowly rather than flying the middle out. It is a month, and it is honestly a month — anyone selling you this in a fortnight is selling you airports.

The route

  • Days 1–3

    Tromsø · Norway

    Begin at 69°N, where the sun does not set in June and does not rise in December.

  • Days 4–6

    Lofoten (Svolvær) · Norway

    South-west into the islands: granite straight out of the sea, cod on racks.

    ↳ 236 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Tromsø

  • Days 7–8

    Abisko · Sweden

    Over the border into Sweden by the Iron Ore Line, through Lapland.

    ↳ 176 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Lofoten (Svolvær)

  • Days 9–10

    Stockholm · Sweden

    The night train south, to a capital on fourteen islands.

    ↳ 1004 km — fly, or give the overland crossing a day of its own from Abisko

  • Days 11–12

    Copenhagen · Denmark

    Across the Öresund bridge into Denmark.

    ↳ 522 km — a long rail day, or a short flight if the days are tight from Stockholm

  • Days 13–14

    Hamburg · Germany

    Into Germany, and Europe's third port.

    ↳ 289 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Copenhagen

  • Days 15–17

    Berlin · Germany

    East to the city that carried most of the twentieth century.

    ↳ 255 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Hamburg

  • Days 18–20

    Vienna · Austria

    South to the imperial capital, and the first hint of the south.

    ↳ 523 km — a long rail day, or a short flight if the days are tight from Berlin

  • Days 21–23

    Ortisei & the Dolomites · Italy

    Over the Alps into the Dolomites, and Ladin-speaking valleys.

    ↳ 398 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Vienna

  • Days 24–25

    Venice · Italy

    Down out of the mountains to the lagoon.

    ↳ 136 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Ortisei & the Dolomites

  • Days 26–28

    Rome · Italy

    South through Italy to the city everything else was measured against.

    ↳ 394 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Venice

  • Days 29–30

    Naples · Italy

    Further south, for the archaeological museum and the pizza.

    ↳ 188 km — a comfortable train leg, half a day at most from Rome

  • Days 31–33

    Athens · Greece

    Across the sea to the end of the line.

    ↳ 872 km — a long rail day, or a short flight if the days are tight from Naples

Experiences along the way

An aurora night with a forecast, not a promise

A guide who drives to clear sky — sometimes into Finland — is worth more than one who stays put; nobody can guarantee the lights, and anyone who does is selling something.

Tromsø · Wildlife & nature

A Sámi reindeer herding afternoon

Book with a Sámi-owned operator; the difference between a working herd and a photo backdrop is obvious within ten minutes.

Tromsø · Wildlife & nature

A night in a working rorbu

The old cod-fishing cabins on stilts; the good ones still smell faintly of tar and are still owned by fishing families.

Lofoten (Svolvær) · Walk or hike

Cold-water surf at Unstad

Beach break inside the Arctic Circle, 6mm of neoprene, and a sauna afterwards that you will remember longer than the waves.

Lofoten (Svolvær) · On the water

The first hut on the Kungsleden

One day out and one back gets you the landscape without the fortnight; the huts sell chocolate and take card.

Abisko · Walk or hike

Archipelago on a commuter boat

The Waxholmsbolaget boats are public transport that happen to cross one of the world's great island landscapes.

Stockholm · On the water

A proper fika circuit

Four bakeries, one afternoon, and the argument about whether the cardamom bun beats the cinnamon one.

Stockholm · Food & table

Swim in the harbour

Free, supervised in summer, and the result of thirty years of cleaning up a working port.

Copenhagen · On the water

Smørrebrød in the correct order

Herring first, then fish, then meat, then cheese, with aquavit between — a lunch that takes three hours and is meant to.

Copenhagen · Food & table

The 62 ferry, not the tour boat

A public transport ferry down the Elbe past the container terminals; same view, a fraction of the price.

Hamburg · On the water

What you will be eating

  • Norway — Brown cheese (brunost) — caramelised whey, sliced thin, an acquired and worthwhile taste
  • Sweden — Fika: coffee and a cardamom bun, treated as an appointment rather than a snack
  • Denmark — Smørrebrød: open sandwiches taken seriously, with rules about order
  • Germany — Bread: 3,000 registered varieties, and the bakery is the first thing to find
  • Austria — Wiener Schnitzel, veal and beaten thin, with potato salad
  • Italy — Pasta by region — trofie in Liguria, orecchiette in Puglia, carbonara only in Rome
  • Greece — Whatever is in the pot that day — ask to see the kitchen, which is normal

What to pack

  • Layers for both ends: it is 8°C in Tromsø and 32°C in Athens on the same trip
  • A rail pass, and the patience to reserve the legs that need reserving
  • Boots that will do a fell in Norway and a marble street in Rome
  • An eye mask for the north and a hat for the south

What this estimate covers
