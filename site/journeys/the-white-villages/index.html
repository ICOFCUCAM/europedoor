Journey

The White South

Andalusia and the Maghreb's long shadow

15 days · 6 stops · 1 countries · 671 km

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture✝ Sacred places● Food≈ Coast & beaches

Eight centuries of Islamic rule left more than the Alhambra, and this route is the rest of it: the horseshoe arch in the village church, the irrigation channel still running, the word for the vegetable. Short legs, hot afternoons, and everything happening after six.

The route, in order

  6. Days 13–14 2 nights

    Málaga Spain

    Back east along the coast, to finish with Picasso and grilled sardines.

Experiences along the way

Flamenco in a peña

Members' clubs that admit visitors quietly; no castanets, no stage, and the singing is the part that matters.

Seville · Music & performance

The Nasrid palaces, on a timed ticket

Half-hour entry slots, sold out weeks ahead. Take the earliest of the day — the courtyards were designed for low light and few people.

Granada · Sacred site

Tapas, the Granada way

Every drink comes with a plate you did not order and cannot choose; four bars is a dinner.

Granada · Food & table

What you will be eating

  • Spain — Tapas, which is a way of eating rather than a dish — and free with a drink in Granada and León

What to pack

  • A hat
  • A siesta
  • Shoes for cobbled hills
  • Modest dress for the mosque-cathedral
