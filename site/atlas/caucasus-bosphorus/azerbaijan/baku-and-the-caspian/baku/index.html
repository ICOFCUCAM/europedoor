Baku & the Caspian, Azerbaijan

Baku

A UNESCO-listed walled city with a mysterious stone tower, ringed by oil-boom mansions and three flame-shaped skyscrapers.

⌂ Architecture⚑ History & ruins● Food▦ Big cities

What earns the time

  • Icherisheher, the walled old city, and the Maiden Tower nobody can fully explain
  • The Heydar Aliyev Centre, Zaha Hadid's curved white building
  • Ateshgah, the fire temple, and Yanar Dag, a hillside that has burned for centuries
  • Gobustan's 6,000 petroglyphs and the mud volcanoes beside them
