Tallinn & the North Coast, Estonia

Tallinn

A walled hanseatic town where the merchants' houses, the guild halls and the city wall all survived, and a startup district grew up beside them.

⚑ History & ruins⌂ Architecture▦ Big cities◧ Design & making● Food

What earns the time

  • The intact town wall and twenty of its towers
  • Telliskivi's converted rail yards, where the country's design lives now
  • Kadriorg palace and the KUMU art museum behind it
  • The Seaplane Harbour, a 1917 concrete hangar full of ships
