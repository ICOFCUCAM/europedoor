Tuscany & the Centre, Italy

Florence

A banking city that funded the Renaissance and kept nearly all of it inside a twenty-minute walk.

◈ Art & museums⌂ Architecture● Food◑ Wine & drink

What earns the time

  • Brunelleschi's dome, climbed at opening
  • The Uffizi, booked, and the Bargello, which is empty and holds the sculpture
  • San Miniato al Monte at vespers, with the monks singing
  • Bistecca alla fiorentina, which is a cut and a thickness, not a doneness
