The South & the Islands, Italy

Naples

Loud, dense, Greek in origin, with the best pizza and the best archaeological museum in the world.

● Food⚑ History & ruins◈ Art & museums≈ Coast & beaches

What earns the time

  • The Archaeological Museum, which holds everything good from Pompeii
  • Pizza in a room with a wood oven and four tables
  • Naples underground: Greek quarries, aqueducts and war shelters
  • Pompeii and Herculaneum, twenty minutes by local train
