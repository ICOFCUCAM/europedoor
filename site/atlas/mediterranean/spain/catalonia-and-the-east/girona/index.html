Catalonia & the East, Spain

Girona & the Empordà

A walled river city with the widest gothic nave in the world, forty minutes from Barcelona and a fraction of the crowd.

⚑ History & ruins● Food⌂ Architecture◑ Wine & drink

What earns the time

  • The Jewish quarter, one of Europe's best preserved
  • The city walls, walkable, with the Pyrenees on the horizon
  • Empordà wine and the coves of the northern Costa Brava
