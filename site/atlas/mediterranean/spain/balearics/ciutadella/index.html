The Balearic Islands, Spain

Ciutadella, Menorca

Menorca's old capital: a sandstone harbour town on an island that is a UNESCO biosphere reserve and mostly farmland.

○ Islands≈ Coast & beaches⚑ History & ruins● Food

What earns the time

  • Camí de Cavalls: 185 km of bridle path round the whole coast
  • Talayotic stone monuments, on the UNESCO list since 2023
  • Beaches on the north coast reached only on foot
