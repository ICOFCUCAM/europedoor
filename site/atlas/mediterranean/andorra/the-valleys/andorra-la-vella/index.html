The Valleys, Andorra

Andorra la Vella

Europe's highest capital at 1,023 metres, in a narrow valley, mostly shops with mountains directly behind them.

▲ Mountains❄ Snow & winter

What earns the time

  • Casa de la Vall, the 16th-century parliament house
  • Caldea thermal spa, fed by hot springs
  • Ski areas twenty minutes up the valley in either direction
