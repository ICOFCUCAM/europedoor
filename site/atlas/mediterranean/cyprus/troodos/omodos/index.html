The Troodos Mountains, Cyprus

Omodos & the wine villages

Cobbled mountain villages growing indigenous grapes nobody else has, around a 12th-century monastery.

◑ Wine & drink✝ Sacred places▲ Mountains● Food

What earns the time

  • Xynisteri and mavro, grape varieties grown almost nowhere else
  • The painted churches of the Troodos, ten of them UNESCO-listed
  • Commandaria, documented since the crusades

The barn-roofed painted churches

Small stone chapels under enormous pitched roofs, frescoed inside floor to ceiling; several need a key fetched from a village house.
