The Islands, Greece

Ikaria

A steep island where people routinely live past ninety, festivals start at midnight and shops open when they open.

○ Islands● Food♣ Wild nature♫ Music & nightlife

What earns the time

  • Panigiria: village festivals that run until dawn and are not for tourists
  • Radioactive hot springs at Therma, used since antiquity
  • Nas and the west coast, which is nearly roadless
