Østlandet & the East, Norway

Fredrikstad

Scandinavia's best-preserved fortress town, on an island in a river mouth, reached by a free ferry.

What earns the time

  • Gamlebyen: ramparts, moat and cobbles, still inhabited
  • The free city ferry, which is public transport and runs all day
  • Hvaler's skerries, an hour south by bus and boat

The free ferry, all day

Fredrikstad runs its river crossings free of charge as ordinary transport; ride them between the old town and the new for as long as you like.
