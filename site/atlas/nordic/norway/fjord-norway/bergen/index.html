Fjord Norway, Norway

Bergen

A hanseatic trading port wedged between seven mountains, wet 200 days a year and entirely unbothered about it.

≈ Coast & beaches⚑ History & ruins● Food♫ Music & nightlife═ Slow travel by rail

What earns the time

  • Bryggen's leaning wooden warehouses, and the fire history that explains their gaps
  • The Fløibanen funicular to a mountain you can walk down in an hour
  • The fish market — better one street back, where the locals buy
  • The Bergen Railway east: seven hours across a high plateau
