Nordkapp (Honningsvåg)

A cliff at 71°N sold as the top of Europe, on an island reached by tunnel, with a fishing town below it.

≈ Coast & beaches♣ Wild nature❄ Snow & winter

What earns the time

  • The plateau at midnight in June, with the sun still up
  • Gjesvær's bird cliffs: puffins, gannets and 100,000 pairs
  • Honningsvåg itself, which is a working port and better company than the cape
