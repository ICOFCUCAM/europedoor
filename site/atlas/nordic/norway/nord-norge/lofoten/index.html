Northern Norway, Norway

Lofoten (Svolvær)

Granite peaks that rise straight from the sea, red fishermen's cabins below them, and cod drying on racks every spring.

▲ Mountains≈ Coast & beaches○ Islands♣ Wild nature

What earns the time

  • Reine and Hamnøy, the two views that put Lofoten on every screen
  • Ryten and Reinebringen — short, steep, and worth the calves
  • Sleeping in a rorbu, the cabins built for seasonal cod crews
  • Unstad's cold-water surf, wetsuits mandatory

A night in a working rorbu

The old cod-fishing cabins on stilts; the good ones still smell faintly of tar and are still owned by fishing families.

Walk or hike · moderate

Cold-water surf at Unstad

Beach break inside the Arctic Circle, 6mm of neoprene, and a sauna afterwards that you will remember longer than the waves.
