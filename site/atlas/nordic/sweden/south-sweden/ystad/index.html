Skåne & the South, Sweden

Ystad

Half-timbered market town on a long empty beach, with a Bronze Age stone ship on the cliff above.

What earns the time

  • Ales stenar: 59 boulders in the shape of a ship, on a headland
  • Three hundred half-timbered houses, still lived in
  • The night watchman's horn, sounded every quarter hour since the 1600s
