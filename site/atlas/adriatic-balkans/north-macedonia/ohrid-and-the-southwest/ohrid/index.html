Ohrid & the Southwest, North Macedonia

Ohrid

A town of Byzantine churches on a cliff over one of the world's oldest lakes, with a Slavic literary tradition that began here.

✝ Sacred places♣ Wild nature⚑ History & ruins≈ Coast & beaches

What earns the time

  • Sveti Jovan Kaneo, on a cliff over the water
  • The lake itself: 3–5 million years old, 288 metres deep
  • Plaošnik, where Cyrillic literacy was taught in the 9th century
  • Swimming from the town beaches in July and August
