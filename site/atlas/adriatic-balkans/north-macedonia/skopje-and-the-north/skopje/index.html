Skopje

A capital with the largest Ottoman bazaar in the Balkans on one bank and a marble-clad neoclassical rebuild on the other.

What earns the time

  • The Stone Bridge, and the argument in stone on either side of it
  • Matka canyon, twenty minutes out, with caves and a kayak club
