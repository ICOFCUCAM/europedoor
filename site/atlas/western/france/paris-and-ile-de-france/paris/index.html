Paris & Île-de-France, France

Paris

Nineteenth-century town planning at monumental scale, with the world's densest concentration of art inside it.

What earns the time

  • The Musée d'Orsay in a railway station, better than the Louvre for a single visit
  • Père Lachaise, a walled city of the dead with its own map
  • The covered passages of the 2nd, from the 1820s
  • A market street — rue Mouffetard, rue des Martyrs — early on a Sunday

The covered passages
