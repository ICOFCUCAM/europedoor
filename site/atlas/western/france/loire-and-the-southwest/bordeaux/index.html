The Loire & the Southwest, France

Bordeaux

An 18th-century stone city on a river bend, cleaned in the 2000s, with the world's most famous vineyards at the gates.

What earns the time

  • The Place de la Bourse and its mirror of water
  • Saint-Émilion, a monolithic church cut into rock, forty minutes east
  • The Cité du Vin, which is better than it sounds
