The Loire & the Southwest, France

Sarlat & the Dordogne

A honey-coloured medieval town in a valley of castles, walnut groves and the oldest painted caves in Europe.

⚑ History & ruins● Food♣ Wild nature⌂ Architecture

What earns the time

  • Lascaux IV, an exact facsimile because the original is closed for its own survival
  • Castles facing each other across the river from the Hundred Years' War
  • Saturday market: walnuts, foie gras, and truffles in winter
