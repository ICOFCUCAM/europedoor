The North & the Wadden Islands, Netherlands

Texel

The largest Wadden island: dunes, sheep, seals and thirty kilometres of empty North Sea beach.

○ Islands♣ Wild nature≈ Coast & beaches❦ Wildlife watching

What earns the time

  • Seal colonies on the sandbanks, seen from a boat
  • Bird reserves on the migration route, best in spring
  • Local lamb and a brewery, on an island you can cycle around in a day

Experiences here

Wadlopen — walking the sea floor

Guided walks across the exposed seabed at low tide, thigh-deep in mud, timed against a returning sea. Never attempted alone.

Walk or hike · moderate
