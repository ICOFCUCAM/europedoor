Balaton & the Wine Country, Hungary

Tihany, Lake Balaton

A lavender peninsula with an abbey on a cliff, sticking into a shallow warm lake.

What earns the time

  • The abbey and its 1055 founding charter — the oldest written Hungarian
  • Lavender fields in late June
  • Water warm enough to stand in for hours, and rarely deeper than four metres
