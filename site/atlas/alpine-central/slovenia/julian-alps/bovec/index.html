The Julian Alps, Slovenia

Bovec & the Soča

An alpine valley on an unreally turquoise river, and the front line of the Isonzo battles above it.

▲ Mountains♣ Wild nature⚑ History & ruins❦ Wildlife watching

What earns the time

  • Rafting and kayaking on the Soča in early summer
  • The Walk of Peace: open-air museums along the 1915–17 front
  • The Vršič pass, 50 hairpins built by Russian prisoners of war
