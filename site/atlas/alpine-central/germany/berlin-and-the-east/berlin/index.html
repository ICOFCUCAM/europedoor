Berlin & the East, Germany

Berlin

Poor, sexy, enormous and unfinished — five museums on one island and a wall memorial that refuses to be comfortable.

⚑ History & ruins◈ Art & museums♫ Music & nightlife▦ Big cities⌂ Architecture

What earns the time

  • Museum Island, especially the Pergamon's scale and the Neues Museum's Nefertiti
  • The Berlin Wall Memorial at Bernauer Straße — the honest one, not Checkpoint Charlie
  • Tempelhof: an airport turned into the largest urban park in the world
  • The Stasi Museum, in the ministry's own offices
