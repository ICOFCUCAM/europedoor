You have not heard of most of Europe

130 places in the Atlas are somewhere almost nobody has told you to go. Europe's problem is not the number of visitors; it is that they arrive in the same eleven places in the same six weeks. There is a score for how far a place is from being the obvious choice, and it is published in full.

Abisko

Spain · Aragón & the Pyrenees

Albarracín

A pink town wrapped in a river bend and a wall that climbs the ridge above it, in one of Europe's emptiest provinces.

Norway · Northern Norway

Alta

Finnmark's largest town, with 6,000-year-old rock carvings and a cathedral shaped like the aurora.
